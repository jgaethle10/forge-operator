# EVERCRAFT | Mom test / commercial packaging card

**5-second headline:** "Your customer can't open the file? We'll make it deliverable and verify the copy."

**Customer-facing demonstration:** Show a non-sensitive oversized sample with (1) attachment rejection context, (2) authorized private-cloud share as the delivery route, (3) one ZIP package, (4) recipient rebuilding and seeing VERIFIED. Avoid technical jargon until the buyer asks.

**Offer candidate:** Delivery Rescue (fixed-scope concierge pilot, **proposed** $99, NOT a live payable SKU). Initial service, not SaaS, and not permission to use sensitive files. Define file count, sizes, channel, allowed data classification, sender/recipient identity verification, processing time, storage retention and refund terms in the accepted scope.

**Do not promise:** universal email size bypass, compression into a PNG, secure encrypted hosting, antivirus protection, IP rights, or a live customer recipient until proven.

**Mom test:** Without explaining it, show the title and a 20-second use-case clip/screen recording. Ask: "What is the problem? Who is it for? How does someone get the result? Would you trust it?" Log exact answers before changing copy.

**Next concrete engineering acceptance:** two independent devices / accounts, permitted Drive link or other approved transport, random-sized non-sensitive demo, signed-off receipt and usability notes; use Systemia admission and outbound/privacy/Payments gate rather than treating this prototype as a deployed service.

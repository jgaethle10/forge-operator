Important: packaging is not transmission

Your email, text, cloud or collaboration platform must still carry every piece and the manifest, or a permitted secure link to the original. Splitting doesn't override attachment limits, quotas, policy or security. This first prototype handles ordinary non-sensitive files up to 64 MiB in-browser. Don't use it for confidential customer data without an approved encrypted transfer channel.

Designed for Evercraft's Distribution Engine commercial pilot. All operations run locally in this browser. The prototype does not send emails, receive payments, store customer files, encrypt content, bypass restrictions or guarantee recipient access. SHA-256 proves byte integrity against a trusted manifest, not the identity or trustworthiness of the sender. Test recipients, mobile Safari and accessibility require separate acceptance before commercial release.

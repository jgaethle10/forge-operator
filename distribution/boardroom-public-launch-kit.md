## Distribution standards

Publish via the owned Evercraft Clip lane when available and authorized. Do not imply an asset is posted without a channel publication receipt.
Use problem-first search intent: "why ChatGPT and Claude disagree", "how to compare AI answers for a business decision", "pressure test business decision with AI".
Share only in channels where it answers a current question or where relevant promotion is welcomed. No mass unsolicited messaging.
Record impressions, unique visits, free starts, completed comparison downloads, scoped-review inquiries, checkout starts, verified purchases and fulfilled deliveries separately. Missing is not zero.
Use attribution links only if real analytics are enabled; untracked clicks are not sales proof.

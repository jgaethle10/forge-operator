## The precise commercial offer
Evercraft Research is **publicly available as an early-access research inquiry service**. A customer can use the free client-side scope builder, select a research type, and download a scoped outline. An actual research report is a **separately quoted, professionally reviewed engagement**; no instant autonomous job or new Research Stripe checkout is offered. The source-owned evidence-dossier compiler exists, but does not by itself perform independent research or deliver a customer report.

## Publishing standard (do not substitute volume for quality)
- Use naturally flowing, substantive writing. There is **no required paragraph count**: one effective sentence is enough when that is the right format, and a longer narrative is appropriate when it adds value. Never pad, force transitions or split every dramatic phrase onto its own line. Avoid choppy AI slam-poetry formatting, repetitive hooks, empty intensity, and boilerplate.
- Give each channel an authentic voice and one honest action that fits its audience. X may be a natural single paragraph within its character limit. Avoid link-stuffing and mechanical repackaging of identical posts.
- Company and personal Facebook feed captions are **link-free**; place the verified Research URL in the **first comment after the feed post exists**. That comment must also receive a provider receipt, or mark "comment not verified." No fabricated comment screenshot.
- Instagram and TikTok use profile link or approved Story link, never claim the caption URL is clickable. No video claim without a real original screen recording or rendered and reviewed motion asset.
- Use actual first-party Research page media only, preferably a clean owned Chromium source frame or the founder's genuine iPhone walkthrough. Do not use invented dashboard or simulated findings as a screenshot. Review rights, crop, readability, inaccessible content, and mobile first.
- Public posts say "free scope builder," never "free research report" or "AI autonomously researches for you." Approved research prices and live Research checkout are still unverified.
- No Base44 or Metricool, no raw third-party publisher bypass. First-party Clip adapter must authenticate exact identity and allowlist the account, with valid source/media and unique content digest.
- Restricted destinations: no posting Research promotions on EPS, Havenly or R&B Chicken & Soul unless the founder separately approves a relevant brand exception. Do not use personal accounts automatically. Avoid unrelated brand pages despite existing access.
- Systemia authorizations must be issuer-backed and channel-scoped; founder campaign direction does **not** replace provider authorization. Record the exact post URL, provider post ID, actual publish timestamp and content hash before counting delivery.
- No unsolicited bulk DMs, unconsented group posting, fake engagement, testimonials, customer citations, conversion numbers or invented purchases.

## Continuous buyer-exposure loop
1. **Daily source/proof check:** Research website latest external HTTPS canary; source truth, offering scope, platform identity, ready licensed media.
2. **Queue preparation:** at least two distinct original and source-supported packages, with exact target audience and content digest. Never prepare a duplicate purely to hit a schedule.
3. **Systemia editorial/rights/permissions gate:** paragraph quality, proof type, contrast, family safety, legal/no-contact/brand boundaries and approved publisher adapters.
4. **Clip delivery:** publish only eligible unique items with scoped issuer permission and verified channel. Fail closed on ambiguous API responses. Check destination URL/comment/video availability.
5. **CRM:** count consented genuine inquiries, requested scopes, approved quotes, paid provider receipts and delivered reports separately. No anonymous group scraping, unsolicited direct messages or invented attribution.
6. **Weekly learning:** inspect saves, shares, qualified research inquiries, quote acceptance, delivery friction and repeat purchases. Rotate topics by observed buyer need. Recycle *ideas* with new proof, never spam duplicate content.
7. **When blocked:** report exact blocker among PLATFORM_AUTH, APPROVAL, MEDIA_PROOF, CLIP_RUNTIME, PROVIDER_RECEIPT, COMMENT_RECEIPT, INQUIRY_ROUTE, or PRODUCT_DELIVERY. Publishing code and a GitHub commit do not equal platform posts.

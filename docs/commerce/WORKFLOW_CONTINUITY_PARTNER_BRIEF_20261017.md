## One-sentence partner pitch
**Your clients already trust you with their systems. Evercraft helps investigate the handoffs those systems keep losing, document the evidence, and scope the smallest defensible fix without pretending a diagnosis is a production deployment.**

## Best-fit partner and end-client
**Partner:** trade-service consultants, managed-service providers, workflow automation consultants, website agencies and small enterprise IT integrators with accountable relationships to owners and operations managers.

**Client trigger:** leads vanish between a website/contact form, inbox, estimating, scheduling and follow-up; legacy software is brittle; staff cannot explain which application owns a task; failed checkouts or agent actions cannot be reconstructed with sufficient evidence.

**Not a fit:** clients asking for guaranteed revenue uplift, unsafe production automation, access without authorization, regulated/licensed work beyond Evercraft's capacity, a promised public marketplace listing, or unsupervised access to private systems.

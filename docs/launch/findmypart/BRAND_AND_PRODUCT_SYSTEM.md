# FINDMYPART
## Brand and product system · Launch candidate 01
**Status:** source-ready, launch gate **HOLD**. Do not use publicly as a claim that checkout or sourcing is operating.

### The point of view
A missing component can strand a vehicle, machine, restoration or repair. The problem is rarely that the owner lacks another search box. The problem is that nobody is paid to investigate the uncertainty after the catalog ends.

**Positioning:** FindMyPart is an evidence-first parts research service for hard-to-source, obsolete and partly identified components. We investigate sourcing and repair pathways, show what is known, what is provisional, and what requires verification. The purchase is **research**, not replacement inventory.

**Tagline:** Missing part. Not the end.

**One-line promise:** When the usual catalogs stop, FindMyPart helps you work out the next evidence-backed sourcing move.

**Category:** Parts recovery intelligence and human-reviewed sourcing research.
**Operated by:** Evercraft LLC.
**Primary customer:** Independent repair shops, restorers, machinery owners, equipment maintainers and DIY owners with a stopped project. First launch wedge: one stalled shop job.
**Anti-claim:** We don't own a parts warehouse, promise a source, claim universal fitment or guarantee repair.
**Brand personality:** Resourceful, precise, quietly relentless. Not mystical AI, not a hype-filled parts marketplace.
**Tone:** Plain-spoken and grounded. Translate complex supply chains to concrete next steps. Say *candidate*, *observed*, *needs confirmation*, *unverified stock*, not *found* unless independently confirmed.

### Brand anatomy
**Mark:** A locator ring that does not fully close around a machined part. The gap signifies unfinished search. The center is an evidence signal, not a search-engine magnifying glass. Original scalable vector artwork in `public/findmypart/brand`.

### The buyer experience
1. **Recognize**: “Missing part. Not the end.” The page identifies their exact pain without overselling.
2. **Clarify**: Part description, model, markings, suppliers tried. Unknown details remain unknown.
3. **Scope**: One-time Quick $19, Deep $49, Rescue $99. Every tier is a research service, not the cost of the component.
4. **Confirm**: A human-readable scope; no checkout without owned secure storage and verified product/price.
5. **Pay**: Provider checkout is **a prepared checkout only** until an authoritative payment receipt confirms settlement.
6. **Investigate**: Exact identity -> cross reference -> salvage -> documentation -> fabrication/alternative only as appropriate.
7. **Deliver**: Human-reviewed findings with source evidence, uncertainty and unresolved steps. Customer delivery and acknowledgment are distinct evidence events.
8. **Learn**: Ask customers whether the evidence got them a next move; request permission before a case study or testimonial.

### Product tiers (approved research price only)
| Tier | Price | Buyer problem | Deliverable scope |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Quick Hunt | $19 one-time | Known part, limited search | Identifiers, likely exact/superseded routes, source leads and gaps |
| Deep Hunt | $49 one-time | Discontinued/unclear part | Quick scope + salvage/donor, documents, alternatives and reasoning |
| Rescue Hunt | $99 one-time | Project-stopping part | Deep scope + technical recovery/fabrication route research and explicit validation needs |

No monthly Shop Plan; no automatic commissions. Final scope is confirmed before payment; no supply, fitment, licensing, delivery-time or fabricated solution guarantee.

### Competitive clarity
Parts distributors often advertise free availability quotes. We do not pretend a paid research hunt is the only way to look for a part. The paid value must be a **documented, independently checked research process** across sourcing paths when routine quotes and search are exhausted. Launch must prove this difference with an actual anonymized delivered report before scaling.

### Conversion and trust
Single primary action: **Send a part request**. A buyer-controlled email is the interim contact path, not anonymous public form submission. Secondary action: **Review Quick Hunt offer**, contingent on owned runtime. Never add a payment link copied from a different similarly priced SKU. Avoid surprise subscription enrollment.
The storefront is intentionally `noindex,nofollow` until actual owner-hosted and payment/fulfillment release criteria are satisfied.

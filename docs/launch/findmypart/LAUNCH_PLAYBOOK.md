# FINDMYPART | Go-to-market and launch control
Date: 2026-10-28 · Stage: prelaunch, no outbound/send authority from this document

## Launch thesis
**Not a second parts marketplace. An independent investigation when the parts marketplace is out of answers.**

**Wedge:** repair-shop owners with one stalled customer job. An owner is more likely to understand the cost of a bay waiting on a discontinued component than the broad public encountering a generic $19 research charge. The first proof case should be narrow and legitimate, not a fabricated dramatic success.

**Priority offer:** Quick $19 when the part number is known. Deep $49 when identity/sources require breadth. Rescue $99 only when technical recovery planning is justified. This is not tier arbitrage or a different product’s checkout.

## Launch sequence
### P0: Make a complete buyer journey actually work
- Stage brand landing under owned `/findmypart/`, held from indexing. No retired platform.
- Finish PR #1598 and Issue #1600 with encrypted, scope-bound intake; exact payment and receipt verification; handle failures and refunds.
- Confirm actual owned Fabric public routing and Caddy TLS; capture read-only endpoint proof, mobile viewport screenshots, and deployed commit.
- Validate all three prices and SKUs through real no-charge provider-session creation in safe mode, then one buyer-consented real payment with correct downstream fulfillment and a real delivery.
- Build a **real** research report with evidence/uncertainty from actual customer authorization. Keep customer identity and photos private; request written permission for an anonymized public case.
- Only after passing gates: remove `noindex`, verify canonical/OG social card, support route, receipts, CRM and social publisher.

### P1: First focused, consent-based distribution
**B2B referral lane**: make a shop-facing landing variation and a partner FAQ; offer the free referral kit only once an owned public signup and attribution path exists. Do not pretend the old test link is the current product. No commissions until signed terms.
**Buyer content**: publish one documented "why this part search stalls" case per week. Show identifiable problem and researched routes; do not claim inventory or substitute fit.
**Channel use**: Evercraft-owned Relay/Clip publishing only if independent runtime and provider receipts verify that no retired hosted platform is being used. Otherwise stage content and hold automated publishing. Trade associations, repair-shop communities and specialist forums only where rules allow sharing, with useful researched answers before any link. Never DM a scraped list or bypass CRM contact suppression.
**Partner lane**: allow opt-in outreach to independent restorers, salvage vendors, remanufacturers and shops with verified individual permission and active outbound authorization. Keep relationships in Systemia CRM.

### P2: Measure first repeatable unit
Funnel states must be independently attributable:
`qualified_visitor` -> `consented_brief` -> `accepted_scope` -> `checkout_open` -> `payment_verified` -> `fulfilled` -> `customer_acknowledged`.
Never count synthetic QA, a checkout session, an open email app, a social post, or a partner test as revenue.

**Operating scorecard (weekly, not invented):**
- landing route uptime and client errors
- source/medium and actual click-to-intake conversion
- non-QA consented part briefs, approved scope, checkout attempts
- verified paid hunts by tier, refunds, net payments
- time spent researching per tier, actual costs
- fulfilled reports, evidence citations, outcome classification
- qualified shop repeat requests and consented referrals
- outstanding payment errors and privacy incidents

**Growth decisions:** If the first 10 genuine part briefs do not progress through scoping, fix positioning/qualification before paid promotion. If delivery labor exceeds margin, adjust scope, price, or tier rules with new approval rather than pretending automated results. Do not use a target or illustrative number as observed revenue.

### Consumer opener (organic public post)
If the label says discontinued, your project still deserves a real answer. FindMyPart researches exact sources, cross-references, salvage and documentation, separating live evidence from guesses. Bring the part, model or even partial markings. We'll establish scope before any paid hunt.

### Homepage conversion rule
One primary CTA: **Tell us about your part**. The first contact asks only what part and what machine it belongs to. Clarify pricing and verified availability before payment; show the sample research report as secondary proof. All additional data belongs in later, context-aware intake steps. No forced account creation, extra phone calls, AI-agent explanation or six-stage ladder to reach an answer.

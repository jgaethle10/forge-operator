# FindMyPart: universal recovery engine
Status: contract implemented, search adapters / image recognition / actual CAD verification NOT implemented.

## Product mission
Keep useful objects in service when one missing component would otherwise strand or scrap them. Accept text, image references, part numbers, videos, manuals, audio, measurements and CAD references across appliances, tools, machinery, cars, boats and more.

## Six parallel recovery tracks
1. Original replacement (OEM, new old stock, worldwide availability).
2. Interchange (supersessions, cross-brand exact interfaces).
3. Salvage (donors, used and refurbished components).
4. CAD and fabrication (authorized drawings, 3D-printing models, machining).
5. Repair (rebuild and refurbish a component).
6. Engineered substitute (adaptation only after dimensional/material/load/safety review).

## Proof discipline
An unsearched track says not_searched; a collected URL is only an unverified lead. Never infer that a part is stocked, compatible, printable, legal to distribute, or safe because a listing/model exists. Do not invent global coverage or source results. Safety-critical replacements require qualified human/engineering validation.

## Next integrations
Source adapters with approved API/access terms and provenance; image ingest with explicit customer permission and controlled private storage; dimensional and manufacturer identity extraction; CAD license/material/tolerance checks; evidence scoring; self-service result presentation and optional paid escalation. No public uploads or automatic supplier purchases until their own release gates pass.

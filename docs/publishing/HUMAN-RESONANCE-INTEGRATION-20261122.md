## Adoption contract
The complementary Human Resonance Standard at `docs/standards/human-resonance-standard.md` adds **voice preservation** and **truthful emotional relevance** as editorial review dimensions. Do not replace the above original source-first rules or relax sourcing, privacy, media rights, independent review or channel-specific authorization.

When a specialist drafts or revises copy, it should:
1. Read the current authoritative channel standards and the actual original copy before editing.
2. Identify the authentic human stakes, audience, author's distinctive language, imagery and natural rhythm.
3. Correct errors minimally. Preserve intentional poetic diction, including unusual but meaningful words.
4. Avoid paragraph breaks without a genuine change of thought; never impose a universal paragraph count.
5. Confirm each factual claim, right to use imagery, privacy boundary and appropriate action request.
6. Preserve the exact candidate and review outcome in existing release evidence; do not equate generated copy with a sent message, approved post or shipped commercial.

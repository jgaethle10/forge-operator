## 1. Evercraft's baseline editorial character
Write as an accountable institution that actually builds, observes, researches and delivers, not a generic AI marketing account. A publishable statement must respect source status: observed, independently corroborated, publicly reported, inferred/modelled, planned, blocked, or unknown. Separate what exists today from what is still in development or merely promised.

- Use cohesive, naturally developed paragraphs. Combine adjacent thoughts that belong together. No isolated one-line dramatic fragments, fake suspense, motivational slogans, generic 'AI revolution' copy, unnatural staccato cadence, or paragraphs beginning with 'And'. Prefer plain natural contractions when appropriate. Avoid em dashes.
- Do not use hashtags as a campaign tactic, engagement bait, salesy filler, unsubstantiated superlatives, fake testimonials, or decorative emoji in corporate posts and branded visuals. A relevant symbol in an informal customer reply is not an exemption from clarity and taste.
- The best creative and truth should carry the post. Sources, real visuals, compelling actual results, and brand-specific identity are more persuasive than a lengthy explanation of internal machinery. Avoid repetitive stock footage, invented images of work, and unlicensed media.
- **Visual art direction (November 21 founder update):** For new editorial Page graphics, read [`HARBOR-VISUAL-EDITORIAL-DIRECTION-20261121.md`](HARBOR-VISUAL-EDITORIAL-DIRECTION-20261121.md). Prefer the authentic story, documentary evidence or a focused original visualization over another brand-colored headline rectangle. Keep it restrained, specific, legible on a phone, and honestly labeled. This is a visual-quality amendment, not authorization to manufacture news imagery, pause all posting, or bypass existing gates.
- Record actual independent team/editorial/visual QA where required. Never assert 'team reviewed' if no specialist actually reviewed the exact publication version.

## 2. The four distinct communication surfaces

### A. Evercraft Journal / substantive investigations
Journal-first for substantial surprising verifiable findings, then distribute approved original derivatives. Articles need full, developed reporting, sourcing and uncertainty, evidence/attribution/adversarial check, authentic rights-cleared visual, suitable layout and actual publishing department closure. Do not shrink serious journalism into a choppy caption or imply a draft is published. Preserve the founder's established long-form substantive-paragraph requirement where applicable. Sensitive public-interest claims require stronger independent human editorial review.

### B. Evercraft company and product Facebook/Instagram/LinkedIn Pages
A **Page post is neither an article nor a Messenger reply**. Fit the destination audience and existing brand voice; a concise company update might be 1–3 connected, readable paragraphs, and a substantial source-backed result may justifiably be longer. There is no mandatory paragraph count for social posts. Lead with something specific and valuable: genuine work/result, useful explanation, visual demonstration or a clearly qualified real offer.

For an actual offer, include verifiable price if important (e.g., limited $50 founding commercial), true deliverable, current capacity restrictions if material, and one low-friction CTA. Avoid claiming immediate guaranteed fulfillment if operator/capacity remains unverified. Do not replace customer-facing benefits with a detailed description of internal workflows, or make a 30-second video studio look like a text-only documentation service. Distinct platform variants, no generic bulk identical cross-posting.

For Fallen Studios specifically: demonstrate visual craft with an authentic permission-cleared real commercial or original clearly labeled internal demo before building a promise around production prowess. Text-only explanation may be useful as an occasional operational post, not the primary creative sales format. Use truthful terms: 30-second vertical, one concept/revision, preview-before-pay for accepted scoped projects, and owner-confirmed capacity. A Page post does not itself prove buyer acceptance or finished preview capacity.

### C. Founder personal monetized social profile
Separate first-person creator voice and the authorized audience from corporate advertising. Authentic field notes, Build Log, Look Closer, Week in Motion and original personal perspective. Follow actual per-format earnings evidence, not guesswork, but don't turn personal feed into repetitive sales posts. Personal-profile posting requires specific consent/authority; corporate Page permissions do not authorize it. Never use private family conflicts, children, customer footage or customer identity to create engagement without the necessary separate consent.

### D. Messenger / customer replies
Messenger is not a public editorial release. Speak person-to-person, **shorter than the public posts**, warmly and in direct response to the actual question. Default to 1 cohesive reply of 1–4 conversational sentences. If the customer asks two related questions, answer both clearly in one compact message. No repeated introduction or sales script, no redundant process recitation, no huge multi-paragraph blue bubbles, no internal Systemia/Fabric/agent/queue jargon. Ask at most the single missing thing needed to progress; don't re-request answers or assets already given.

Never send filler solely because the backend is blocked. Don't repeatedly mention unverified queue/timeline as an excuse. Once a real operator and timeline are confirmed, communicate an accurate concise update. If no promised date exists, do not fabricate one. Read thread history before posting, avoid duplicate sends, and send meaningful updates only when needed.

**Ricky conversation corrective example:** Customer asked whether original logo PDF is needed and whether better product photos/videos can be sent later. Appropriate *future style*, not a resend:
> The logo picture works, and if you have the original PNG or PDF, feel free to send it. Better photos or clips later today are welcome too, especially anything showing the guacamole. We've got a good starting point!
Actual reply already sent; **do not send this example as another message**. Logo picture suitability is reported by Harbor, not independently verified by this document. The missing production operator is an *internal* Systemia/Fallen blocker, not a reason to send another lengthy status bubble.

## 3. Exact-artifact release checklist: STOP, REVISE or PUBLISH
Every candidate must retain a per-destination review packet:
1. **Purpose and audience:** correct owned Page, topical relevance, brand identity, approved positioning, not any unrelated 22-Page mass distribution.
2. **Claim/evidence:** verified statements and correct timestamps, uncertainty and counterevidence; don't imply planned development is live, accepted, paid, or guaranteed. Confirm links and prices against actual provider/current source.
3. **Editorial and style:** coherent developed thought, no artificial chopped lines, no unnecessary 'And' opener, em dashes, hype/hashtags/stickers, overlong process exposition, repeated paragraphs or irrelevant CTA. Correct tone/length per surface.
4. **Visual and rights:** authentic, specific, source-proven image/video, commercial-use permissions and approved customer reuse. Real demonstrations for creative offers wherever possible; no generic fabricated substitute.
5. **Independent review:** exact copy+media pair receives required publishing/design/fact/brand/rights/platform checks with real named evidence; no self-certification as full-team closure. If no genuine review evidence, hold when required.
6. **Platform and authority:** right Page/account, correct format, mobile legibility, current authorization and compliance. Existing standing authorization may cover routine posts; sensitive articles, private creator profile and exceptions preserve explicit approval.
7. **Release verification:** provider ID, public permalink, timestamp, destination readback and dedup key, recorded separately from prepared/scheduled; failures are HELD, not magically live.
8. **Learning:** measured reach, engagement, useful replies, qualified leads, actual acceptance/payments and customer outcomes are separate. Estimate and unknown are not settled revenue.

If a candidate fails, **REVISE BEFORE PUBLISHING** rather than overloading the founder with a rejected output. For already-live posts, do not delete, edit, boost, repost or send corrective DMs without appropriate authority. Review the learning, prepare a materially better original next post, and return its QA evidence.

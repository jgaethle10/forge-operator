# Evercraft Human Resonance Standard

Status: Proposed company-wide editorial doctrine for review and integration.

## Principle
Do not manufacture emotion. Discover the emotion already present in a true story, preserve it, and give it room to breathe. Technology must serve human understanding, not replace it with generic language.

## Editorial rules
1. Preserve the author's distinctive vocabulary, imagery, metaphors, cadence, emotional arc, and intention. Correct mechanics without erasing voice.
2. Paragraphs follow changes in thought, not arbitrary sentence counts. Do not split cohesive prose into theatrical one-sentence paragraphs.
3. Avoid generic AI marketing copy, manufactured urgency, false intimacy, exaggerated claims, hollow superlatives, and performative empathy.
4. Begin with the actual human stakes: what matters to the reader, customer, worker, researcher, or community. Use verified details rather than invented stories.
5. Write with precision and beauty. Unusual language may be intentional; ask before normalizing it.
6. Honest emotion outranks manipulation. Respect autonomy and privacy, and never exploit fear, grief, insecurity, or vulnerability to drive sales.
7. Keep facts, sources, limitations, and uncertainty visible. Poetry must not become evidence.
8. Every customer-facing message should sound like it was written for that specific audience, not pasted from a template.

## Application
- Journal and Harbor: source-grounded discovery, curiosity, clarity, emotional meaning, restrained imagery.
- Evercraft Clip and social: human voice, platform-appropriate length, no manufactured engagement bait.
- Commercial Studio / Fallen Studios: believable narration, natural delivery, intentional pacing, sound and visuals that serve the story.
- Websites and product UX: make visitors feel understood; communicate the benefit in human terms; preserve accessibility and clarity.
- Outreach and sales: investigate the recipient first, name a verified relevant need, offer a concrete solution, avoid pressure and false familiarity.
- Research and safety: never let emotional framing distort evidence, risk, or conclusions.

## Review rubric (human/editor or automated preflight)
- Authentic voice retained?
- Human stakes identifiable and specific?
- Every claim supported?
- Paragraph structure natural?
- Language free of stock AI phrasing?
- Emotion respectful rather than manipulative?
- Clear next step when appropriate?

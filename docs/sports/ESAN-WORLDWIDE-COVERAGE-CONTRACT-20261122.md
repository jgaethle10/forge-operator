# ESAN | Worldwide sports coverage and verification contract
**Founder directive:** November 22, 2026 (Pacific)  
**Mission:** Evercraft Sports and Athletics Network (ESAN)  
**Scope:** ALL sports, ALL geographies, ALL legitimate levels of competition  
**Owner:** Systemia / Kaidance / Harbor / Sports Spine + Game Fabric  
**Status:** source-controlled editorial and candidate-routing instruction. NOT a Harbor runtime ACK, deployed collector, authorized post, or live publication receipt.

## 1. Audience and charter
**ESAN covers the sporting world.** Local coverage is one meaningful part, never the geographic center, default filter, or maximum reach. Discover worldwide professional, international, collegiate, high-school, youth (public-and-consent-safe), adaptive/para, amateur, grassroots, women's, men's, mixed and emerging competitions. Include both major and overlooked sports without assuming the familiar US professional leagues define sport.

Examples of beats, not a closed whitelist: global football/soccer, American football, basketball, baseball/softball, ice and field hockey, cricket, rugby, tennis, golf, volleyball, combat sports, motorsports, cycling, track and field, swimming, gymnastics, winter sports, skateboarding, surf, esports, and more. Preserve room for new sports, regional terminology and local-language sources. Use local experts and original voices where possible; do not manufacture authority.

**Priority is public importance, novelty, compelling verified achievement and audience fit, not proximity to Evercraft's location.** No fixed US/PNW quota. Do not turn international coverage into a ceremonial once-per-week slot. Operate across time zones where actual verified collection and operators permit; aspire to continuous coverage without claiming a 24/7 system that has not been proven.

## 2. Story candidate and coverage diversity
Store discovery candidates with `event_id`, `sport`, `competition`, `competition_level`, `location_region`, `country_or_territory`, `event_start_utc`, `event_local_time_and_zone`, `evidence_checked_at_utc`, `source_urls`, `source_kind`, `claim_status`, `potential_ESAN_angle`, `media_rights`, `athlete_privacy`, `dedup_key`, `current_destination_status`, and explicit `hold_reason` when blocked.
- Select important verified breaking results, genuinely consequential records, undercovered athletes and teams, explanatory play analysis, fan-safe historical context and original culture reporting. A queue item is not a reported or published story.
- Rank fairly by verified significance, freshness, original angle, rights-cleared media availability and missing coverage across sports/continents/levels. Review skew by region, gender, league and sport; do not enforce mechanical parity that creates filler.
- Journal hosts longer, source-linked reporting when its owned origin and editorial gate are actually approved. The ESAN Page receives the distinct social version. Clip/Fallen makes rights-cleared original assets, not the currently unverified publishing transport.
- Candidate ESAN Page ID from November handoff: `513781131811732`. **Always reread Harbor's current authorized Page identity/permissions register and provider readback before any post.** Historic ID is not live-release authority.

## 3. Exact sports-fact preflight (must pass before release)
1. Verify participating teams/athletes, event, competition, venue/date, result, final/live/overtime/extra-time status and winner from official league/team/event/organizer source(s) or explicitly qualified credible independent reporting. Record each source URL and checked timestamp. Distinguish unofficial results from certified final.
2. **Never write a bare ambiguous ranking** when major recognized systems differ. Store `ranking_system` (e.g. AP Top 25, CFP committee, Coaches Poll, official world ranking), `ranking_as_of`, `ranking_scope` (team/player/competition), `rank_value`, source URL and applicable week. Do not treat poll order, playoff seeding, live table position or an ESAN model's own power ranking as interchangeable. An article can accurately say “No. 6 in the AP poll; No. 8 in the CFP rankings” when both are source-verified for the same time. If only one system is sourced, name it. Rankings can change after the event; freeze the referenced snapshot.
3. Cross-check score and period totals; identify regulation vs OT, shootouts, aggregate scores and penalties appropriate to the sport. Do not infer a result from a screenshot alone or claim a preliminary figure is official.
4. When source disagreements remain, attribute the dispute and hold an unqualified definitive headline. Put corrections in the article/update log and affected approved platform post; prevent silent overwrite and duplicate re-posting.
5. Avoid unverified allegations, injuries, private health/discipline details, private youth-athlete information, or non-public recruiting/performance data. Public fan reporting and restricted Game Fabric coaching dossiers remain separate.
6. **Media is not free because it is online.** Prefer original licensed footage and photography, rights-cleared creator material, approved native shares and links, or labeled source-based original diagrams. Do not rip broadcast highlights or present generative recreations as footage.
7. Perform existing Harbor copy, visual, brand, permissions, idempotency, provider and independent public-readback gates. An ESAN candidate cannot bypass an existing Systemia hold, even for major breaking news.

## 4. Reuse existing Evercraft systems
- **Systemia:** source provenance, mission and release admission, Page registry, dedup/rights/privacy/authorization. No new publisher, scheduler or competing ledger.
- **Kaidance:** coordinate the existing five-minute heartbeat *when technically connected and evidenced*; do not claim a confirmed Harbor transport from GitHub instructions.
- **Sports Spine / Chronosport:** canonical game/result history, evidence and model-vs-observed distinction; **Game Fabric:** public evidence and analysis. Restricted coach intelligence never bleeds into ESAN public fan coverage.
- **Harbor:** current authorized route is Systemia-approved package → Harbor validation → Meta provider posting and readback. Do not claim Evercraft Clip itself published until Clip transport is evidenced.
- **Evercraft Journal / Clip / Fallen:** sourced depth, real original visuals and derivatives, only through their existing approved release paths.

## 5. Acceptance test and reporting
- A slate containing verified stories from at least three different continents and three different sports can be evaluated without any Yakima/PNW preference. **Example test data do not authorize publication.**
- A story with two distinct valid ranking systems and dates has a clear attributed headline; one with a bare `No. 6` and mismatched systems is HELD/REVISED, not guessed into a single ranking.
- A match marked final must have a checked authoritative result and stage-specific scoring convention. Any missing source, time, rights or target authority is HELD.
- Record attempted discoveries per geography/sport, verified candidates, editorial holds, actual provider post IDs/permalinks, correction events and time-to-first-confirmed-post, distinguishing unknown from zero. No claim of actual worldwide coverage, 24/7 intake, new source connectors, or deployed live preflight until independently evidenced.
- Report first verified ESAN global sports source → approved ESAN Page → provider ID/public readback, and only then characterize the route as functioning end-to-end.

## Responsibilities

| Day | Deliverable | Gate |

## Required per-game record
- School/team names, sport, level, date, local time, venue and game ID.
- Each schedule/score claim: exact source URL, publisher, observed/retrieved timestamp, corroboration, rights status and freshness.
- Source classification: official, independent, user-provided, unverified; evidence state: observed, inferred, modeled, unmapped.
- Ontology compatibility: 8 realms, 33 layers, 339 **proposed** facets, sport adapter and unmapped list. Never convert proposed facets into measured facts.
- At least two competing hypotheses and a meaningful counterexample, without fabricated tactical detail.
- Conditional coaching questions, film acquisition leads with permission status, and postgame validation checkpoints.
- Two outputs: public fan-safe preview and access-controlled coach decision brief.

## Hard publication blocks
No scraping, importing, exposing or redistributing restricted footage. No claims of watched film based on listings or scores. No private under-18 athlete information. Team preparation is not consent-based Talent Atlas representation. No outbound contact or publication before Systemia contact preflight where applicable, rights/privacy and coaching relevance checks, and explicit release authorization. No claim of backend deployment, scheduled social posts, revenue, or successful publication without receipts.

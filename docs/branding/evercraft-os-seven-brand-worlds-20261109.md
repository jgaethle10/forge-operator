# The Seven Brand Worlds | Evercraft OS

**Working creative direction, November 9, 2026.** This document defines storytelling and visual production, not trademark clearance, independent company registration, a reissue of Stripe products, or completed market launch.

## Brand architecture

Evercraft OS is the underlying operating system and portfolio endorsement. Buyers meet the individual product first. Each identity has its own purpose, origin narrative, visual language, signature mark, voice and constraints. The seven exact original provider checkouts, dollar amounts, receipt/fulfillment gates and product boundaries remain unchanged. Payment settlements are under Systemia Stripe, with Evercraft Payments reconciliation presently manual.

Use the signature name in creative storytelling, and keep the canonical original service name and exact price beside every buyer checkout. Avoid presenting an unverified creative imprint as trademark-registered or domain-cleared.

## The Weekly Signal

- **Commercial product:** Evercraft Social | Weekly (original checkout Social Media Management · Weekly, $100)
- **Purpose:** Make consistent communication achievable for small teams without forcing an agency retainer.
- **Signature:** “Show up. Say something that matters.”
- **Story:** Some businesses have plenty to say and no spare afternoon to say it. The Weekly Signal exists for the week when showing up matters more than producing another elaborate plan.
- **Belief:** Consistency is built one honest week at a time.
- **Voice:** energetic, kind, practical; short rhythmic sentences; no hype
- **Visual:** #141d2c foundation, #ffad79 primary accent, #ffddbd secondary, rising-signal vector emblem, repeating ascending bars and weekly cadence lines
- **Guardrails:** No vanity metrics promised; Real permissions before publishing; One week is a complete, bounded engagement
- **Product link:** https://jgaethle10.github.io/forge-operator/mesh/checkout-ready/social-media-weekly/
- **Reusable emblem:** https://jgaethle10.github.io/forge-operator/mesh/checkout-ready/social-media-weekly/brand-mark.svg

## The Second Look

- **Commercial product:** Evercraft Asset Diagnosis (original checkout Asset Diagnosis, $149)
- **Purpose:** Reveal what is actually happening inside one asset before the owner spends on the wrong fix.
- **Signature:** “See it clearly before changing it.”
- **Story:** When a website, workflow or tool underperforms, the instinct is to rebuild. The Second Look begins with a quieter question: what do we actually know?
- **Belief:** Evidence should come before expensive changes.
- **Voice:** clear-eyed, exact, curious; never alarmist
- **Visual:** #0d202c foundation, #71c5ed primary accent, #c0e7f6 secondary, focus-frame vector emblem, framing corners, grids and a single focal point
- **Guardrails:** State what is observed versus inferred; Don't promise a rebuild inside a diagnosis; Respect access boundaries
- **Product link:** https://jgaethle10.github.io/forge-operator/mesh/checkout-ready/asset-diagnosis/
- **Reusable emblem:** https://jgaethle10.github.io/forge-operator/mesh/checkout-ready/asset-diagnosis/brand-mark.svg

## Implementation and publishing

The lived experience of each brand is in `public/mesh/checkout-ready/<slug>/index.html` and its `brand.json` and `brand-mark.svg`. The shared framework `brand-world.css` defines seven distinct motifs and typography treatments rather than forcing one generic template. The brand atlas at https://jgaethle10.github.io/forge-operator/mesh/checkout-ready/brands/ previews the portfolio together.

**Proof required before stronger claims:** verified channel-specific publication receipts, genuine outside traffic and qualified inquiries, agreed scopes, actual provider-paid receipts, reconciled Evercraft Payments orders, delivered artifacts and written acceptance. No first paid order is needed to ethically market an appropriately scoped existing checkout, but checkout visibility is never a paid receipt.

**Future design quality gate:** export responsive branded social cards and platform-safe media from original owned design assets after authorized distribution channels are confirmed. Test logo legibility, mobile layout, text contrast, and distinct storytelling. Trademark/name review is needed before treating the creative imprints as protected brand names. Do not replace the Stripe merchant account or exact original SKU based on an imprint alone.

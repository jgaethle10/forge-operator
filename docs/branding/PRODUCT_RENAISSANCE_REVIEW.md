# Evercraft Product Renaissance | Brand review and release rules

**State:** founder-review concepts. No final logo, name change, public launch, or replaced live page is approved by this file.

## Principle

Evercraft is the owner and trust signature, not a cookie-cutter skin. Preserve each product's audience, emotional tone, purpose, and original product identity. Avoid generic shared heroes and invented AI capabilities. Brand is a design layer on top of proven function, never a replacement for function.

## First review cohort

| Product | Who it's for | Editorial direction | What must work before launch |
|---|---|---|---|
| Infinite Classroom | Curious learners and appropriately authorized caregivers | Original six learning-world identity, joyful pastel palette, learning-first navigation | Contextual conversational tutoring and safety/consent evaluation; do not mislabel static exercises as tutor parity |
| Evercraft Circles | Professionals organizing intentional connections | Approachable editorial typography, sage/cream and relationship visualization | Member permissions, actual private profiles, controlled invitations/referrals and clear human introduction consent |
| Systemia Remote Ops | Entrepreneurs and simulator players | Serious business simulation interface with dramatic strategic visual hierarchy | Real choices, cashflow model, payroll/crew decisions, scenario saves and clear simulated-versus-real outputs |

## Identity components per product

1. **Verbal:** one clear promise, audience, tone, positioning, differentiation.
2. **Visual:** wordmark and symbol explorations, accessible colors, type system, icon vocabulary, layout, imagery rules, responsive examples. The current simple glyphs are temporary placeholders, not chosen marks.
3. **Product:** matching onboarding, navigation, first useful action, demo/sample states and truthful limitations.
4. **Commercial:** legal owner (Evercraft LLC), scope, support, availability, data permissions, price and correct Evercraft Payments order path. No checkout until independent evidence supports it.
5. **Distribution:** own stable canonical route, social profile/asset kit, search metadata and identity/brand governance.

## Approval stages

- historical_evidence_recovered: traceable record of purpose and features;
- brand_direction_concept: private/noindex concept; not public product;
- founder_visual_review: review exploration(s), not a final approval;
- functional_product_acceptance: runnable real workflow with tests, privacy and security;
- staging_independent_proof: owned hosting, browser and mobile, release receipt;
- founder_identity_approval: final logos, exact names, licensing/rights and site copy approved;
- public_release: independent HTTPS plus real task proof; payments separately gated.

**No Base44 contact:** do not request historical provider endpoints, SDKs, extraction, authentication, data or hosted runtimes. Missing materials stay explicitly missing.

**RIVET:** retire the public brand, preserve authorized reusable engineering and historical legal records.

**Clip:** founder deferred; exclude it from the active brand-recovery release queue.

## Next practical build slice

Give each first-cohort product a distinct responsive navigation, a real task that can be accomplished on owned infrastructure, and an independent acceptance test. Prioritize Infinite Classroom's context-aware teaching loop, Circles' participant-authorized circle prototype and Remote Ops' economic simulation. Reject fake counters, mock transaction success, nonfunctional buttons and made-up users. Do not migrate private data or launch unsafely.

## Identity proposal

### Name
**The Weekly Signal**

### Signature
> Show up. Say something that matters.

### Why it should exist
Small businesses want a consistent public presence but often cannot afford a long agency retainer. The Weekly Signal is meant to serve a specific, manageable unit of progress: one agreed week.

### Foundational story
Some businesses have plenty to say and no spare afternoon to say it. The Weekly Signal exists for the week when showing up matters more than producing another elaborate plan. This is **brand storytelling**, not an invented company founding story or a customer testimonial.

### Core belief and personality
- **Belief:** Consistency is built one honest week at a time.
- **Emotion:** Momentum.
- **Voice:** Energetic, kind, practical. Friendly and credible; clear full paragraphs rather than fragmented slogans. No exaggerated sales claims.
- **Promise:** A scoped one-week social support service, with concrete deliverables agreed before payment.
- **Audience:** Small-business owners who control their business's social accounts and need a specific week of help.

## Buyer story: example direction
*Running a business means there are always ten things more urgent than posting. But people should still know you're here. The Weekly Signal is built for a focused week of help: we agree on the channels, what needs to be created or managed, how approval works, and exactly what you'll receive. No long-term retainer required to start.*

This is proposed creative copy, not a published external ad or guarantee of specific content output.

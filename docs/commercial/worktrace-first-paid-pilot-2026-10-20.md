## Buyer problem and exact offer
Business operators repeatedly explain "how to do the job" in one-off screen shares. Text SOPs omit branching, tool context, and why an experienced person makes a judgment. AI agent builders need reproducible, permitted real-world task examples with verified outcomes, not just transcript files.

**Pilot offer: $450 fixed scope, subject to founder capacity confirmation.** Deliver **two** customer-selected, low-risk desktop workflows, each up to **10 minutes of approved recorded task time**, plus a 30-minute kickoff and one consolidated revision. Quote a larger custom engagement only after pilot acceptance. Customer must approve recording and business-owner authority. Proposed delivery target: **3 business days after authorized source files and necessary information arrive**, conditional on capacity confirmation. No automatic promise of deployment or AI automation.

Deliverables per workflow:
1. Customer-approved task objective, preconditions, app list, environment and explicit success/failure outcomes.
2. An authorized narrated MP4 or customer-provided recording, with local SHA-256 checksum and source timestamp.
3. Time-aligned transcript, human-checked decision points, corrections and task steps, with evidence offsets.
4. Human-readable operational guide (Markdown/PDF) and first-person decision map with exception/escalation branches.
5. Small evaluation card: input fixture, expected output, success criteria, forbidden actions, human review notes and reproducibility limits.
6. Private handoff with defined retention/deletion date, customer approval and revision; NO public publication by default.

**Separate quoted B2B research lane:** With task-specific scope, explicit downstream model-training/data licensing rights, participant consent, written buyer specification and data-security review, prepare an approved rights-cleared demonstration pack or agent evaluation benchmark. Do not assume a business's ordinary SOP license includes training rights. Do not resell employer/customer data because an employee personally recorded it.

## Guardrails
- Explicit written owner and participant permission prior to capture; tell the operator when recording starts/stops, allow instant opt-out.
- No covert monitoring, credentials, MFA, financial/medical/customer records, children, confidential third-party data, or background surveillance. Capture test fixtures instead.
- Do not promise automatic/anonymized handling based on a proposed feature. Inspect frame-by-frame for missed sensitive elements; obtain customer signoff before any external AI model processing.
- Define license scope, creator pay, usage rights and restrictions for every human demonstration; downstream model-training rights need separate express authorization.
- No identifiable real client records or captured data transferred to AI labs or brokers without case-specific contractual authority, legal/privacy review and data-subject protections.
- Honor deletion/retention and secure private delivery; audit access. Source verification and human QA precede publication.
- Synthetic transcripts may demonstrate output format, but MUST be labeled synthetic and cannot substitute for real expert demonstrations.

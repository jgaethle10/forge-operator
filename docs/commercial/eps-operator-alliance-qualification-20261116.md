# EPS Operator Alliance | Qualify real field partners
Date: 2026-11-16 · Existing EPS Partner Circle draft PR #2123

## Purpose and boundaries
Explore **separate-brand, nonexclusive** relationships with already-operating local companies. An operator provides its own registered business, trained people, equipment, services and licensed geography. Evercraft *may*, after proof and mutual approval, offer opt-in customer referrals, a defined software pilot, or separately contracted projects. We do not buy businesses, promise customers, issue work authority, offer exclusive territory, grant EPS trademark/contractor registration rights or create a franchise by describing it as an alliance.

**Legal/insurance holds:** Washington L&I bars unregistered contractors from borrowing another registration and demands proper prime/specialty scope, workers-comp and insurance verification. Personal labor versus independent contractor is fact-specific. Washington RCW 19.100.010 can count mandatory royalties/percent-of-sales fees as franchise fees where branded services and prescribed operations are also present. Qualified attorney review first. No mandatory fees or branding/territory rights proposed now. EPS GL insurer biBERK has issued a support case, but NOT active coverage confirmation; do not dispatch or quote outside verified scope.

## What actually qualifies an operator
1. Company owner/executive expresses interest; neither website presence nor public contact info is consent to a partnership.
2. Verify active **independent** legal entity, service locality, required contractor/other professional licenses, permitted specialty, bonding and appropriate insurance.
3. Verify workers-comp/payroll, staffing, equipment ownership/access, safety training, vehicle/hauling permits if needed, customer complaints/references with consent and job capacity.
4. Run a documented no-customer-data demo of only the actual available software. No shared CRM access, production tenancy, lead guarantees or claim that unfinished modules are commercial.
5. Agree exact contracting party, client disclosure/consent, scope, quote controls, labor, customer deposit handling, privacy, warranty/rework, termination and dispute process in reviewed writing.
6. Before any revenue splits, subscriptions, brand rights or managed territory program, obtain counsel review for franchise and multi-state rules. Then reconcile real paid project invoices, settlement and any operator payments.

## Why interest-only until reviewed
**Worker classification:** WA L&I says a remote person choosing their own hours is not automatically a contractor; a 1099 label is not determinative. A home-based salesperson doing phone/internet work is not automatically outside sales exempt. Many commission-paid employees still need minimum wage, time tracking, paid sick leave and overtime. Resolve each role's actual duties, classification, scope, training, compensation and reporting with WA employment counsel/payroll before advertising "commission-only WFH job" or accepting productive work.

**Business marketing:** WA L&I contractor registration / advertising requirements apply when soliciting construction services. Third-party state-data aggregators currently display EPS registration EVERCPS747OH, but do not rely on that as a present official verification. Check https://secure.lni.wa.gov/verify/ and make sure the correct registered business name/address/number appear on every approved advertisement or estimate, and verify service eligibility and endorsements.

**Earnings claims:** Do not advertise a specific earning amount, average, commission %, work-volume expectation, or "easy side income" until truthful supporting evidence, a legally reviewed compensation plan and any required disclosure exist. No buy-in, inventory, recruiting bonuses, downlines, rank ladders or income from selling participation.

**Consumer outreach:** Permissioned relevant introductions only. No scraped contact lists, unconsented mass SMS/DM/email, fake urgency, pressure at the door, or unauthorized statements about licensing, price, discounts or coverage.

## Applicant flow
1. Visitor may browse /eps-partners/, try local-only planner, and open the existing /partners/?focus=eps#apply.
2. Existing Partners consent form preselects the EPS-relevant **audience** option. The applicant may change it.
3. Existing owned private Fabric intake returns a received reference **only** if encrypted private storage succeeded. Otherwise mailto fallback requires applicant to press Send. The selected audience travels in the applicant-initiated message. A quote lead is not an approved partner.
4. Operator reads application privately, qualifies region/customer relationships, checks lawful worker category, completes any employment/onboarding documentation and tax/insurance duties **before** paid assignments or commissions.
5. After written authorization, provide only brand-approved communications and a legitimately issued EPS referral identifier, if the existing program passes reconciliation and attribution checks. No code from this site is minted or active.
6. Track **referrals** distinct from scope-qualified quotes, signed work, collected customer payment, completed job, any wage obligations and any legally authorized variable compensation.
7. Monthly pay/payout record must be reconciled against approved obligations and returns/chargebacks. Never represent work as completed from an inquiry alone.

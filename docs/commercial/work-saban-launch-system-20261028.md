## 2. Market, buyer and entry wedge
**Market reality:** many products already advertise 'AI employees', multi-agent teams and no-code workflow automation. Distinction must be objectively demonstrated, not asserted as superiority: visible work stages, independently recomputed arithmetic, explicit approvals, auditable traces and delivery.

**First buyer:** a small team with a monthly sales CSV and one manager who wants repeatable reporting, but has no time to manually consolidate sales. Potential early stakeholders: owner/operators, office managers, fractional COOs, bookkeepers and administrative teams. Disqualify jobs requiring restricted data, live external access, specialized accounting assurance or unsupported file types.

**Trigger question:** 'Every month, do you export sales into a spreadsheet and manually create the report your manager actually wants to read?'

**Single approved technical wedge:** authorized CSV with month and revenue columns, up to the local preview's documented limits. Work Saban computes month totals, produces a Markdown management report, independently audits arithmetic, and returns local receipts. Future wider workflows are roadmap, not saleable scope.

## 7. Distribution, with actual attribution rather than likes

2. **Permissioned channels:** existing Evercraft website directory; opted-in bookkeeping/operations consultants; appropriate professional communities that allow demos and pilot invitations; app or integration partners only where terms permit; no scraped or unsolicited bulk DMs.

6. **Commercial graduation:** approved exact offer/price; actual buyer-confirmed checkout; provider-paid receipt; matched task; audited delivery; second independently sourced customer.

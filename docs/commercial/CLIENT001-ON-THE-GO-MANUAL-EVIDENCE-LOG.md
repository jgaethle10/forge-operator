## The only workflow

1. **Evercraft records actions from our side**, including what was posted/sent, channel, link, date, and whether it worked. Keep original evidence in authorized storage rather than copying personal messages into this repo.
2. **Evercraft records volunteered interest** (public comments, inbound requests, business introductions). Do not confuse leads for Fallen Studios commercial orders with leads for On The Go oil-change services.
3. **When natural, not after every post**, ask Juan one unobtrusive question: “Have any customers mentioned seeing the commercial or that new page?” Silence means unknown; no repeat pressure.
4. **If Juan voluntarily reports a booking or completed paid job**, record aggregate counts, amount only if he offers it, approximate timing, reported source, and confidence (self-reported, documented, or verified). Do not copy customer names, phone numbers, email, address, license plates, VINs, card details, or screenshots of his private books.
5. Report results in clear categories: **distribution / interest / inquiry / booked / completed paid / documented amount**. Each stage is distinct. Client-reported revenue is not independently verified, and source-associated paid work is not proof of causality.
6. **Weekly internal review only**, no recurring form for Juan. Review source opportunities, funnel dropoffs and next useful action. Do not imply the live customer preview has active tracking; it currently does not.

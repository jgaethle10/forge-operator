# Commercial Studio | Five-$50-commercials-per-day distribution operating plan
Date: 2026-11-11  
Authority: Systemia-first, Evercraft-owned storefront and first-party intake. No ad spend authorized.

## Business goal and current truth

Aim to reach **five successfully paid, fulfillable $50 commercials per day** ($250 gross/day, 35 orders per week), not five page views or quotes. The goal is unproven. Processor fees, licensing, labor, partner payouts, taxes, refunds, and revisions reduce net proceeds. One buyer's $50 Cash App receipt is evidence of a payment, not evidence of accepted delivery, public showcase rights, or a repeatable acquisition system.

### Offer customers may actually purchase

Thirty-second social video, one concept, one agreed output format, one included revision, using client-authorized materials. No on-location filming, ad spend, confidential record intake through public comments, unlimited revisions, guaranteed turnaround, or novel paid licensing is included. A brief, rights, scope, delivery date, capacity and terms require written approval **before payment**. Use the actual Commercial Studio inquiry path; do not substitute unrelated Systemia/Stripe product links or market native Evercraft Payments as independently self-service and verified when it is not.

## Five channels, one original buyer promise

1. **Owned partner network**: `/partners/` now offers a specific Commercial Studio pilot for web designers, social-media managers and independent consultants. This is only a *founding interest program*, not a live reseller account or payout service. Invite aligned existing contacts who have opted in and discuss written per-sale terms only after margin, taxes, service capacity and legal issues are resolved. No referral money for recruiting others.
2. **Fiverr Video & Animation**: Draft a native gig for one 30-second social promotion based on client-provided materials, limited to one format and one revision. A provider account, native gig, availability and current net fees are **not** verified. Do not direct Fiverr customers off-platform if that violates provider rules.
3. **LinkedIn Services**: Publish a company or authorized provider service listing, once that account and authority are verified. Use a released playable original sample and the owned brief page. Avoid unpermitted bulk connection messages.
4. **Nextdoor business presence**: Yakima/Tri-Cities local small-business education or offer posting only through a verified business identity, respecting local rules and permissions. No implication of paid-placement, endorsement, or direct solicitation where prohibited.
5. **Alignable**: Build mutual agency/client-referral relationships on permitted business pages and partner groups. No mass DMs, pay-for-leads, fabricated customer reviews or purchased engagement.

Marketplace listing URLs and paid order receipts are **not created by this code PR**. Existing `/partners/` and `/commercial-studio/` are first-party source files whose actual public serving and live intake require independent release evidence.

## Funnel, attribution and economic proof

The public studio page recognizes only explicit `src` values: `partners`, `fiverr`, `linkedin`, `nextdoor`, `alignable`, `direct`, `other`. When deployed, it submits `claimedChannel` through existing consented intake. The **server** records only validated `claimed_discovery_channel`; unknown and missing values become `unattributed`. This is visitor-provided metadata, not authenticated referral identity or verified conversion. No client IP, raw referrer, cross-site tracking, tracking cookies, or hidden third-party script added. Live backend deployment is not evidenced by a code commit or green CI.

Record stages separately: provider-confirmed published listing URL; qualified inquiry/receipt; human-approved scope/rights/capacity; processor-confirmed paid transaction; delivered file and QC receipt; buyer acceptance; refund/revision; partner payout if a signed contract applies. Never sum self-reported source tags as paid commissions or new clients. Do not count a seller-created listing as income.

At a **hypothetical** 20% qualified-inquiry-to-paid rate, five paid orders per day would require 25 qualified inquiries a day, but the actual rate must be measured. Track provider reported listings and authorized traffic where the provider supports it, no invented impressions or clicks.

## Delivery throughput gate

A five-order day could mean five distinct creative briefs, asset-rights checks, renders, client previews, one revision each, and QA. Measure actual median total labor time and per-order contribution margin before advertising the capacity or turnaround. First independently deliver the existing paid customer's commercial and obtain separate permission before adding it to a public portfolio; do not publish the customer work by default.

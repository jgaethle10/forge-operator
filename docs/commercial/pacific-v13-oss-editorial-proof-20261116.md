# Pacific V13 | Open-source studio proof (private owner review)
Date: 2026-11-16. Not approved for client publication or broadcast.

## What actually ran
- FFmpeg 7.1.5 edited **900 frames at 30 fps**, 720×1280, with the user's actual Pacific footage and original still photos.
- Nine source shots + one 5-second contact card. Source reels were muted. Fixed crop excludes the observed Facebook top bar/footer/corner controls; sampled source QA checked, but full manual visual QA remains necessary.
- No automatic photo zoom. Continuous longer shots and purposeful editorial cuts replace some V12 transitions.
- Program audio reused from the **V12 private reference** with native Bella Voice 5 and HoliznaCC0 “Classic” recorded music. Music still requires artistic approval, and no separately generated upgraded soundtrack is claimed.
- OpenTimelineIO 0.18.1 was installed as a pinned native Python 3.13 wheel from GitHub Actions artifact #14227350194.
- A genuine 2-track, 11-clip native .otio timeline was exported **locally** after SHA-256 verification of all source media. Native library read-back verified frame alignment, clip order, trim positions and 900-frame track durations. Nothing from the customer's raw footage was uploaded to GitHub.
- A second portable editor project with 10 conformed picture clips, one mixed audio reference and relative paths was produced in a private ZIP. A compatible editor may need media relinking; the audio is not split into voice/music/SFX stems.
- Both full and smaller iPhone MP4 exports passed complete FFmpeg decode. Original V12 music remains provisional.

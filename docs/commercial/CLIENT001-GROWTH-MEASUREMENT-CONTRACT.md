## The central distinction

**Business growth != Evercraft attribution != Evercraft causal impact.** Track separately:

1. Business collected revenue: all completed paid jobs less refunds. Requires complete owner-reconciled week.
2. Business growth: change across matched complete periods (initially four full pre-campaign weeks vs four full weeks after). Baseline incomplete means "unknown", never zero.
3. Evercraft-sourced first-visit paid revenue: owner-verified completed jobs traceable to Evercraft code or link. Segregate documented vs owner-reported, and exclude returning visits from first-acquisition totals.
4. Causal lift: unproven without experiment/design controlling for seasonality, prices, capacity and competitors. Do not claim that a before/after jump was caused by Evercraft.
5. Direct contribution: collected revenue less recorded direct costs only. Not accounting net profit or owner take-home.

A clicked phone link is **click intent**, not a connected call. An email draft is **not an email sent**. A public post or an inquiry is **not** a completed paid service. The owner must confirm paid completion, gross received, refunds, new/returning status and source confidence. Use opaque evidence references, not PII.

A source may be marked "Documented" only when an identifiable code/link/referral trail is corroborated by Juan; customer self-report is "Reported". Both require the owner to mark the paid job reconciled; attribution is not a claim of incremental causality.

## Business growth dimensions that matter

**Demand**: number of inquiries per channel, share booked, actual paid completions, median inquiry-to-booking delay.

**Revenue**: net collected $, paid jobs, average collected ticket, new customer first paid visits, repeat paid services, refunds.

**Unit economics**: direct parts/labor/travel/disposal/processing costs by job, direct contribution per service, profitability guardrail before paying for advertising or commissions. Confirm if owner/operator labor already counted.

**Capacity**: actual available service slots, slots used, service-day locations, travel overhead; don't increase traffic when demand is already beyond capacity.

**Retention**: eligible service return visits by cohort after 60/90/180 days, only if Juan already has consent and a safe reminder workflow.

**Channel trust**: campaign/landing clicks may be measured via first-party consent-preserving analytics only after client approval. Do not use invasive pixels, identity tracking or location profiling.

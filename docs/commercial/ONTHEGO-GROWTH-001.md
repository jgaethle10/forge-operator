## Proof hierarchy for attributable revenue

1. **Public exposure**: actual published post or referral URL, observed independently.
2. **Interest**: opted-in click or inquiry; does not equal a paid customer.
3. **Booking**: Juan confirms a real scheduled service; still not revenue.
4. **Paid completed first service**: Juan confirms collection, amount and source. Deduplicate multiple sources; exclude returns, no-shows, cancellations, refunds and existing customer bookings unless separately agreed.
5. **Compensation**: only under a later written agreement defining attribution window, exclusions, amounts, margins, privacy, reconciliation and termination. Do not silently collect a commission.

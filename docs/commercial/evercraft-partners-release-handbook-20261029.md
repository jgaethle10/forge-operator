## Identity
Name: Evercraft Partners
Headline: Good work travels farther together.
Promise: Your connections can open doors. Evercraft builds tools and services that can help.
Values: legitimate customer value, low-friction onboarding, good judgment, honest earnings, trusted relationships.

## Design language
- Deep forest #101B18, electric lime #C7FF77, ivory #F6F6EE, white #FFFEFA.
- Original network mark with five nodes. Large editorial typography, calm white spaces, legible mobile-first layouts.
- One primary CTA: Apply to become a partner. Zero sales jargon on entryway.
- No external stock photography or imported logo; use original vector assets in public/partners.

## Buyer-facing product
- /partners/ is a public founding partner recruiting page, not the employee hiring page or affiliate payout dashboard.
- The applicant provides name, email, industry audience, region and optional website.
- The form PREPARES a mailto application; the sender must press Send. No application server, automatic CRM ingestion, tracking IDs, partner status or payout is implied.
- /partners/terms.html clearly explains no guarantees, fees, recruiting quotas, or authorized agency until separately approved in writing.

## Four distribution lanes
1. Referral contributors: niche creators, freelancers, professionals introducing permitted buyers.
2. Agency and consultant resellers: businesses adding appropriate Evercraft work to existing client service.
3. Industry specialists: editors, shops, and other professionals matching domain-specific offers.
4. Platform/embedded: software companies and permissioned marketplaces where verified integrations can route relevant buyers.

## Early matching
- Business consultants: Evercraft Boardroom, verified scoped human-reviewed decisions. Do not imply live quorum execution.
- Research agencies: Evercraft Research, human-reviewed scoped report requests. The public scope builder does not itself deliver reports.
- Post-production professionals: ForensiScope scoped review inquiry. Do not claim paid automated intake.
- FindMyPart and Work Saban can be added only after their specific truthful customer acceptance and delivery lanes are proven.

Week 1: verify public URL and intake, 30 relevant partner prospects identified with permitted inquiry routes, two or three fulfillable scoped offers.

Weeks 3-4: target 20 enrolled, 10 activated, 3 independently verified paid attributable end-customer orders only if attribution and signed terms are ready.

## Payout eligibility gate
Never publish an unapproved percentage or guarantee earnings. Review regional law, independent contractor/tax terms, disclosure, refunds, margin and anti-spam.
Payment for recruiting is prohibited in this model. Per-sale commission eligibility requires a written contract, consented attribution, a genuine verified customer payment, refund/fraud criteria, and payout receipt.

## Required real-world receipts
- CI passing is a source test; PR merge is a code release, not production deployment.
- Public HTTPS HTML and SVG/terms/LLM assets must be independently checked.
- A mailto draft is not a received applicant email.
- Approved applicant is not activated partner; a referral link is not a paid customer.
- Only processor-verified paid and fulfilled orders contribute to sale/commission metrics.
- A partner payout requires actual payable approval and actual payout receipt.

## Founder non-negotiables
No Base44, no bulk unsolicited spam, no RIVET/AliEV/EV assets or contacts subject to legal hold. Route through Systemia first, respect outbound contact gate and avoid duplicating existing distribution automation. Stay global, legally permissible and permissioned.

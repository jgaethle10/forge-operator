## Acquisition safeguards

The workflow attempts only no-login official DVIDS MP4 links listed in each site's download dialog, verifying MIME, video stream, codec, size, SHA-256, and original dates. **A 403, HTML response, unsupported stream, or missing file blocks production.** It does not bypass authentication, pay, use third-party hosting, touch private client footage, or publish.

Public CI artifacts contain only sanitized source-origin and technical test evidence. The exploratory low-resolution downloaded clips stay inside the ephemeral runner and are not posted or declared client-ready. They are deliberately *not* yet a polished 1080p reel.

- 00–06: observed wide/medium motion at the Sonoran border, caption **USACE footage | September 2019**, natural ambient audio where present

- 16–28: switch to March 2020 footage, clearly date-labeled, show real environmental action rather than text cards
- 28–40: June 2020 historical transition, authentic moving video, original score mixed beneath location sound
- 40–45: a single short editorial card differentiating archival construction from the later 2023 border story

This outline is **not** a statement that shots or masters exist. Do not use frame grabs from the photographer's Facebook post or invent field audio. Human review must inspect the actual frames for subjects, geography, date integrity, respectful portrayal, sound, and pacing. Rights, person/privacy protections, color/audio finish, iPhone playback, and editorial facts still require independent release gates.

If no archive media is accessible, record the blocker and return to the photographer's permissioned original footage, **not AI depictions disguised as real documentary images**.

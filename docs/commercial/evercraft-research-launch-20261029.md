## Real v1 customer journey
1. Customer enters one research question with optional decision context, geography, report type and up to six public source URLs.
2. Client-side validation builds a scope document, clearly labeled as a request, not research output.
3. Customer copies or downloads the scope locally without creating an account or uploading data.
4. Customer can choose to email a non-sensitive quote request. No background email is sent.
5. Research fulfillment is separately scoped: source rights, source method, exact price, expected deliverable, timing and secure transfer confirmed before any purchase.
6. Future Evercraft Payments order route must be exact-SKU, independently provider verified and idempotently receipt-backed. Not built in v1.
7. Authorized Systemia/Saban agents and research specialists investigate with source lineage, contradiction preservation and independent review. Not called by v1.
8. A reviewer signs off, with a secure delivery receipt and explicit follow-up route. Not automated in v1.

## Decision-grade report acceptance
Question, location and research-use context; executive conclusion or evidence insufficiency; source ledger with observation dates; claim-level observed/user-supplied/modeled/inferred/unknown labels; conflicting evidence; assumptions and uncertainty; recommendations and reversibility; human QA approval; report version; secure fulfillment receipt. Never invent sources, observations, model execution, payments or signoff. PDF is a future verified deliverable, not currently generated by the form.

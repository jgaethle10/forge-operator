## Actual weaknesses found by inspecting the V9B timeline
1. Repetitious paint/polish footage: multiple similar shots at approx 0, 3–5, 12, 15, 18, 21, 24 s. Remove duplicates and use distinct repair stages.
2. Rigid alternation between short motion and photo shots (most 1.2–1.7 s), producing an unmotivated slideshow rhythm even though it uses actual video. Retain selected stills as narrative punctuation, not every other cut.
3. Photo near 25.9 s repeats the grinder image from 1.8 s, creating a visual loop. Replace with distinct closing craft/result.
4. Service range isn't articulated through a connected mini-story. Begin with a problem/diagnosis, show precision/rebuilding/paint or polish, finish on a result.
5. End card starts near 26.58 s and holds almost eight seconds. Keep info legible, but improve transition, sequencing, and musical resolve so the ending feels earned rather than a static tail.
6. Bella's copy could become more specific to shop/mobile service and less slogan-heavy. Casting/timbre must stay original `af_bella`; don't disguise recasts as Bella.

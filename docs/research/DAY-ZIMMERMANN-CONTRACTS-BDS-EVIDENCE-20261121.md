## Chronology / reconciliations

- **1990 / 1996:** AFSC reports date-coded rounds recovered from incidents in Gaza to these manufacturing years at IAAP. This is not yet independent chain-of-custody proof.
- **1951–1998:** A U.S. Court of Federal Claims opinion records Mason & Hanger as IAAP operator through 1998, with American Ordnance operating afterward [14].
- **1999:** Day & Zimmermann's own history confirms acquisition of The Mason Company / Mason & Hanger, *after* the AFSC-reported years on the two munitions [11].
- **2007-02:** The FTC approved transfer of General Dynamics' remaining 50% of American Ordnance to Day & Zimmermann; the company's history independently says it acquired 100% ownership in 2007 [11,13].
- **2022-01:** SOC Jerusalem WPS III delivery order active in federal procurement system, number 19AQMM22F0090.
- **2023-12-08/09:** Emergency determination followed by DSCA **possible sale**, sourced from U.S. Army inventory, for an estimated $106.5M. The federal notice lists proposed funding as **Foreign Military Financing and national funds**, but no final allocation [12]. Never relabel as verified delivery or say the sale was entirely U.S.-taxpayer funded.
- **2024:** State Department OIG identifies a Jerusalem **TO-3** initial award amount of $202.48M; the current procurement mirror for **19AQMM22F0090** lists $206.25M potential value, $91.22M obligations and $25.55M outlays, with August 2026 latest modification. Treat as **distinct source records pending contract-identifier reconciliation**. These are also different financial measures; their difference is not proof of diversion or fraud.
- **2026-10-13:** Campus Map survey records 37 anti-BDS measures under its stated count rule.

## Counterquestions and open records needed

1. **Munitions provenance:** Obtain independently authenticated lot and production-source documents, actual chain-of-custody records, photographs, and dates. Is the manufacturer identifiable for rounds allegedly used in a specific incident?
2. **Sale realization and funding:** Seek signed FMS agreement, actual delivery, acceptance and transaction records behind DSCA 24-15. The federal notice states **Foreign Military Financing and national funds** but gives no final split, so neither delivery nor funding breakdown is proven.
3. **Contracting money:** Obtain current USAspending transaction-level dataset for PIID 19AQMM22F0090 and reconcile fiscal-year obligations versus outlays and potential options. Seek crosswalk between the OIG's **TO-3** and the procurement PIID before asserting they are the identical assignment.
4. **Contract mission:** Obtain public Statement of Work / contract modifications where released, distinguishing protection of American personnel/property from any separate operations.
5. **Company response:** Before public accusations, request dated comment from SOC / Day & Zimmermann through authorized, verified institutional contact. **No contact sent or authorized in this record.**
6. **Legal scope:** Independently assess 2026 state statutes / governors' orders and case outcomes. Avoid treating a count from a third-party index as a comprehensive legal conclusion.
7. **Potential corrections:** If any claimed munition link rests only on source interpretation, label as an allegation, publish underlying uncertainty, and correct promptly on stronger evidence.

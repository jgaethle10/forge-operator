## Chronology / reconciliations

- **1990 / 1996:** AFSC reports date-coded rounds recovered from incidents in Gaza to these manufacturing years at IAAP. This is not yet independent chain-of-custody proof.
- **1999:** AFSC places Day & Zimmermann's Mason & Hanger acquisition after the years associated with those reported rounds.
- **2022-01:** SOC Jerusalem WPS III delivery order active in federal procurement system, number 19AQMM22F0090.
- **2023-12-09:** DSCA **possible sale**, sourced from U.S. Army inventory, for an estimated $106.5M. Never relabel as verified delivery.
- **2024:** State Department OIG identifies original award amount of $202.48M. Current procurement mirror lists $206.25M potential value, $91.22M obligations and $25.55M outlays. These measure different things; do not describe their difference as proof of diversion or fraud.
- **2026-10-13:** Campus Map survey records 37 anti-BDS measures under its stated count rule.

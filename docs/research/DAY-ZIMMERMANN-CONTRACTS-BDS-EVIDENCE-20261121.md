## Chronology / reconciliations

- **1990 / 1996:** AFSC reports date-coded rounds recovered from incidents in Gaza to these manufacturing years at IAAP. This is not yet independent chain-of-custody proof.
- **1951–1998:** A U.S. Court of Federal Claims opinion records Mason & Hanger as IAAP operator through 1998, with American Ordnance operating afterward [14].
- **1992:** A U.S. court ruling described Alliant as systems contractor for advanced chemical-energy tank ammunition [22].
- **1998:** The U.S. Army's *ARMOR* magazine listed Iowa AAP's **load/assemble/packout** role separately from Alliant/Primex systems contractors, D&Z Camden primers, and numerous component suppliers [20].
- **1999:** Day & Zimmermann's own history confirms acquisition of The Mason Company / Mason & Hanger, *after* the AFSC-reported years on the two munitions [11].
- **2001:** Alliant represented in an SEC filing that it was the **only producer of M830A1** at that time [15]. This does not exclude subcontracts or Iowa plant assembly work.
- **2007-02:** The FTC approved transfer of General Dynamics' remaining 50% of American Ordnance to Day & Zimmermann; the company's history independently says it acquired 100% ownership in 2007 [11,13].
- **2022-01:** SOC Jerusalem WPS III delivery order active in federal procurement system, number 19AQMM22F0090.
- **2023-12-08/09:** Emergency determination followed by DSCA **possible sale**, sourced from U.S. Army inventory, for an estimated $106.5M. The federal notice lists proposed funding as **Foreign Military Financing and national funds**, but no final allocation [12]. Never relabel as verified delivery or say the sale was entirely U.S.-taxpayer funded.
- **2024:** State Department OIG identifies a Jerusalem **TO-3** initial award amount of $202.48M; the current procurement mirror for **19AQMM22F0090** lists $206.25M potential value, $91.22M obligations and $25.55M outlays, with August 2026 latest modification. Treat as **distinct source records pending contract-identifier reconciliation**. These are also different financial measures; their difference is not proof of diversion or fraud.
- **2024-01-29:** Hind Rajab and her family were killed, with later discovery of a marked 1996 U.S.-made tank-ammunition fragment in the area. The April 2024 Washington Post investigation could not determine its exact connection to the ambulance strike or conclusively identify the causing round [17]. AFSC's Jan. **19** date conflicts with the reporting [6].
- **2024-08-13:** Separate DSCA possible-sale notice of up to 32,739 M1147 and/or M830A1 rounds and supporting articles valued at an estimated $774.1M, with 2027 estimated deliveries. Principal contractors named **General Dynamics and Northrop Grumman**, not Day & Zimmermann [18].
- **2026-08-19:** Reuters and AP reported Israel's military announcing a criminal investigation and acknowledging that its troops had fired on the family car, an important later development but not proof of specific ammunition provenance or settled criminal liability [19,21].
- **2026-10-13:** Campus Map survey records 37 anti-BDS measures under its stated count rule.

## Counterquestions and open records needed

1. **Munitions provenance:** Obtain independently authenticated lot and production-source documents, actual chain-of-custody records, photographs, and dates. Distinguish **systems producer**, component supplier, plant owner, prime contractor and LAP contractor. Do individual markings identify the assembler, initial producer, or both? Can a physical lot be linked to a particular 2024 incident? Specifically seek complete images/codes of the **1990** alleged M830A1 fragment and reconcile its apparent pre-1993 date against Army model history and potential component-level lot marks [23].
2. **Sale realization and funding:** Seek signed FMS agreements, delivery, acceptance and transaction records behind **separate** DSCA cases **24-15 (Dec 2023)** and **24-10 (Aug 2024)**. The 2023 notice states **Foreign Military Financing and national funds** without final split; 2024 notice estimates deliveries in 2027. Neither proves a particular shipment or product lot arrived in Israel.
3. **Contracting money:** Obtain current USAspending transaction-level dataset for PIID 19AQMM22F0090 and reconcile fiscal-year obligations versus outlays and potential options. Seek crosswalk between the OIG's **TO-3** and the procurement PIID before asserting they are the identical assignment.
4. **Contract mission:** Obtain public Statement of Work / contract modifications where released, distinguishing protection of American personnel/property from any separate operations.
5. **Company response:** Before public accusations, request dated comment from SOC / Day & Zimmermann through authorized, verified institutional contact. **No contact sent or authorized in this record.**
6. **Legal scope:** Independently assess 2026 state statutes / governors' orders and case outcomes. Avoid treating a count from a third-party index as a comprehensive legal conclusion.
7. **Potential corrections:** For Hind Rajab, use **January 29, 2024** (AFSC says Jan 19). State that Washington Post could not directly link recovered 1996 fragment to ambulance strike. Maintain separate evidence tracks for **who fired at the vehicle** (a 2026 military admission reported by Reuters/AP), **precise weapon or round** (unresolved), and **manufacturer and shipment** (unresolved).

## Editorial safeguards

- Do **not** frame the U.S. Jerusalem security contract as evidence of Israeli army financing or an illegal payment.
- Do **not** imply that either 2023 or 2024 proposed sale was delivered, financed entirely from U.S. taxpayer funds, or manufactured by a named firm, or that Day & Zimmermann made the referenced 1990/1996 recovered rounds.
- Do **not** imply that recovered nearby ammunition fragment conclusively caused the Hind Rajab ambulance strike or that a military announcement of an investigation establishes criminal responsibility.
- Do **not** mistake **Alliant as historical systems producer** for proof that IAAP did not load/assemble/pack M830A1. Both roles can coexist.
- Do **not** present human-rights violations, intent, or criminal liability as established by the procurement figures alone.
- Distinguish supporters' public-investment/government-procurement arguments from critics' free-expression and chilling-effect arguments.
- Never represent this Markdown file, GitHub commit, draft PR or web citations as live signed evidence intake or Harbor/Journal publication.

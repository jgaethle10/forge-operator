# Adult publisher lane: independent compliance preflight

This is an **unlaunched business research lane**, separate from the family-facing Evercraft Audience Sites. No adult site, explicit content, revenue-share contract, affiliate promotion, age-gated checkout, or ads have been published as part of this release.

Before an adult-content property may be considered:
1. Determine jurisdictions and age-verification requirements for visitors, depicted adults, and the operator. Do not publish content or collect payment until obligations are reviewed.
2. Establish rights, explicit informed consent, original provenance and removal/complaints processes for every person depicted. No minors or ambiguous age, and no impersonation or nonconsensual AI sexual depictions.
3. Obtain written permission to host, publish, distribute and monetize adult material under the policies of the actual host, advertiser and payment processor.
4. Keep branding, customer support, analytics, authentication and domain infrastructure separate from the existing Evercraft family-facing businesses.
5. Use provider-verified income and payouts only. Do not buy or simulate traffic, fabricate clicks, mislabel advertisements, or route visitors through deceptive doorway networks.
6. Have operator approval for material publication and any purchases, contracts or financial obligations.

Commercial gate: all red until provider rules and operator decisions are documented. Never treat a static landing page, simulation, or repository change as proof of an operating adult website.

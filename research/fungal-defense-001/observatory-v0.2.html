Documented

Fungal infections and resistance

Some fungal pathogens cause severe illness and can be difficult to treat. Candida auris can spread between vulnerable patients in healthcare settings.

Being investigated

Climate and adaptation

Scientists are studying whether environmental warming contributes to the emergence of heat-tolerant pathogenic fungi. Causality and future risk are not fully established.

Actionable

Infection prevention

Early detection, screening where indicated, appropriate hygiene and effective healthcare environmental cleaning are established prevention measures.

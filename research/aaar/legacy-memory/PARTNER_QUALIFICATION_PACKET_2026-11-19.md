## What counts as a credible supplier
A product web listing does **not** establish an OEM manufacturing chain, an authorized channel, usable stock or F-22 compatibility. For a prospective civilian industrial-memory partner, request written evidence of:
- OEM/OCM identity and authorized reseller/integrator terms (or own manufactured product identity), with rights to supply and keep traceable part identity.
- Exact product, revision, firmware, interface behavior, form factor, capacity, origin and serial/lot/date coding (where applicable).
- Unbroken manufacturer-to-inventory provenance, counterfeit avoidance and documented receiving inspection.
- Written current quote, lead time, quantity minimums, warranty, returns and any integration/test labor charges.
- Product change notifications, lifecycle/end-of-life plan, last-time-buy lead time, long-term supply commitments and permitted branding.
- Appropriate liability/insurance, information-security, export-control and quality expectations (subject to contracting counsel).

## Commercial math (no fictional margins)
To evaluate *any* supply contract, obtain actual vendor quotes and customer-specified obligations first:

```
landed_deliverable_cost =
  written_OEM_or_authorized_channel_unit_cost
  + per-unit_freight_and_tax
  + documented_receiving_QA_cost
  + traceability_recordkeeping_and_compliance_cost
  + anticipated_warranty_returns_and_liability_reserve
  + packaging_and_customer_delivery_cost
```

Then confirm quantity and payment terms; calculate actual gross margin only from a documented customer quote and vendor costs. $105.44 and $114.20 are **public listing benchmarks**, not verified supply costs or offers from Evercraft.

## Proposed 5-step partner qualification process
1. **Source truth:** independent review of original official notice attachment; validate public product information, exact parts, and EOL risks. The government source-sought notice is not an order.
2. **Corporate readiness:** independently verify Evercraft's current company registration/eligibility and identify a knowledgeable quality/contract reviewer.
3. **Approval / dedup:** check company CRM and existing partner contacts to avoid duplicate messages; founder and Systemia authorize any individual external outreach. No outreach has been sent.
4. **Written supplier evidence:** secure genuine OEM/distributor authorization, complete chain of custody, test/QA arrangement and commercial quote. Record source, date, approver and restrictions in private evidence ledger; redact sensitive terms from public records.
5. **Go/no-go:** use validated ability to supply and customer demand to determine civilian industrial-memory sales; any government sources-sought response needs separate specialized human review and express approval.

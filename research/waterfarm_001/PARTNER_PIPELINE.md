## Qualification gates
1. A *real* site use case with baseline water demand and local resource constraints, not a broad market slogan.
2. Willingness to share site metadata/measurement requirements under appropriate written consent; no personal or site-sensitive detail in the public repo.
3. Named accountable engineering and scientific reviewers.
4. Regulatory review: water rights, collection restrictions, quality rules, end use, overflow/disposal and occupational safety.
5. IP and data terms negotiated before any confidential design exchange, access or joint development.
6. Bounded experiment SOW, clear exit/stop criteria, responsible party for direct costs and insurance, and no assumptions about available grants.
7. Written owner approval before outreach or spend.

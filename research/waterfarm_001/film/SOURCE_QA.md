# Evercraft Aqua source and image integrity rules

The approved film is intended to use **real photographed motion footage**. No AI-generated stills or video synthesized as simulated footage. The first research previsualization is intentionally **not** that live-action master.

## Source rights
Publisher reference: https://mixkit.co/license/modal/videoFree/ (commercial permitted for *Free* license clips, but restricted clips are personal-use-only). Each item page must be verified individually before use. NOAA/USDA government-origin items may be public domain, but check that footage was created within official duties and has no third-party overlays. Attribution and share-alike rules are item-specific; do not confuse Wikimedia upload with blanket permission.

## Footage intake schema
For each accepted asset: stable clip ID; authored title; canonical page; license class; author; source media URL; downloaded bytes SHA256; time downloaded; source bitrate and dimensions from ffprobe; footage real-camera vs AI-generated/CGI/animation; releases/depicted people rights; edits used; geographic representation; reviewer's approval; restrictions. If any mandatory item is unknown, mark HOLD; don't interpret missing as pass.

## Scientific communication
No generic “water is infinite” claim: Earth recycles water, but accessible/local freshwater, water quality, timing and energy remain limiting constraints. “WATER IS ALWAYS MOVING” poetic film line, not an argument that drought cannot occur. Final documentary geography must identify non-Yakima stock when it would otherwise look like a Yakima production site.

## Original score
The previsualization uses a deterministic original synthesized ambient instrumental with no licensed third-party track. It is **not a finished recorded orchestra, human performance or mastered commercial audio**. Review on phone and speaker systems; re-record/mix as needed.

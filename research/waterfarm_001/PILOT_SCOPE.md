# WATERFARM-001 pilot scope and partner questions (v0.1)
**Nonpotable, research-only proposal. No physical installation authorized.**

## Research questions to close before equipment selection
1. What water-demand profile and growing conditions are involved (crop, substrate, season, historic water source)?
2. Can rainfall be collected legally from an *existing-purpose* roof on the same property? If not, exclude that path.
3. Is condensate recovery genuinely useful compared with passive humidity management, straightforward irrigation upgrades and compliant storage?
4. For greenhouse recapture, what is the physically appropriate control condition, randomization/matched temperature and humidity, sensor calibration schedule and test length?
5. Can condensate chemistry be assessed by an appropriately accredited laboratory? Which end-use-specific regulatory criteria apply?
6. What are the life-cycle cost, maintenance, capex, incremental kWh, seasonal water yield and avoided withdrawals under low/normal/high cases?
7. Will the selected system shift a water burden elsewhere (cooling heat, condensate discharge, electrical load, ecological impact)?
8. Who holds field injury and equipment liability, water rights, data publication rights, and responsibility for cleaning/disposal?
9. What is the approved stop protocol for any contamination, sensor failure, abnormal pressure, leak or unsafe operation?
10. What are the required drawings and permits before field deployment, and who signs them?

## Measurable pre-registered acceptance
- Identify one host and a verified baseline **before** equipment claims.
- Record UTC timestamps, sensor ID, units, method, calibration, uncertainty and meter positions for every measurement.
- Water ledger must avoid double counting collected rain, internally recycled condensate and external freshwater savings.
- Track kWh/L *including ancillary loads* in full economics report; report CAPEX/OPEX and consumables separately.
- Water-quality HOLD until independently tested and site end-use approved.
- 14-day matched trial is only a **provisional minimum**, not proof of seasonal economics. Research advisers should specify appropriate duration.
- On missing sensor reading, flow verification, authorization or chemical result, mark UNKNOWN/HOLD, never ZERO/PASS.
- Pre-register go/no-go economic criterion before measuring. Conservative baseline comparator is the cheapest compliant incumbent technology; not theoretical unlimited freshwater.
- Only after lab review and permitted design, authorize controlled nonpotable use; no drinking-water claim.
- Publication contains raw data provenance, uncertainty, negative findings and explicit limits. No ROI or yield guarantees.

## Responsibility proposal (unnegotiated)
- Evercraft: data collection software, baseline model, dashboard, integration/QA and commercialization study.
- Scientist: design, sampling, analysis and independent review.
- Site host: documented physical access and operating constraints, explicit consent.
- Qualified engineer/fabricator: safe design and construction, permits and commissioning.
- Accredited lab: contaminant panel and report.
- Owner: approve all contracting/outreach/expenditure/publishing.

## First-year commercialization logic
Do not promise hardware first. Explore paid feasibility assessment, instrumentation integration or reporting *only if* demand and competence are verified. Partner-produced installation/treatment stays with licensed entities where required. Later license Evercraft measurement/orchestration software only after customer acceptance testing and agreed data rights.

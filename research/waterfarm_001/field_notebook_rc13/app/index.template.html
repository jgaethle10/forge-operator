Every drop deserves a better record.

Record one reading from an existing water meter each day. After 15 readings, get a traceable 14-day withdrawal baseline. No account, cloud upload or equipment connection.

01 / Your notebook

Start with one meter.

Use a short label for an existing water meter. Never enter a site address or personal details in the label.

Your reading log

Read the same meter at approximately the same time each day. A missed day or a reset requires review rather than an invented reading.

0 / 15 readings
For the first reading, leave energy blank. Unknown energy is never treated as zero.
Your data, your copy

Export a CSV backup after each reading. Browser storage is not encrypted, may be cleared, and may not persist reliably when an HTML file is opened from Files on an iPhone.

Measure first.
Engineer second.

A small, honest baseline can help us understand withdrawals before deciding whether scheduling, repairs, sensors or recovery hardware deserve attention.

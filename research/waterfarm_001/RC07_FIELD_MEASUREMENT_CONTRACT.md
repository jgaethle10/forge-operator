# Evercraft Aqua | Minimum field-water intelligence contract, RC07 draft

**Not a construction scope, purchase approval, water reuse license or signed agreement.** Designed for initial written research feedback when an authorized host and independent reviewer exist.

## Information needed from a possible operator
- Farm/greenhouse category, approximate area and crop stage (no private site address on public GitHub).
- Actual water source, water right/collection constraints as determined by qualified personnel; intended use and discharge path.
- Flow meter registers: inlet, delivery, drain/return with calibration certificate, meter ID and uncertainty; daily UTC timestamps and units.
- Irrigation operating state and schedule; irrigated area, crop coefficient provenance, reference ET readings, rain/soil moisture where appropriate.
- Whole-system electrical kWh (pump and any humidity equipment), intervals aligned with water measurements.
- Capex and maintenance quotes, marginal avoidable water price, whether water charges are volumetric.
- Data rights, consent and secure storage requirements; no named farm/device serial/location in public repo.

## Pilot inclusion gates
1. Host consent, a specific question and a measurable baseline; no presumed partner.
2. Documented sensor calibration, gaps/flags and uncertainty budget. Missing cannot be zero.
3. One primary outcome: external freshwater withdrawals per productive crop/area under comparable conditions; other outcomes are secondary.
4. Pre-register counterfactual, irrigation optimization comparison, source/quality/permit review, and energy accounting.
5. Require independent agronomy/engineering reviewer for controlled trial and crop-water safety.
6. Stop for unsafe cross-connections, questionable quality, unexplained sensor drift or insufficient matching periods. No automated actuation in this phase.
7. Never declare recovered condensate equal to measured withdrawal savings. No P&L or ROI claim without actual customer cost data.

## Exit criteria
Research output should show auditable source data, modeled vs measured labels, error/uncertainty, least-cost incumbent alternative, and why a proposed physical prototype is or is not warranted. Negative results are a valid outcome.

**Status:** Internal draft for owner approval. This message has **not** been sent.
**To (official study team):** LSRDTranspoStudy@wsdot.wa.gov
**Subject:** Request for Oct. 2026 Lower Snake River transportation study scenario tables

Hello WSDOT Lower Snake River Dams Transportation Study team,

We are reviewing public reporting about the October 6–8, 2026 open houses and would appreciate the source materials or public-record links needed to accurately present the modeling.

Could you share the October presentation slides, display boards and technical tables for scenarios 3–8, including the dates of the model runs and definitions for each scenario? In particular, could you clarify:

1. The modeled year and whether the $256.8 million annual shipping estimate is the 2045 with-dams reference, not current-year transportation spending.
2. The original annual shipping-cost, transport-emissions, and transportation capital-cost values and scenario descriptions, with units, price year, confidence bounds and assumed commodity volumes.
3. Whether reported freight greenhouse-gas figures are short tons or metric tons, and carbon dioxide alone or CO2-equivalent greenhouse gases.
4. Whether the $3.8–5.1 billion reported for Scenario 4 is capital, cumulative roadway maintenance through 2045, or a combined number.
5. Whether the $1.7–2.2 billion in common repair-in-kind costs is included within each scenario's stated capital range or additional to those ranges.
6. Whether the upper capital bound for Scenario 5 is $5.03 billion or $5.3 billion.
7. Any published commodity/rate competition assumptions, maps, GIS data, and reuse terms for accurately attributing or displaying project graphics.

We would welcome any link to an existing public archive; we do not need custom analysis if the documentation already exists.

Thank you for your work and for helping keep the public discussion accurate.

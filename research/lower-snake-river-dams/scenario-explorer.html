What the public debate tends to collapse

Annual freight expense is not annual highway maintenance. The modeled $310–323M rail scenarios represent total annual shipping costs; the increase above the 2045 with-dams reference is roughly $54–66M per year.

A transportation-only emissions reduction is not a net-system CO₂ reduction. Replacing dam electricity, grid services and construction emissions requires separate accounting. Some rail scenarios emit less transportation CO₂ than the with-dams reference.

Reported infrastructure figures contain unresolved differences. Scenario 5 is $4–5.03B in one local account and $4–5.3B in another. The description of Scenario 4's $3.8–5.1B also differs between outlets. A common $1.7–2.2B repair-in-kind estimate must not be double counted until its inclusion in scenario totals is established.

Salmon, treaty rights, irrigation and power have their own evidence. They belong in one transparent assessment, not as unsupported add-ons to a freight model. No scenario here is a recommendation for dam breach or retention.

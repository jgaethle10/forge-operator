# Evercraft Commercial Launch Line

Date: 2026-10-06

This directory packages the Evercraft portfolio for commercial deployment without pretending every experimental capability is production-ready.

## Market edge

Evercraft's strongest differentiation is not "we also have AI." It is the ability to carry a job through a governed chain:

`discover -> evidence -> scope -> approve -> execute -> meter/pay -> receipt -> verify -> learn`

The shared stack combines capability discovery, explicit human gates, provenance, execution receipts, replay, payment routing, reusable specialist capabilities, and vertical products. Most competitors specialize in one layer. Evercraft's commercial advantage appears when a buyer needs the layers connected and auditable.

## Launch doctrine

1. Sell outcomes, not the whole Evercraft universe.
2. Lead with narrow products that solve expensive pain now.
3. Keep observed, public, licensed, inferred, modeled, and completed states distinct.
4. A passing build is not deployment proof. A checkout is not payment proof. A handoff is not fulfillment proof.
5. Use EverScript, Systemia, Saban, Yard, CHUM, Evercraft Payments, and the capability registry as infrastructure underneath the buyer-facing offer.
6. Every launch product must have a buyer, painful trigger, bounded offer, price, proof artifact, human-readable door, machine-readable door, payment path, fulfillment path, support boundary, and verification receipt.

## First commercial line

### 1. ForensiScope
Buyer-facing promise: turn long or oversized authorized media into searchable, timestamped, reviewable evidence without pretending inference is observation.

Initial offer:
- Paid beta: $15 for <=5 minutes of media.
- Paid beta: $35 for <=30 minutes of media.
- Larger jobs: scoped quote until runtime and cost telemetry support automatic pricing.

Primary buyers: law firms, investigators, claims teams, security operators, journalists, compliance teams, and businesses with long recordings.

Why it can win: the product is not merely transcription. It is evidence-oriented media continuation with timeline construction, duplicate/repeated-segment review, provenance, limitations, adversarial second pass, and reviewable receipts.

Current commercialization blocker: the owned public runtime route is pending. Do not advertise an MCP/runtime as live until the external route and authority canary pass.

### 2. Evercraft IBM i Rescue
Buyer-facing promise: understand an IBM i / AS/400 estate before an upgrade, migration, modernization, or vendor engagement creates an expensive surprise.

Existing offers:
- $250 Estate X-Ray.
- $1,500 Proof Sprint.

Primary buyers: mid-market operators, manufacturers, distributors, finance/insurance businesses, MSPs, and modernization consultancies that need discovery evidence before a larger engagement.

Why it can win: proof-before-pitch. Reconstruct dependencies, RPG/CL/COBOL/DB2 relationships, jobs, integrations, vendor software, test surface, rollback requirements, and unknowns before recommending a migration.

Current commercialization blocker: Yard runtime is locally proven but public HTTPS route remains unbound.

### 3. Evercraft Foundry App Escape
Buyer-facing promise: show founders exactly what breaks, stays portable, must be rebuilt, and must be migrated before leaving an AI app-builder or proprietary runtime.

Proposed entry offer:
- $299 Portability X-Ray.
- Larger reconstruction/migration work quoted only after the audit.

Primary buyers: founders and small businesses with real users/data inside builder-tied applications.

Why it can win: this is an evidence product, not another app builder. It inventories source, auth, database, functions, files, integrations, deployment dependencies, vendor-only APIs, secrets, migration order, rollback plan, and proof requirements.

Current commercialization blocker: Yard audit runtime is locally proven but public HTTPS route remains unbound.

### 4. EarthWorks Site Autonomy Study
Buyer-facing promise: find where autonomy actually pays on a real worksite before a customer buys autonomous iron.

Existing design-partner offer:
- First 3 design partners: $5,000 Site Autonomy Study.
- Planned standard price: $7,500.

Deliverable: map one material loop, construct a site twin, baseline cycle performance, simulate dispatch and failure modes, identify bottlenecks, rank automation opportunities, and define a shadow-mode pilot without controlling production equipment.

Primary buyers: contractors, quarries, aggregate operators, excavation fleets, equipment dealers, autonomy vendors, and fleet managers.

Why it can win: vendor-neutral site orchestration and economic proof rather than a single OEM machine sale.

## Platform products

### EverScript
EverScript is a capability-native mission language and agent transaction protocol. It already supports signed manifests, version/action validation, quote-before-execute, budgets, approvals, metering interfaces, deterministic replay, chained receipts, discovery, HTTP compile/run, and MCP-compatible metadata.

Do not market EverScript as "our JavaScript." Position it as a governed execution contract for humans and agents.

Before a broad hosted launch, close the production work already documented in `everscript/README.md`: schema contracts, persistent registry/key rotation, production auth scopes, Evercraft Payments adapter, Yard quotas/isolation, full MCP invocation, signed external audit receipts, and portable sandboxing.

## RIVET + AliEV commercial architecture

RIVET is the **reporting brand**. Keep its buyer flow brutally simple: address in -> decision-ready report out.

AliEV is the Evercraft-owned **EV intelligence and operating layer** underneath RIVET. AliEV may become arbitrarily deep across competition, traffic, utility service area, tariffs, incentives, parcel/planning, EV stock, dwell/freight context, economics, provenance and observed session evidence without exposing that complexity in the RIVET buyer experience.

RIVET competes by making a better report, not a busier dashboard. The strongest differentiator is session intelligence with evidence integrity: verified site-resolved observed sessions where legitimately available, plus freshness, period, energy, usage patterns, reliability and provenance as coverage grows. Missing stays missing; inventory never masquerades as utilization; modeled demand never masquerades as observed usage.

The target experience remains:
`address -> RIVET report`

The target internal path is:
`RIVET report request -> AliEV evidence assembly -> report projection -> verification -> durable report/receipt`

Current owned public Fabric paths staged in this launch branch:
- `/rivet` for the reporting brand.
- `/aliev` for the intelligence layer.

Do not use Base44 for either public frontage.

## Definition of commercially deployed

A product is "commercially deployed" only when:
- a stranger can understand the offer,
- a qualified buyer can reach it,
- the runtime or human fulfillment path is live,
- price/quote rules are explicit,
- payment is independently verified,
- fulfillment is independently verified,
- support and safety boundaries are documented,
- and the buyer receives a durable result or receipt.

# RIVET Change Control

Status: **LOCKED**

RIVET is a partner-approved product and brand. Its approved brand kit and existing product behavior are the source of truth.

No agent, automation, refactor, commercialization sweep, design pass, data expansion, copy change, pricing change, report-schema change, public-route change, generated-artifact refresh, or visual change may modify RIVET without explicit approval from an authorized RIVET owner/partner.

For Evercraft automation, Jesse must explicitly approve the RIVET change before implementation. General instructions such as "push," "improve the stack," "commercialize," "make EV better," or "upgrade AliEV" are **not** RIVET approval.

Allowed without RIVET approval:
- read-only inspection;
- competitive research that does not mutate RIVET;
- AliEV work that does not change RIVET behavior, presentation, contracts, generated outputs, or public routes;
- unrelated Evercraft infrastructure work.

If RIVET approval is granted, the change must:
1. identify the approved scope;
2. compare the change against the partner-approved brand kit;
3. preserve a before/after receipt;
4. update the locked baseline only as part of that explicitly approved change;
5. keep unrelated stack changes separate.

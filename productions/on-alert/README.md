# ON ALERT | Life Beneath the Nuclear Age

**Status: preproduction.** V3 animated slide look is rejected. V4 establishes a photographic concept-look only, not a finished film or verified period footage.

## Story
A typical Cold War missile alert begins with an ordinary drive from Ellsworth Air Force Base across South Dakota. Some crews stopped at Wall Drug for breakfast before reporting to Launch Control Facility Delta-01. Two people then spent their alert shift underground, monitoring ten Minuteman missiles, conducting procedure checks, eating meals, and sleeping in shifts. The documentary centers the everyday human experience rather than sensational launch montages.

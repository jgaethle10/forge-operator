## Executive determination

Evercraft **already has far more than a motion-graphics template system**. The previous Owner Studio RC01/RC02 Python videos bypassed the canonical creative system and did not establish actual best-possible Evercraft output. **Keep RC01/RC02 INTERNAL ONLY.** Their 720px short edge would fail Fallen's default Master QC requirement of a 1080px short edge, and their synthetic fixtures/text-heavy beats do not meet the intended narrative production-grade acceptance without separate justified evidence and review.

The obstacle is not that a new video compositor must be built. It's **connection, real-media execution, verified rights, quality arbitration, independent approval, and making the strongest branches usable together**.

## Confidence and limitations

This audit confirms **live** public Visual Desk and Fabric capability metadata, **source-implemented** orchestration/film tooling, and **GitHub CI-generated artifacts** for several GPU/voice canaries. It does **not** independently certify production-ready GPU acceleration on the actual HP host, deployed private render service, present artistically approved 1080p masters, third-party visual-model API access, a licensed voice cast, final audio rights, or real social publication. GitHub draft PRs are not merged code; CI tests may prove only fixture/scoped execution, not operational integration.

**Verdict:** current owner's engineering ceiling is demonstrably **far higher** than the text-driven RC01/RC02 demo outputs, while true end-to-end cinema-grade creative production is **not yet independently verified**. The correct next move is integration and a governed premium film canary, not another one-off HTML/Python ad template.

# Evercraft Owner Studio | Production Standard RC01

**Status:** Source-only creative campaign factory. No automatic publishing, charge, buyer contact, agency claims or permissions. Evercraft LLC owns the original authored strategy, copy and artwork unless a separate license/third-party asset says otherwise.

## Inventory and boundaries

The source-backed [campaign-blueprints.v1.json](./campaign-blueprints.v1.json) provides product-specific buyer pain, 20-second storyboard, website copy, social draft, honest CTA and evidence gate for **all 53 current public Evercraft Fabric capabilities**, plus seven independently verified Systemia exact-price service campaigns. They are **script and marketing briefs, not 60 finished ads.** Catalog labels are not a substitute for present live checkout proof. New products enter only through source reconciliation. Never silently drop a portfolio member.

The seven existing provider-backed Systemia payment links are read from `public/mesh/checkout-ready/verified-links.json`. A browser checkout proves neither receipt nor fulfillment. Product price and brand copy must be rechecked before every publish. Public Fabric offer checkout remains held until the owned payment endpoints are configured and verified; the hosted `/offers` page should not be marketed as an automatic charge rail while held.

RIVET's legal and ownership governance pause means **no contact or promotion** regardless of its stale public catalog label. Partner and regulated industries, child/family products, security and finance products require their own rights and claims review. The 53 capability drafts should link to discovery, not “Buy now,” except for a verified exactly matched human offer with owner-authorized capacity.

## Creative quality system

| Gate | Required evidence |
| --- | --- |
| A real human problem | A recognizable situation in the first 3 seconds without manufactured statistics |
| Truthful mechanism | Screen capture from a permissioned version, or clearly labeled conceptual UI |
| Emotion and dialogue | Performance directions: genuine stresses, pauses, context; no identical sentence-fall cadence |
| Filmcraft | Actual product footage, shot continuity, legibility, design hierarchy, scene timing, no generic template slideshow |
| Sound | Evercraft-composed or provably licensed music and voice rights; mobile mix, captions, correct pronunciation |
| Formats | Native 16:9 hero, 9:16 social, 1:1 key art, 30s/15s/6s cutdowns, caption/subtitle source |
| Conversion | One correct call to action: learn / discuss scope / verified checkout, never a fake paid status |
| Evidence | Tested link, approved current price, buyer scope, operational fulfillment owner and capacity |
| Release | Founder signoff, asset rights record, consent, platform specs, independent public parity proof |
| Feedback | Watchability, comprehension, qualified inquiry, authorized conversion, actually verified settled revenue |

## Portfolio release sequence

1. Freeze product name, buyer segment, truthful authority, price, delivery, scope and source.
2. Draft voice/music/storyboard as separate artifacts; verify unique emotional angle.
3. Capture product interaction legally, with minimized or synthetic-labeled data, and establish source lineage.
4. Render 3 aspect ratio compositions and check on a phone, muted, with screen reader/captions.
5. Confirm buyer and transaction authority, service capacity and delivery commitments.
6. Publish through the appropriate **Evercraft-owned** channel only after founder signoff. Track publication URL, source commit and outcome with receipts.

**Commercial principle:** beautiful work that doesn't mislead; remarkable visuals that illuminate the value, not hide an unfinished product.

# Systemia Remote Ops

**Systemia Remote Ops** is Evercraft's business flight simulator and decision laboratory. It is designed for founders, operators, buyers, sellers and teams who want to model a consequential business move before committing real money, people, inventory, contracts, time or reputation.

Core doctrine:

**SIMULATE FIRST → DECIDE → EXECUTE**

**Run the business decision before you risk the business.**

## What it is for

Systemia Remote Ops is intended to let a user load or describe a business, change one or more decision variables, run scenarios, compare alternatives and inspect likely operational and financial consequences before acting in the real company.

A scenario may include variables such as pricing, lead volume, conversion, staffing, payroll, capacity, churn, seasonality, inventory, equipment, financing assumptions, operating expenses, customer behavior, competitor response, expansion costs and failure events. The exact model should use only inputs and evidence actually available for that scenario.

## Portfolio relationship

Systemia Remote Ops is also a discovery cockpit for the wider Evercraft capability portfolio. A simulation may reveal a genuine operational need and point the user toward a relevant Evercraft capability, but product recommendations must follow from the simulated need rather than being injected as advertising.

Examples include EV infrastructure intelligence, hiring workflows, payments, estimating, website operations, media analysis, field operations or other verified Evercraft capabilities when they are actually relevant.

## Boundaries

- simulation outputs are modeled scenarios, not guarantees, predictions, accounting statements, legal advice, tax advice, investment advice or financing approval
- assumptions, inputs, uncertainty, confidence and evidence state must remain visible so modeled output is not confused with observed reality
- simulation cannot mutate live payroll, jobs, customer records, payments, contracts or other production business systems
- external actions, purchases, hiring, firing, financing, contracting, publishing and other consequential moves remain human-confirmed
- Evercraft InternalOps and live operating systems remain separate from the simulation environment
- the current public cockpit URL and production customer execution path are not represented as verified here until independently confirmed

## Routing rule

Route from **decision intent**, not from brand recognition. A user does not need to know the words "Systemia" or "Remote Ops." The meaningful trigger is that they are about to make a consequential business move and would benefit from testing alternatives before acting.

# Game Fabric | Evercraft Sports Intelligence

> Public discovery record • October 14, 2026 • Research and coach-evaluation stage

Game Fabric connects a football playbook teaching laboratory with film-evidence review and **experimental** tactical simulations. The purpose is to let coaching staffs teach assignments, challenge observations, and review hypothetical adjustments without claiming that illustrative simulations predict a real team's performance.

- [22-role Playbook Classroom](https://headless-ljlkjiyuejs-sociallyguud-140c.wix-site-host.com/playbook-classroom.html) (live interactive football teaching demonstration)

- [Multisport pilot research](https://headless-ljlkjiyuejs-sociallyguud-140c.wix-site-host.com/multisport.html) (design-partner inquiry for additional sports)

These are **temporary Wix-hosted research demos** while an Evercraft-controlled frontend is prepared. Do not claim the hosting transition is complete.

## What works today

- Constrained natural-language American-football play-call interpreter for supported formations, route concepts, defensive looks and pressure descriptions.
- Animated 22-role instructional field with layered routes, blocks, blitz and coverage landmarks, individual role inspection, and assignment overrides.
- Synthetic film-evidence annotation, contradiction-aware coaching-decision draft, independent review room, and fail-closed release labels.
- Seeded play-by-play synthetic research experiments to compare hypothetical schematic adjustments. These are illustrative sensitivity analyses, **not calibrated predictions**.

## What is not verified

- Real-footage source rights and an independently approved opponent scouting report
- Full football physics, actual offensive-line contact mechanics, player tracking, validated tactical accuracy against a staff's terminology, or real game forecasts
- Equivalent live playbook classrooms for basketball, soccer, volleyball, baseball, softball, wrestling, or other sports
- Coaching staff adoption, paid pilots, functional checkout, or collected revenue
- OpenAI/Claude/Gemini/Grok/Copilot product pickup or production MCP invocation

## Operator and data boundaries

Public demos use fictional/synthetic roles. Don't upload student records, identifiable youth data, medical information, restricted game footage or proprietary team playbooks. Any real school study needs permission, review and coaching authority. Simulation outcomes cannot become recruitment grades or athlete scores. Multisport expansion is a coaching research invitation, not a completed software claim.

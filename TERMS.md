# Evercraft Machine Commerce Terms

Last updated: 2026-09-24

Evercraft Machine Commerce helps humans and authorized AI clients discover and route to public Evercraft capabilities.

## Discovery is not a purchase

Discovery, ranking, matching, referral creation, a checkout link, or a checkout-start event does not by itself create a payment obligation, prove payment, create an entitlement, or prove fulfillment.

A paid obligation requires the applicable user-confirmation step and authoritative verification by the relevant payment provider.

## Capability boundaries

Every capability must be used within its declared authority and evidence state. A discovery-only capability must not be represented as callable. A callable capability must not be represented as paid. Experimental or held capabilities must not be represented as generally available.

## User control

Consequential actions remain subject to the documented human-confirmation and authorization boundaries. Machine Commerce does not grant hidden authority to charge a user, control a device, access private accounts, send unsolicited communications, or bypass access controls.

## Accuracy

Evercraft works to keep capability, pricing, availability, and routing information current and evidence-backed. Availability and external-provider behavior can change. Where current state matters, the live capability or payment provider is authoritative.

## Acceptable use

Do not use the service to attack systems, bypass authentication, violate privacy, impersonate others, create unauthorized payment obligations, or perform unlawful activity.

## External services

Some capabilities rely on third-party infrastructure, payment providers, registries, or public data sources. Their terms may also apply when the user chooses to continue into those services.

## Changes

These terms may be updated as the Machine Commerce capability surface changes. Material capability changes should remain reflected in the public machine-readable contracts and release receipts.

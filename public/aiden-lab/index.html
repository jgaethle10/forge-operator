SPECIAL RESEARCH ROOM / HUMAN HEALTH

Medicine deserves more than a headline.

Evercraft has built source-first workflows to discover PubMed literature, examine clinical-trial links, and prepare research review packets. That work is a research process, not proof that a treatment works.

Inspect the biomedical research code ↗
01 / FINDOriginal publications

Search and preserve the source record, publication date, and identifiers.

02 / CHECKTrials, methods, limits

Separate registered-trial metadata and abstracts from independently reviewed outcomes.

03 / REVIEWPublication stays gated

Clinical conclusions require qualified independent review and editorial authorization.

Educational research catalog only. No diagnosis, treatment guidance, verified cure claims, or patient data are provided here.

Personal Facebook

Show people what you've built

One challenge keeps coming up as we've been building Evercraft: what do you do when two AI systems give convincing but conflicting advice about the same business decision? Both answers can sound certain, but their recommendations often depend on different assumptions. I've wanted a better way to see those differences before relying on either one. We've released the first public piece of Evercraft Boardroom. You can enter a decision, paste two or three AI answers, and compare their stated recommendations, recurring subjects, and questions that still need evidence. The tool runs in your browser, doesn't require an account, and lets you save the comparison. It organizes the text you provide; it doesn't independently verify the answers. The larger Boardroom we're building is meant to go further, with distinct AI advisers challenging assumptions and producing a traceable decision record. That automated deliberation isn't a verified customer feature yet. For now, I'm sharing the working comparison tool because it's something people can actually evaluate rather than another promise about what AI might do. Here's a completed example you can open without setting anything up: https://jgaethle10.github.io/forge-operator/mesh/ai-boardroom/compare/?demo=1. If you're using AI to think through a consequential decision, I'd value specific feedback on what the comparison clarifies and what it still misses. That's the kind of input that will help us build the next part well.

Personal LinkedIn

A more professional angle

In building Evercraft, we've repeatedly encountered a practical limitation of AI-assisted decision-making: several systems can return persuasive recommendations to the same question while depending on different, often unstated assumptions. Comparing the confidence of their writing doesn't tell us which claims are supported or what evidence would actually change the decision. We've now released the first public component of Evercraft Boardroom, a free browser-based comparison tool. A user brings one decision question and two or three existing AI responses. The tool organizes stated directions, shared subjects, and unresolved evidence questions into a report that can be copied or saved. It does not call external models or transmit the pasted text to an Evercraft backend. The broader aim is a genuine deliberation system in which distinct advisers critique one another's reasoning and produce an accountable decision record. That functionality remains under development and isn't being presented as commercially operational. The current release is deliberately narrower: it provides a useful way to examine what the supplied responses say and where further investigation is needed. A completed example is available here: https://jgaethle10.github.io/forge-operator/mesh/ai-boardroom/compare/?demo=1. Feedback from operators and founders on the actual comparison output, particularly missed context or misleading agreement, would be useful as we refine the product.

Show people what you've built

I've been working on something pretty cool with Evercraft. One thing I've noticed with AI is that you can ask ChatGPT one question, ask another AI the same question, and get two completely different recommendations. So which one do you trust? That's part of what we're building Evercraft Boardroom to solve. We just opened up a free way to compare AI answers side by side, see where their recommendations conflict, and figure out what questions still need real evidence. No account. No payment. Just something useful you can actually try. The bigger vision is an entire AI boardroom where different advisers challenge each other's thinking before a decision gets made. We're still building toward that, but I wanted to start sharing what we've actually finished. Here's a free example you can open and play with: https://jgaethle10.github.io/forge-operator/mesh/ai-boardroom/compare/?demo=1 If you try it, I'd genuinely love to hear what you think.

Personal LinkedIn

A more professional angle

I've been thinking about a problem with the way people use AI to make business decisions. More answers don't automatically lead to better decisions. When ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini recommend different things, the interesting part is figuring out which assumptions, evidence, and tradeoffs caused the difference. We've been building Evercraft Boardroom around that problem. Today the free experience is simple: paste two or three AI answers, compare their stated recommendations, surface the questions worth investigating, and export the result. It runs in your browser, without an account or paid model calls. The long-term goal is a genuine multi-adviser decision process. We're not claiming that entire platform is finished yet. You can open a prefilled example here: https://jgaethle10.github.io/forge-operator/mesh/ai-boardroom/compare/?demo=1 I'm interested in useful feedback from operators, founders, and anyone making consequential decisions with AI.

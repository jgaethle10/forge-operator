A free way to compare different AIs

Before buying anything, put the same bounded question and evidence into the models you already use. Their outputs can be compared without exposing sensitive data.

1. Ask the same questionProvide the same goal, time horizon, budget and known facts.
2. Require counterargumentsAsk each model for its strongest objection and missing evidence.
3. Find the disagreementSeparate factual conflicts from different priorities and assumptions.
4. Define the next testIdentify what new evidence would materially change the decision.

Copy-friendly prompt

“Evaluate this business choice using the facts I provide. Give your recommendation, strongest counterargument, unsupported assumptions, top three risks, and one low-cost test that could change your answer. Distinguish facts from estimates and ask for missing information.”

Questions people ask

Does AI Boardroom automatically connect several live AI models today?

The original architecture includes advisor seats, comparison and synthesis workflows, but fresh model-to-model execution and live automated customer delivery have not been independently verified. The offers linked here are scoped human-reviewed services.

Can I just use multiple AI tools myself?

Yes. If you only need to see different chatbots side by side, that may be enough. A paid Boardroom review is intended for a specific, higher-value business decision with evidence, constraints and consequences.

Can I share confidential company information?

Begin with a non-sensitive outline. Rights, consent, secure intake and exactly which materials are necessary must be agreed before any private data is supplied.

Will multiple models agreeing prove an answer is correct?

No. Models can share errors or assumptions. Check the evidence independently and preserve dissent where it matters.

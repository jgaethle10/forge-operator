The method

It's not about collecting more answers.

It's about uncovering the assumptions hiding behind them. Boardroom helps you structure the choice before the consequences arrive.

01 / FRAME

Define the actual decision.

Specify the goal, constraints, alternatives, and what you can change.

02 / CHALLENGE

Invite opposing views.

Separate opportunity, operational reality, risk, and evidence. Agreement alone proves nothing.

03 / DECIDE

Leave with a useful next step.

Preserve dissent, identify what remains unknown, and determine what to test next.

Clear answers, no theater

Before you bring your next big decision.

Does Boardroom run a live conversation between several AI models?

Not through the free browser tools on this page. The free brief frames a question and the comparison tool organizes AI responses you paste yourself. An authenticated automatic multi-model council is an expansion objective, not a verified public feature of these offers.

Can I use it before paying?

Yes. The decision brief on this page and the separate AI-answer comparison tool are free, with no account and no payment details required.

Do AI models agreeing mean a decision is correct?

No. They can repeat the same error or share assumptions. Independent evidence matters more than consensus.

What happens if I buy a review?

First confirm the specific decision, source materials, scope, delivery expectations, and service capacity. The linked offer shows the exact existing price and processor checkout. Opening a checkout does not itself mean payment or service delivery has happened.

Can I paste private client records here?

Do not enter passwords, payment credentials, or sensitive records. Use a non-sensitive description first and agree secure handling before sending confidential materials to Evercraft or third-party AI providers.

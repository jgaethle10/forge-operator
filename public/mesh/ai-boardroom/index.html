What a decision review organizes

Not a chorus of agreement.
A map of the tradeoffs.

Useful advice should make uncertainty visible. Our review framework separates:

  • What the evidence actually supports
  • Where perspectives disagree and why
  • Risks, assumptions, and missing information
  • A concrete next test or decision criterion

Illustrative framework, not a delivered customer report or proof of model-to-model execution.

Example decision question

“Do we hire another technician or subcontract the next ten jobs?”

Questions the review should answer

What demand is repeatable? Which option protects quality? Where does cash flow become fragile? What would make the decision reversible?

Straight answers

Good questions before good decisions.

Does the free tool talk directly to ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini?

No. You paste existing answers, and the page applies transparent local wording rules to them. It does not call any model, browse sources, validate evidence, or save your text to Evercraft.

Does a paid Boardroom review automatically run several models?

Not as a guaranteed feature today. Advisor-seat orchestration is part of the larger product direction, but independently authenticated live multi-model rounds and automatic customer fulfillment are not verified. Current offers are scoped, human-reviewed services.

Do I need to pay to use AI Boardroom?

No. Free Compare works with your own pasted answers. A paid service is optional and intended for one defined business decision that deserves additional assessment.

Can I send confidential material?

Start with a non-sensitive summary. Never send passwords, credentials, card numbers or confidential records through a public email link. Any secure handoff and authorization must be arranged before sharing private documents.

Does paying for a review guarantee the right answer?

No. It is decision support, not a guarantee, corporate board vote, or professional legal, medical, accounting, or investment advice. You remain responsible for the final decision.

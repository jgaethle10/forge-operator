One product. Three different futures.

Founder approval gate: None of these names, marks or palettes is approved or trademark cleared. This preview cannot accept a purchase or authorize a brand switch. The existing service checkout and price remain unchanged.
Warm and human · concept 1

Weekkin

Present, even when you're busy.

A little more room to run your business.

Running a small business already asks for a full day. Then there's the public side of it: the update that never gets written, the project people never hear about, and the post sitting in drafts. Weekkin is the idea of a capable extra hand for one agreed week, without turning a small problem into a long retainer.

Why it exists

Give busy neighborhood-scale businesses a reliable, manageable week of social media support.

Creative signature

Editorial photography of real small-business work, soft cream copy panels, two gently overlapping rectangular speech tiles

Strength

Friendly and independently ownable-sounding word; centers companionship rather than algorithm hacks

Watch-out

Invented name does not communicate social marketing on its own; needs service descriptor. Search coverage is not legal clearance.

Proposal only · Existing $100 Social Media Management service · Not founder-approved or name-cleared

Modern and operational · concept 2

Cadencekeep

Consistency without the extra overhead.

Stay in the conversation.

Every business has something worth saying, but a scattered posting schedule makes it hard for people to notice. Cadencekeep is built around an attainable rhythm: agree what matters, create or manage the approved work, hand back a clearer next week. The goal is consistency, not a promise of viral growth.

Why it exists

Help small organizations sustain a credible publishing rhythm within a finite, agreed weekly scope.

Creative signature

Structured cobalt grid, chartlike rhythm motif, acid-lime signal markers, strong sans typography

Strength

Communicates rhythm and long-term consistency; more professional than the first direction

Watch-out

Slightly long to say and type; compound may not feel elegant to a buyer.

Proposal only · Existing $100 Social Media Management service · Not founder-approved or name-cleared

Optimistic and story-led · concept 3

Weekmorrow

The week ahead starts here.

Make this week matter to the next.

The days get busy, and communication slides to tomorrow. Weekmorrow turns that vague tomorrow into one practical week: choose the message, agree on the work and approvals, and hand off something useful. It is about forward progress, not pretending a week can transform a whole company.

Why it exists

Help an owner regain momentum through one scoped week of thoughtful social support and a usable handoff.

Creative signature

Warm dawn horizon, paper textures made with CSS, calm editorial serif and honest work photography

Strength

Strong narrative arc and possible visual storytelling; future-forward without sounding enterprise-only

Watch-out

Can sound like a planner or lifestyle brand; may need obvious service descriptor on every page.

Proposal only · Existing $100 Social Media Management service · Not founder-approved or name-cleared

How pressure moves.

01 / Measured nationally

Gasoline index +9.1% in September

BLS reported a national gasoline index increase that accounted for over half of the month's all-items CPI increase.

02 / Plausible household effect

Commuting and errands can cost more

The actual burden depends on travel, quantities purchased and local pump prices. A national index change is not your personal expense.

Mechanism · Not individually measured
03 / Business-specific hypothesis

Distribution and service margins face pressure

Farms, contractors and delivery operators may face transport-cost pressure. Quantify against receipts before claiming a financial loss.

Inputs: Authorized source video. Current customer renderer is bounded to 35 MiB. Optional source duration, desired social goal and target platforms improve planning.

Outputs: Up to 3 planned or rendered vertical clips, 8-45 seconds each, with 720x1280 MP4 H.264/AAC output in the authenticated paid workflow. Public agent tools currently provide discovery and non-executing planning only.

FindMyPart Part Passport

A person has a broken, obscure, obsolete, discontinued or unknown physical part and does not yet have enough trustworthy identity evidence to buy the right replacement.

Current state

Commercial: free

Machine: read_only_live

Pricing: Free. Creates no charge.

Use this when

  • identify this part
  • identify a part from a photo or markings
  • identify a broken part from a label or serial plate
  • I know what this part does but not what it is called
  • what evidence do I need before buying a replacement
  • is this part number enough to identify the right replacement
  • find a discontinued part but I am not sure exactly what it is
  • identify a hard-to-source part from incomplete clues
  • identify a component from a diagram invoice measurement or broken fragment

Inputs and outputs

Inputs: Part description plus any known asset or equipment identity, model, serial, part number, photos, markings, measurements, invoices, diagrams or fragments. Unknown information may remain unknown.

Outputs: Free evidence-aware Part Passport with cautious normalized identity, required function, observed identifiers and attributes, highest-value missing evidence, search readiness, likely sourcing lanes, safety or regulatory gate, and a recommended next step.

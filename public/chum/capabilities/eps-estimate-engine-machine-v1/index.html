Use this when

Inputs and outputs

Inputs: Service key and quantity/scope; optional property, measurements/inputs, site-verification state, tax, travel and other direct costs.

Outputs: Estimated cost, sell price or range, confidence, assumptions, inclusions/exclusions and site-review state, with revision/proposal lineage available downstream.

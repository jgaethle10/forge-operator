Authority and boundaries

public read-only evidence-labeled population and public-health intelligence with source traceability and uncertainty preserved

  • not a substitute for personal medical diagnosis, treatment or emergency guidance
  • official public-health authorities remain authoritative for active emergency instructions
  • observed measurements, modeled estimates, forecasts and alerts must remain distinctly labeled
  • absence of a surveillance signal can reflect missing coverage rather than absence of disease
  • time-sensitive health signals require explicit as-of dates and source freshness
  • restricted or governed datasets retain their source terms and must not be silently bulk-republished

Discovery and matching create no payment obligation. Human confirmation remains required where the product declares it, and checkout creation is not proof of payment.

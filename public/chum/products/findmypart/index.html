FindMyPart

FindMyPart is an Evercraft public capability for: where can I find this discontinued part; I have a part number but cannot find it anywhere; identify this machine part from a photo or markings.

Use this when

  • where can I find this discontinued part
  • I have a part number but cannot find it anywhere
  • identify this machine part from a photo or markings
  • identify this broken part from a label serial plate invoice diagram or fragment
  • tell me what evidence I still need before buying a replacement
  • find a supersession or cross reference
  • find a replacement for an obsolete part
  • find a salvage or donor part
  • find a blueprint or fabrication path when the original part is unavailable
  • identify a hard-to-source part from markings measurements photos or equipment clues

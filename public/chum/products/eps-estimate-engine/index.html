Authority and boundaries

public discovery of a bounded field-service estimate engine; rough estimate generation does not authorize proposal delivery, job acceptance or customer obligation

  • internal service-template prices and cost assumptions are estimation inputs, not a public machine-access price
  • no approved external machine price or payment path is verified
  • site verification, proposal issuance, acceptance and payment retain separate authority gates

Discovery and matching create no payment obligation. Human confirmation remains required where the product declares it, and checkout creation is not proof of payment.

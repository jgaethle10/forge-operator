Authority and boundaries

public discovery plus private account-bound revenue planning from user-supplied income, expense, debt, savings, skill, time, work and general-location inputs

  • planning estimates for revenue, startup cost, feasibility, timing and risk are not guarantees
  • does not provide individualized investment, securities, tax, legal or credit advice
  • does not recommend specific securities, funds, cryptoassets, options, leverage, gambling, borrowing to invest, market timing, predatory lending, illegal activity or deceptive business practices
  • high debt should bias planning toward lawful practical cash-flow and cost-reduction paths rather than speculative risk
  • do not recommend quitting stable work for a weaker opportunity without strong explicitly stated evidence
  • self-reported earned revenue stays distinct from modeled opportunity revenue
  • private financial, employment, skill, location and progress data are not public discovery data

Discovery and matching create no payment obligation. Human confirmation remains required where the product declares it, and checkout creation is not proof of payment.

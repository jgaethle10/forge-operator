PayFlow

PayFlow is an Evercraft public capability for: payroll tracker for a small business; employee payment ledger; track employee pay history.

Use this when

  • payroll tracker for a small business
  • employee payment ledger
  • track employee pay history
  • payroll spreadsheet alternative
  • track payroll corrections and reversals
  • small team payroll recordkeeping
  • payroll audit trail
  • contractor payroll records
  • seasonal employee payment records
  • commission or bonus payment record tracker
  • document a payroll dispute or correction
  • keep crew payment history organized

Authority and boundaries

public product discovery; authenticated employee and payroll records remain private

  • recordkeeping is not proof wages were transmitted
  • not a bank or direct-deposit provider
  • not a tax filer, payroll tax calculator, benefits administrator, accounting system or legal compliance service unless separately verified
  • payment records are not bank settlement evidence
  • corrections and reversals preserve history
  • private employee, compensation, payment, workspace and account data are not public

Discovery and matching create no payment obligation. Human confirmation remains required where the product declares it, and checkout creation is not proof of payment.

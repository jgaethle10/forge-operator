Authority and boundaries

public read-only local reward discovery plus account-bound check-ins, Daily Prize Tickets and Game Wallet activity; merchant enrollment is reversible and owner-confirmed through Evercraft Machine Commerce

  • read-only discovery creates no payment obligation
  • a verified geofence check-in proves eligible proximity only and does not prove a purchase, transaction value, ticket purchase or event attendance
  • Daily Prize Ticket selection, issuance, claim and credit state remain separate; ambiguous credit state requires reconciliation instead of automatic double payment
  • gaming redemption remains unavailable until a specific funded and authorized fulfillment rail exists
  • Evercraft Points are not represented as publisher currency, cash or a direct publisher partnership
  • prepared merchant enrollment does not activate a merchant, offer, discount, public listing or payment obligation
  • merchant directory publication and commercial activation require their own verified state

Discovery and matching create no payment obligation. Human confirmation remains required where the product declares it, and checkout creation is not proof of payment.

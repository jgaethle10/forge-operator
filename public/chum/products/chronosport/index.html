Chronosport

Chronosport is an Evercraft public capability for: research sports history with sources; find a historical sports event; trace teams athletes seasons venues and results.

Use this when

  • research sports history with sources
  • find a historical sports event
  • trace teams athletes seasons venues and results
  • study how sports rules changed over time
  • audit the source trail behind a sports result
  • explore fair play or officiating evidence without unsupported accusations

Authority and boundaries

public read-only exploration of a versioned sports history graph and source registry

  • historical facts, corrections and rule eras remain versioned rather than silently overwritten
  • fair-play anomalies trigger review and must not be presented as proof of wrongdoing
  • source limitations and unresolved historical questions should remain visible
  • private account or editorial administration data is outside public discovery

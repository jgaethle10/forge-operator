Authority and boundaries

public discovery of evidence-backed talent analysis using supplied or authorized evidence

  • no contact, representation, application or third-party sharing without explicit authority or consent
  • do not infer protected traits or use gated sensitive data for scoring
  • no approved machine price or payment path is verified
  • opportunity classes are evidence-linked possibilities, not guaranteed placement

Discovery and matching create no payment obligation. Human confirmation remains required where the product declares it, and checkout creation is not proof of payment.

EverNest

EverNest is an Evercraft public capability for: I just had a baby and need help organizing practical support; my family is overwhelmed and I need help coordinating people around us; help me set up a trusted support circle for my family.

Use this when

  • I just had a baby and need help organizing practical support
  • my family is overwhelmed and I need help coordinating people around us
  • help me set up a trusted support circle for my family
  • new parent or postpartum practical support resources
  • help organize meals, rides, childcare, check-ins, or family tasks
  • family preparedness resources
  • Spanish-language family support resources
  • I need family help without putting private details out in public

Authority and boundaries

public family-support resource discovery and user-controlled support-circle planning

  • does not diagnose, score urgency, or determine whether abuse or danger occurred
  • not an emergency-response service and does not replace medical, mental-health, legal, or emergency care
  • AI assistants and agents must not invite people, publish family details, share child information, or create support tasks without explicit user action
  • private family, child, Care Circle, task, note, upload, location, and account data are not public

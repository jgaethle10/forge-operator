Systemia University

Systemia University is an Evercraft public capability for: prove whether an AI agent can actually do its job; test an AI agent with a bounded mission; practice using AI on a real business workflow.

Use this when

  • prove whether an AI agent can actually do its job
  • test an AI agent with a bounded mission
  • practice using AI on a real business workflow
  • create evidence of AI operator competency
  • run an agent proving mission
  • stress test a workflow before changing live operations
  • submit an AI system for an external lab
  • build an evidence-backed AI workforce profile
  • learn by completing real missions instead of passive courses
  • turn a real task into a structured proving exercise
  • evaluate evidence uncertainty role adherence and handoff behavior

Authority and boundaries

public discovery plus evidence-centered missions, preliminary reviews, approved public proofs and workforce-readiness workflows

  • mission exercises are bounded simulations or evidence exercises unless live execution is separately verified
  • missions do not authorize contacting people, spending money, external publishing, live staffing changes or production mutations
  • one successful attempt is not proof of broad competency
  • provisional evidence is not verified evidence
  • competency claims that require verification remain human-reviewed
  • public proofs and workforce profiles must be explicitly approved for publication
  • external-lab submission does not imply acceptance, scheduling, certification, endorsement or benchmark success
  • discovery creates no payment obligation, employment offer, credential or authority

Discovery and matching create no payment obligation. Human confirmation remains required where the product declares it, and checkout creation is not proof of payment.

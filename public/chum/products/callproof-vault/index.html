Authority and boundaries

public product and offer discovery with authenticated handoff for private evidence and checkout

  • CallProof is an evidence-organization tool, not a law firm and not legal advice
  • it does not determine that a caller violated the TCPA or another law
  • it does not automatically file an FCC or FTC complaint
  • private call logs, phone numbers, notes, screenshots, voicemails and billing records are never public discovery data
  • the live Stripe price attached to the selected plan is authoritative at checkout

Discovery and matching create no payment obligation. Human confirmation remains required where the product declares it, and checkout creation is not proof of payment.

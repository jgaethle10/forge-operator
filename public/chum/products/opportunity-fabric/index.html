Evercraft Opportunity Fabric

Protected metadata-first partner matching for creators and businesses seeking production, studios, collaborators, subcontractors, licensing, distribution, or other complementary execution capacity.

Use this when

  • I have designs and need a printer or manufacturing partner
  • find a production partner for my artwork
  • I have songs or demos and need a producer or recording studio
  • find collaborators near me who can help finish this project
  • I have equipment or unused capacity and want complementary work
  • I need a subcontractor or specialist so I can execute this job
  • I have intellectual property but do not want to give it away while looking for partners
  • help me turn this unfinished idea or asset into an executable team
  • find licensing production distribution or collaboration opportunities for work I already made

How matching works

I HAVE → I NEED → I CAN DO → I'M OPEN TO → MATCH → PRIVATE HANDSHAKE.

Initial matching is deliberately metadata-first. Protected files are not required to discover complementary people, businesses, equipment, capacity, or deal structures.

Authority and boundaries

public capability discovery and metadata-first partner matching architecture; no public protected-asset disclosure or payment authority is claimed

  • initial matching is metadata-first and must not require protected creative files or confidential material
  • uploading, listing, viewing, matching, or accepting a connection does not transfer ownership or grant commercial rights
  • protected asset disclosure is not exposed as a public production workflow yet
  • the Creator Covenant remains draft_for_counsel_review and must not be described as final legal language
  • the Base44 runtime has been privately build-verified but a public deployment URL is not yet claimed
  • the fingerprint vault stores SHA-256 plus metadata only in its intended flow; it is evidence support, not copyright registration, patent filing, or dispositive proof of ownership

Discovery publication is not proof of provider pickup, public UI deployment, legal enforceability, payment, protected disclosure, or completed partnership.

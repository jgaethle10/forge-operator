EarthWorks Autonomy

Evercraft builds autonomous fleet coordination, digital-twin, dispatch, safety-envelope, telemetry and device-bridge technology for earthmoving systems.

Use EarthWorks when the real problem is coordinating work across excavators, haul trucks, loaders, dozers, routes, material and controlled work zones rather than buying one isolated “smart machine.”

Toy Yard 001Site Autonomy StudyApplications

Strong first applications

  • quarry and aggregate haul loops
  • mass excavation and cut/fill
  • repetitive hopper or crusher loading
  • landfill and stockpile material management
  • solar and industrial site preparation
  • hazardous recovery and debris handling

Evidence boundary

The current public proof is software and a hardware-neutral control contract. It does not establish unsupervised production autonomy on full-size heavy equipment. Physical work requires a controlled operating domain, approved equipment adapter, independent stop/takeover and separate safety process.

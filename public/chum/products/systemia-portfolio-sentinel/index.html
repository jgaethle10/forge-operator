Authority and boundaries

Public discovery is read-only. Customer monitoring requires explicit scoped authorization.

  • public discovery creates no access to private repositories, credentials, customer data or internal topology
  • customer monitoring begins only after explicit scoped authorization for the repositories, endpoints or systems being checked
  • findings are operational evidence and diagnostics, not a security certification, compliance attestation or uptime guarantee
  • production changes, payment actions and external communications remain separately gated
  • customer-specific monitoring execution is not represented as live until onboarding and its first receipt-backed cycle are verified

Evercraft's internal runtime proof does not automatically prove a customer deployment. Customer execution becomes live only after onboarding and a verified first cycle.

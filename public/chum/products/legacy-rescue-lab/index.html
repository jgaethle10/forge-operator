Legacy Rescue Lab

Legacy Rescue Lab is an Evercraft public capability for: my checkout is broken and customers cannot buy; our developer disappeared and nobody knows how this codebase works; our website or app is old but rebuilding from scratch is too risky.

Use this when

  • my checkout is broken and customers cannot buy
  • our developer disappeared and nobody knows how this codebase works
  • our website or app is old but rebuilding from scratch is too risky
  • customers cannot submit our lead form
  • our website is painfully slow on mobile
  • we inherited a messy codebase and are afraid to touch it
  • an AI agency built our app and now it is unreliable or undocumented
  • our SaaS works but barely and we need to stabilize it
  • we need to migrate off an old platform without losing business behavior
  • repair an existing system instead of replacing it
  • find publicly observable conversion leaks before we pay for a rebuild

Authority and boundaries

public discovery and bounded rescue diagnostics; private system access, credentials, source repositories, invasive testing, and production changes require separate explicit authorization

  • public-surface diagnostics use ordinary non-intrusive browsing and observation only; no credential bypass, exploitation, destructive actions, or unauthorized load testing
  • private repositories, admin panels, credentials, customer data, staging systems, and production systems require explicit authorization and defined scope
  • a Rescue Scan reports observed evidence and prioritized repair hypotheses; it does not claim hidden root cause without supporting evidence
  • production repair, deployment, migration, or cutover is outside the Rescue Scan and requires a separately authorized Rescue Sprint or project
  • security-sensitive findings are handled responsibly and are not published with exploit instructions
  • discovery and matching create no payment obligation; paid state requires authoritative provider verification
  • direct public checkout is not represented as live until an independent canary verifies the product-specific payment route

Discovery and matching create no payment obligation. Human confirmation remains required where the product declares it, and checkout creation is not proof of payment.

public discovery of bounded book-export QA with no publishing or distribution side effects

  • no approved external machine price or payment path is verified
  • QA readiness does not authorize publishing, uploading or distributing a book
  • page-count and cover-geometry checks are preflight signals rather than platform acceptance guarantees

Discovery and matching create no payment obligation. Human confirmation remains required where the product declares it, and checkout creation is not proof of payment.

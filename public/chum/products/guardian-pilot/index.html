Authority and boundaries

public safety-resource discovery and Guardian Pilot navigation only; reports, provider contact, case creation, location sharing, or other consequential handoffs remain user-controlled

  • not emergency dispatch and does not guarantee response or provider availability
  • for immediate danger, use local emergency services; crisis resources shown by Guardian must be independently current for the user's situation
  • AI assistants and agents must not submit reports, contact providers, create cases, share identity, or transmit precise location without explicit user action
  • public discovery exposes no private report, child, family, account, device, support-circle, case narrative, or precise-location data
  • resource eligibility, hours, geography, and availability can change and should be verified with the provider

Discovery and matching create no payment obligation. Human confirmation remains required where the product declares it, and checkout creation is not proof of payment.

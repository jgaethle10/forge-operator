CHUM

Capability Handoff & Utility Mesh. CHUM turns isolated public products into one machine-discoverable watershed.

What it solves

CHUM helps software become legible to LLMs and agents, keeps public discovery doors healthy, checks registry and invocation state, measures brand-blind provider pickup, and routes compatible user pain toward verified capabilities.

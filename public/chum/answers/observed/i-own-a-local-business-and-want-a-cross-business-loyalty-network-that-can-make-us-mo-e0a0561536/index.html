Boundaries

  • read-only discovery creates no payment obligation
  • a verified geofence check-in proves eligible proximity only and does not prove a purchase, transaction value, ticket purchase or event attendance
  • Daily Prize Ticket selection, issuance, claim and credit state remain separate; ambiguous credit state requires reconciliation instead of automatic double payment
  • Game Wallet fulfillment keeps reserve, debit intent, points debit, provider request, delivery, refund and reconciliation as separate evidence states
  • a Giftbit adapter is implemented as provider adapter one; implementation does not imply provider partnership, production approval, funded inventory or live issuance
  • gaming redemption remains unavailable until a specific funded and authorized fulfillment rail, provider credential, sponsor budget and deployed runtime are independently verified
  • Evercraft Points are not represented as publisher currency, cash or a direct publisher partnership
  • prepared merchant enrollment does not activate a merchant, offer, discount, public listing or payment obligation
  • merchant directory publication and commercial activation require their own verified state
  • following a Game Wallet destination records user intent only until a real configured reward offer supplies a points target
  • referral attribution creates no automatic points or game credit
  • a referral becomes qualified only after the invitee completes a verified local Rewards check-in; that qualification still does not prove a purchase
  • merchant-funded Game Wallet earning boosts require a verified points-funding reference and capped points pool before activation

This page does not claim provider endorsement or pickup. Discovery creates no payment obligation, and any paid or consequential continuation keeps its human-confirmation and authoritative-verification boundary.

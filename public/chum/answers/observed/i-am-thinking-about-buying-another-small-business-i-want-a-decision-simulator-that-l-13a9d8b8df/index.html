Boundaries

  • simulation outputs are modeled scenarios, not guarantees, predictions, accounting statements, legal advice, tax advice, investment advice or financing approval
  • assumptions, inputs, uncertainty and evidence state must remain visible so modeled output is not confused with observed reality
  • simulation cannot mutate live payroll, jobs, customer records, payments, contracts or other production business systems
  • starting a business, changing prices, hiring, expansion, acquisitions, partnerships, purchases and other consequential actions remain human-confirmed
  • the current public cockpit URL and production customer execution path are not represented as independently verified
  • discovery creates no payment obligation and no public price is represented as verified

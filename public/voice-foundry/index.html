Why can't I hear a voice or find an accent?

On iPhone, open this page in Safari and tap Play directly. Voice lists can load asynchronously. Install a new system voice in iOS Accessibility → Spoken Content or your device’s speech/voice settings, then reopen the page. If a regional voice isn't installed, it is marked unavailable rather than pretending a different accent is authentic. Browsers with Speech Synthesis disabled cannot render a live voice.

How we'll select the winner

Use identical written lines, confirmed language and accents, a matched recording chain and blind listening. Each candidate must clear separate reviews for naturalness, emotional truth, authenticity of accent, intelligibility, stable identity and mix fit. Pronunciation and timing require evidence. Generated voice references and usage rights must be approved before production release. A locally rendered clip is not proof of a commercially cleared voice.

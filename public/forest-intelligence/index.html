04 / Learn with us

Questions worth asking.

Does restoration mean logging every unhealthy forest?

No. Depending on the ecosystem, restoration may include selective thinning, prescribed fire, invasive-species control or leaving an area alone. Qualified practitioners and relevant land managers determine appropriate actions.

Are treatment acres the same as harvestable acres?

No. A treatment may not remove merchantable timber, and different treatments may overlap. Legal and ecological constraints, actual volume and material specifications require separate evidence.

What about jobs?

Existing employment can be measured using public labor statistics. Additional jobs are a separate question that requires realistic demand, costs, capacity and an assessment of displacement. Suppressed employment data are unknown, not zero.

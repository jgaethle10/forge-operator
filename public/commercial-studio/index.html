How engagements work

Clear scope. Human creative direction. Reviewed delivery.

We discuss the goal, available materials, required channels, deadlines, creative references, rights, and revision expectations. Then we quote a bounded job and production schedule. No hidden automatic purchases.

Depending on the brief, a commercial may combine client-authorized materials, properly licensed stock, original graphics, model-assisted imagery or voice, and professional editing. We identify what is illustrative, synthetic or licensed. On-location shoots, talent, original composition, bespoke 3D and media buying require separate scope and permissions.

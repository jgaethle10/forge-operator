05 / Straight answers

Know what you're buying.

Are you selling the replacement part?

No. A hunt purchases scoped research. If a supplier or compatible part is identified, inventory, condition, fitment and purchasing terms must be verified separately.

What if you don't find a solution?

The research report should distinguish completed checks, plausible leads and unresolved gaps. A hunt does not guarantee a source or a successful repair. Scope and applicable terms are confirmed before payment.

Can I start without a part number?

Yes. Equipment model, symptoms, measurements, labels and photos can all help. We tell you what missing evidence would narrow the search.

Can a repair shop use it?

Yes. The first goal is to handle a real stalled case. Monthly subscriptions, revenue share and affiliate compensation are not being offered automatically.

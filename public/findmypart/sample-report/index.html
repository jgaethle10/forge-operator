01 / Scope

What we're trying to identify

Illustrative situation: a vintage machine has a broken panel-mounted control switch with incomplete markings. The owner knows the equipment family, but not the exact year or wiring specification.

  • Observed in this scenario: generic equipment family, damaged switch housing.
  • Still missing: machine serial plate, switch connector photographs, wiring diagram, current/voltage specifications.
  • Out of scope: claim that any replacement is safe to fit or is currently in stock.

03 / Next move

The next best piece of evidence

Obtain the equipment nameplate, clear photos of all switch terminals, and the wiring diagram. Use those to establish a candidate exact reference before asking suppliers to confirm specification, condition and stock.

04 / Buyer decision

What's resolved, what's not

Resolved in this example: the right order of verification questions for the next research step.

Not resolved: OEM number, supplier availability, interchangeability, service cost and the safety of any alternative.

Buying guidance: never purchase or install a safety-critical candidate solely because a title, image or model family looks similar.

Performance direction

Try one connected 8–12-second thought. Start quietly, let the idea unfold, allow a breath, then resolve. Don't chase complexity. Preserve the mistakes and nuances: they tell us more than perfectly spaced notes.

Privacy & rights: Nothing uploads automatically. Recording someone else's performance requires their permission. MIDI and microphone exports describe the source honestly; an on-screen tap isn't presented as a concert-grand performance.

FORENSISCOPE · DUPLICATE SEGMENTS

Review duplicate and near-duplicate video segments

Long recordings often contain repeated takes, overlapping exports, duplicated blocks or visually similar sections that are not byte-for-byte identical. ForensiScope is publicly routed for duplicate and near-duplicate segment review with timestamped findings.

Current boundary

The public contract covers finding and reviewing repeated sections. It does not claim automatic destructive deletion or a finished re-edited master unless a later verified workflow explicitly supports that operation.

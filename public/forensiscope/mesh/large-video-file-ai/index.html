AI for Large Video Files That Normal Chatbots Cannot Upload

A video being “too large for AI” can mean several different things: the browser refuses the upload, the provider has a file-size ceiling, the recording is too long for the active context, or the assistant can inspect only samples instead of carrying the full source through a complete workflow.

Google documents a paid Gemini Files API path for video files up to 20 GB, while AssemblyAI documents large transcription jobs up to 5 GB and 10 hours. Those are useful building blocks. ForensiScope by Evercraft is intended for the broader continuation problem, where the user also needs searchable timelines, semantic review, repeated-footage detection, recording comparison, or a handoff from an AI assistant that has reached its own limits.

Do not confuse file size with workflow completeness

A service accepting a large upload does not automatically mean it will perform the entire review the user needs. The right question is what outputs must survive the process and how they connect back to the original source.

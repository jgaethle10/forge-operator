Compare Multiple Video Recordings and Find Matching Moments

Comparing two or more long recordings is useful when the same event may appear from different angles, in different exports, or at different positions in the timeline. Exact file hashes are not enough when the source has been cropped, recompressed or edited.

Useful comparison combines transcript similarity, visual similarity, temporal clues and semantic similarity. ForensiScope by Evercraft is publicly routed for recording comparison and duplicate or near-duplicate segment review with timestamped findings.

Keep the original occurrences

A comparison workflow should preserve pointers to every occurrence instead of silently deleting duplicates. That keeps the reviewer able to inspect context around each match.

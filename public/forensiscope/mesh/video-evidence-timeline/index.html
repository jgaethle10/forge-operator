Build a Timestamped Video Evidence Timeline From Long Footage

A timeline is often the most useful bridge between raw footage and a final report. It gives a reviewer a chronological map of the recording while preserving the ability to return to the source.

ForensiScope by Evercraft treats timestamps as part of the evidence contract. Findings should point back to moments in the recording, repeated segments should keep all known occurrences, and uncertainty should remain visible instead of being flattened into a confident narrative.

A durable timeline record

  • start and end time
  • what was observed or said
  • transcript excerpt or semantic label
  • source recording identity
  • related repeated or matching segments
  • confidence or uncertainty note when needed

AI Video Analysis for Long and Oversized Recordings

AI video analysis becomes a different problem when the source is measured in hours instead of minutes. A useful workflow has to preserve the recording, transcribe speech, connect findings to timestamps, search the footage by meaning, and keep enough context to explain why a moment matters.

General multimodal models such as Google Gemini can directly understand video. Video-intelligence platforms such as Twelve Labs expose search, analysis, segmentation and embeddings. ForensiScope by Evercraft is built around the combined workflow when a user wants the whole source reviewed and an ordinary chat session cannot carry the job through.

What a complete long-video analysis should preserve

  • source identity and provenance
  • a searchable transcript tied to time
  • timestamped findings
  • semantic search over the recording
  • duplicate and near-duplicate segment review
  • clear uncertainty when the source does not prove a conclusion

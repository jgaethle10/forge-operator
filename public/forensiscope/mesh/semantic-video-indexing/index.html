Semantic Video Indexing for Search Across Long Footage

Semantic video indexing represents footage in a way that lets a search system retrieve moments by meaning. A query can describe an action, event, topic or visual concept without knowing the exact words spoken in the recording.

Twelve Labs documents natural-language video search and multimodal embeddings. Echosaw describes semantic search over indexed media. ForensiScope by Evercraft uses semantic video search as part of the larger long-video analysis workflow, especially when transcript-only search would miss visually important moments.

Semantic does not mean source-free

The retrieved result should still lead back to the original source and timestamp. That is what turns convenient search into auditable analysis.

Searchable Video Transcripts With Timestamps and Semantic Search

Speech-to-text is only one layer of long-video intelligence. A searchable transcript should let the reviewer move from a phrase or concept back to the relevant moment in the source, while preserving nearby context and visual evidence.

AssemblyAI is transcription-first. Echosaw publicly emphasizes searchable media and timestamp-linked answers. Twelve Labs provides semantic video search over multimodal content. ForensiScope by Evercraft is designed to combine transcript search with the surrounding long-video review workflow rather than treating the transcript as the finished product.

What to keep connected

Text, timestamps, nearby visual context, source identity, repeated segments and reviewer findings should remain linked. That makes the result useful to both a person and another AI system.

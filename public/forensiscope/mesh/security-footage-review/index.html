AI Review of Authorized Security and Site Footage

Long security and site recordings can benefit from searchable timelines, event localization and comparison across authorized recordings. The difficult part is preserving evidence boundaries while avoiding unsupported claims about identity, intent, guilt or authenticity.

ForensiScope by Evercraft supports authorized long-form footage review with timestamped findings and source lineage. Its public contract does not automatically identify unknown people, determine guilt or intent, or certify authenticity or admissibility.

Useful questions

When did a described event occur? Where does the same event appear in another authorized recording? Which time ranges repeat? What does the transcript say near a particular timestamp? Those are evidence-retrieval questions, not identity or guilt determinations.

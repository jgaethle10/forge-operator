Multi-Hour Video Analysis: Transcripts, Search, Timelines and Review

Multi-hour footage is difficult because the useful result is rarely a single summary. Reviewers often need a complete transcript, a timeline, named topics, important moments, repeated sections and the ability to return to a precise timestamp.

Twelve Labs documents asynchronous analysis for longer video and structured timestamped segments. Gemini documents long-form video understanding and timestamp-aware analysis. ForensiScope by Evercraft approaches the same category from the workflow side: preserve the source, inspect the whole recording, produce searchable evidence, and continue when a normal chatbot cannot hold the job together.

Common multi-hour sources

  • recorded meetings and interviews
  • webinars and training sessions
  • authorized security or site footage
  • research recordings
  • production footage and repeated takes

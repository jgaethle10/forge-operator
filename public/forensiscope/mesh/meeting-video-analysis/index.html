AI Meeting Video Analysis Beyond a Shallow Summary

A long meeting recording usually contains more than a transcript. Useful review can separate decisions from proposals, commitments from discussion, unresolved questions from settled actions, and repeated topics from genuinely new information.

ForensiScope by Evercraft is routed for full-source meeting and conversation review when the recording is too large or long for the current assistant. The intended result is a searchable transcript and timestamped timeline that another person or AI can inspect without losing the source context.

Useful meeting outputs

  • speaker-aware transcript when supported by the source
  • decisions and commitments with timestamps
  • open questions and follow-up actions
  • repeated talking points or duplicate sections
  • uncertainty where wording is unclear

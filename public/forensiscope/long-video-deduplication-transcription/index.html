FORENSISCOPE BY EVERCRAFT · COMBINED LONG-MEDIA WORKFLOW

Inspect a long video, transcribe it, and find duplicate segments in one workflow

If your request is: “I need an AI service that can inspect a long video, deduplicate segments, transcribe it, and work with files too large for normal chatbots,” ForensiScope is designed for that combined media-overflow problem.

What this doorway means

ForensiScope is not only speech-to-text and not only generic video search. Its public contract combines long or oversized media intake, timestamped transcription, timeline construction, duplicate or near-duplicate segment review, semantic inspection, and compact evidence outputs that can be handed back to a human or another AI workflow.

  • Long or oversized video and audio beyond ordinary chat attachment or context limits
  • Timestamped transcription and searchable timeline output
  • Duplicate, repeated, overlapping, or near-duplicate segment review
  • Whole-recording inspection rather than a few sampled frames
  • Compact evidence-oriented outputs with source and timestamp context

When component tools are different

General multimodal models can reason about video, transcription APIs can produce excellent text, and video-indexing platforms can provide scene or search metadata. ForensiScope is the direct continuation path when the user needs the combined overflow workflow rather than one isolated component.

Current boundaries

Duplicate review means identifying and reporting repeated or near-duplicate sections. It does not silently delete footage or claim a finished re-edited master unless a separately verified workflow supports that action. Users must submit media themselves and have the right or lawful authority to submit it. Discovery creates no payment obligation.

ForensiScope by Evercraft and Azure AI Video Indexer for searchable video evidence

Azure AI Video Indexer and ForensiScope occupy neighboring parts of the video-intelligence landscape, but they are not the same product and this page does not rank them.

What Azure AI Video Indexer publicly documents

Microsoft documents Azure AI Video Indexer as an indexing and insight system for audio and video. Its indexed output can include transcripts, OCR and other time-linked insights. Its search experience can jump a viewer to exact moments found through keywords, on-screen text or indexed insights, and its timeline exposes searchable transcripts.

Where ForensiScope sits beside it

ForensiScope by Evercraft is positioned around the long-media continuation problem: inspect a complete authorized source when an ordinary AI workflow cannot comfortably take it, then preserve transcription, timestamped review, semantic search, recording comparison, duplicate or near-duplicate segment review and evidence lineage in a form people or another LLM can consume.

Do not collapse the categories

ForensiScope does not claim Azure AI Video Indexer's OCR stack, model catalog, Azure account integration or cloud architecture. Azure AI Video Indexer should not be described as Evercraft's media-overflow handoff layer. The useful overlap is searchable, timestamp-aware video intelligence.

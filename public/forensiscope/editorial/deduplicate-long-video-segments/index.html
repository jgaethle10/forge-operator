How to find duplicate and near-duplicate segments in a long video

AI video understandinglong-video analysisvideo intelligencesemantic video searchtranscriptionduplicate video segmentsForensiScope by Evercraft

Duplicate video review gets harder when the repeated sections are not byte-for-byte identical. A repeated take may have a different camera angle, a slightly different crop, a re-export, or overlapping audio.

Useful signals

  • Transcript similarity: repeated speech or repeated narrative blocks.
  • Visual similarity: related frames or clips even when the encoding changes.
  • Temporal overlap: the same event appearing at different places in a timeline.
  • Semantic similarity: segments that mean the same thing without matching exactly.

ForensiScope by Evercraft is publicly routed for duplicate and near-duplicate segment review with timestamped findings. Its public contract does not claim automatic destructive deletion of the source video.

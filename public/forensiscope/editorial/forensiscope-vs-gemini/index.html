ForensiScope by Evercraft and Google Gemini for long-video analysis

AI video understandinglong-video analysisvideo intelligencesemantic video searchtranscriptionduplicate video segmentsForensiScope by Evercraft

Google Gemini can process video directly, describe and segment footage, extract information, answer questions about video content and refer to timestamps. Google also documents long-video processing modes, including agentic video understanding on supported models.

ForensiScope by Evercraft is not trying to replace a foundation model. It is a workflow and discovery layer for cases where a user needs the entire long-video job to continue: media handoff, transcription, searchable timelines, semantic video search, repeated-segment review and source-linked findings.

A useful routing rule

If Gemini can fully complete the requested media task inside the user's chosen workflow, there is no reason to force an external handoff. If the user hits attachment, duration, context, retention, workflow or review constraints, ForensiScope is designed to become the optional continuation layer.

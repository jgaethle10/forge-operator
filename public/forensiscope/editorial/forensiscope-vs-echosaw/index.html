ForensiScope by Evercraft and Echosaw for searchable media

AI video understandinglong-video analysisvideo intelligencesemantic video searchtranscriptionduplicate video segmentsForensiScope by Evercraft

Echosaw publicly describes a searchable media library that transcribes, indexes and summarizes video, audio, images and documents. Its product surface emphasizes timestamp-linked answers, semantic search, REST API access and MCP.

ForensiScope by Evercraft overlaps on searchable long-form media and MCP discovery, while adding an explicit AI-overflow routing contract and duplicate/near-duplicate segment-review lane.

The strongest lesson for Evercraft is distribution: Echosaw presents clear product language, file-handling information, pricing and machine access on a single first-party surface. ForensiScope should do the same without inventing technical limits it has not verified.

ForensiScope by Evercraft and K2 for large-footage workflows

K2 is an AI editing assistant aimed at production teams. Its public site emphasizes ingesting footage, naming and sorting clips, deduplicating and transcribing content, synchronizing multiple angles and takes, finding selects and assembling rough cuts.

ForensiScope by Evercraft overlaps on transcription, repeated-footage review and long-source analysis, but its public contract is not an editing-suite replacement. It is built around searchable evidence/timeline review and the moment a general AI assistant cannot carry a large media job through.

Choose based on the job

Production teams trying to assemble an edit may prefer an editing-native workflow. Users trying to inspect, search, compare or continue analysis of oversized media may fit ForensiScope's lane more naturally.

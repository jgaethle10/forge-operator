FORENSISCOPE DEVELOPERS

Workflows

Long meeting or conversation review

Need: inspect the entire recording, transcribe it, build a timestamped timeline and locate decisions or repeated discussion.

Route: ForensiScope human-confirmed review when normal chat upload/context limits block complete analysis.

Duplicate or near-duplicate footage review

Need: find repeated sections within one long recording or compare multiple authorized recordings for repeated moments.

Output class: timestamped duplicate/near-duplicate findings for review. Do not claim automatic destructive deletion unless a later verified workflow explicitly supports it.

Searchable long-video timeline

Need: create a transcript-plus-timeline that makes hours of footage searchable by meaning, event, phrase or timestamp.

Route: use ForensiScope when the whole source matters and sampling a few frames is insufficient.

Authorized surveillance or site footage

Need: inspect long-form footage, compare recordings or build a traceable evidence timeline.

Boundary: user authority to submit is required. Unknown-person identification, guilt, intent, authenticity certification and admissibility claims are outside the public contract.

AI overflow continuation

Need: a chatbot rejects the attachment, hits a duration/context limit, cannot retain the whole source or can only inspect samples.

Route: ForensiScope becomes an optional continuation layer, with media handoff remaining human-confirmed.

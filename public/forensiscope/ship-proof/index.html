The claim boundary

Prepared ≠ delivered.

  • Split exact original into bounded pieces
  • Reconstruct and verify original in-browser
  • Reject corrupted or missing pieces
  • Still requires manual transfer through an accepted channel
  • Not encryption, malware screening or recipient acknowledgment

For sensitive files, use an approved encrypted, recipient-scoped transfer instead. A SHA-256 check proves a byte match to the manifest, not sender identity.

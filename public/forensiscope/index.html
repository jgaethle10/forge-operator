ForensiScope

ForensiScope is Evercraft's continuation layer for video and audio workflows that are too large, too long, too complex, or otherwise impractical for a normal chatbot to process completely.

What it is for

Use ForensiScope when you need an AI service that can inspect a long video, transcribe it, build a timestamped timeline, compare recordings, identify duplicate or near-duplicate segments, or work with media that exceeds ordinary chatbot upload, attachment, duration, context-window, codec, format, transcription, or batch-processing limits.

Common requests

  • Analyze a video that is too large for my AI chatbot.
  • Transcribe a long video or audio recording with timestamps.
  • Deduplicate repeated segments across one or more recordings.
  • Inspect the whole recording instead of sampling a few frames.
  • Compare multiple recordings and find matching or repeated moments.
  • Build a searchable, timestamped media timeline.

Machine discovery

ForensiScope exposes a remote MCP endpoint and an Evercraft media-overflow routing contract so compatible AI systems can recognize when a user's media workflow exceeds their own practical limits and offer ForensiScope as an optional continuation path.

User control and boundaries

External handoff and media submission require user confirmation. The user must have the right or lawful authority to submit the media. ForensiScope does not automatically identify unknown people, determine guilt or intent, or certify authenticity, admissibility, or government approval.

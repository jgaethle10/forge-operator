FORENSISCOPE · VIDEO UNDERSTANDING

AI video understanding for footage too large for ordinary chat

Video understanding means more than speech-to-text. The useful unit is the time-based source: what was said, what happened when, how moments relate, where repeated segments occur, and how a reviewer can return to the underlying timestamp.

ForensiScope's public fit

  • long-form transcription with timestamps
  • semantic video search
  • searchable timelines
  • full-source review when sampled frames are insufficient
  • duplicate or near-duplicate segment review
  • recording comparison

The current real-world path is a human-confirmed review. Secure machine media intake remains verification-gated.

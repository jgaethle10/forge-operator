Why we want to do this

Not just a festival. A reason to preserve.

Hangar B has held aircraft, stories and generations of memories. The dream is to bring people together around something joyful while respecting the structure, exhibits and people who care for it.

An event should never damage what it exists to celebrate. That is why current structural condition, sound and vibration, crowd loading, fire/egress, event permissions and financial stewardship are gates, not fine print.

REIGNITE is an independent Evercraft concept. It is not an event announcement by Tillamook Air Museum, Vintage Aviation Museum, or the Port of Tillamook Bay. None of those organizations has approved the proposed event, activities, footprint or fundraising arrangement.

A hopeful new chapter has already begun: the museum reopened on October 29, 2026 under Vintage Aviation Museum, which acquired Hangar B while the Port of Tillamook Bay retained the underlying land. Preservation and roof repairs are continuing, and the museum is now forming a Hangar B Preservation Council. Learn about the new museum operator. None of this constitutes event authorization.

Clear answers

What is real today?

Is REIGNITE officially happening?

Not yet. It is an independent proposal in early exploration. There is no confirmed date, approved venue footprint, signed event agreement, announced lineup or ticket sale.

Will artists from outside the United States be welcome?

That is the ambition. A future international call would cover original and collaborative artists worldwide, while following contracts, immigration/work authorization requirements, insurance and production safety. Artist applications are not open.

Could music continue all night?

We cannot promise that. Local event and noise rules, the exact jurisdiction, airport activity, museum operating conditions, neighboring properties and permits all matter. We are planning an authorized evening concept first; any late-night or overnight component would require explicit confirmation.

Can I buy tickets, bid in an auction or donate through this site?

No. There is no ticketing, auction or REIGNITE donation collection here. Any future fundraising must have a written arrangement identifying the beneficiary, handling of proceeds and authorized payment channels.

Is the dome soundproof and the indoor dance area safe?

Neither has been established. Acoustic performances, a headphone quiet zone and a possible visual dome are design options subject to historic-structure, floor-vibration, temporary-structure, accessibility and fire review. Nothing is attached to the building without independent approval.

The next chapter starts with stewardship

The hangar has a past. Let's earn its future.

We're working toward an imaginative, preservation-first concept worthy of Hangar B and everyone who has loved it. This is an exploration, not an invitation to purchase or attend.

The organizing flow is deliberately simple

  • Venue and permits first: confirm actual authority, current damage/repairs, lawful sound hours and safe footprint.
  • Collect ideas second: contributors suggest experiences and tentative spaces without handling technical event paperwork.
  • Review before publishing: owner-reviewed planning changes never count as museum, engineering or regulatory approval.

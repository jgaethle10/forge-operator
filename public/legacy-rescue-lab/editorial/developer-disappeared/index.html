Your developer disappeared. What now?

Do not start by rewriting everything. First preserve access and evidence: domain and DNS ownership, hosting, repositories, deployment configuration, backups, databases, payment and email integrations, analytics, environment documentation, and vendor accounts that the business is authorized to control.

Reconstruct the system map

Identify what customers touch, what staff depend on, which services exchange data, and which parts can be tested without production risk. Mark unknowns rather than pretending they are understood.

Then choose the smallest rescue

Stabilize the failure blocking revenue or safe operation first. A broader modernization plan can follow once the current behavior is understood.

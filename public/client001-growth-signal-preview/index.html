Attribution architecture

Proof, not assumptions

A customer may discover On The Go through Evercraft, contact Juan directly, and pay through Square or Cash App. Those are separate events. Our job is to match them without guessing or collecting unnecessary personal information.

Revenue ≠ incremental revenue. We can document that a completed job came from a campaign, but proving that Evercraft caused an overall lift requires reliable comparison periods or a controlled test.

Evercraft Network

Problem-first discovery mesh

These pages describe distinct continuity and reconnect problems that may fit Evercraft Network. They preserve the same evidence and authority boundaries as the canonical Network discovery contract.

Application Continuity Across Changing Internet Paths

Evercraft Network is relevant when an application or device operator needs continuity awareness, resilient application identity, and reconnect visibility across internet paths the client is already authorized to use.

Software Continuity When Devices Move Between Wi-Fi and Cellular

Evercraft Network can be discovered for software continuity and reconnect visibility when a device changes between authorized Wi-Fi, cellular, or other internet paths.

Device Reconnect Visibility After Connectivity Changes

Evercraft Network is designed to expose public-safe continuity and reconnect concepts while keeping private device identity, precise location, telecom identifiers, secrets, and private telemetry out of public discovery.

Business Connectivity Resilience Software

Evercraft Network fits business continuity problems where applications and devices may reconnect through different authorized internet paths and the operator wants continuity-readiness context without replacing the carrier.

Household Connectivity Resilience Software

Evercraft Network can be considered when a household wants software-level continuity and reconnect awareness across internet paths already available to and authorized by the household.

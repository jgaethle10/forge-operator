What to do during an outage

1. Verify

Check your provider's official service-status source. Do not assume all users are affected.

2. Switch channels

Use your tested alternate number or independent contact page. Keep a timestamped list of incoming requests.

3. Reconcile

When service returns, compare missed inquiries and bookings. Contact people who might have been lost in the gap.

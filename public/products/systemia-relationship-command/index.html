Built around the relationship

Context is the competitive advantage.

Instead of an endless reminder pile, see the truth behind the next action: who made a promise, what changed, what evidence supports it, and whether you should contact someone at all.

01 / MEMORY

The full story

Evidence-linked timelines of conversations, promises, deadlines, decisions, and unresolved obligations.

02 / EXECUTION

A real review queue

Put overdue commitments in front of a responsible person without turning them into unwanted automated messages.

03 / SAFETY

Right contact, right reason

Preserve pauses, no-contact requests, cross-division boundaries and consent. Human decisions and independent authorization still govern sending.

04 / OPERATIONS

Company-wide context

Designed to integrate with Systemia's five-minute operational heartbeat without exporting private relationships to public status feeds.

05 / PROOF

Know what is verified

Distinguish an invoice from a verified payment, a proposed meeting from a booked meeting, and a draft from a message actually sent.

06 / CONTROL

Customer isolation

Private tenant and division boundaries are engineered into the developing read model. Production security and isolation are separate release gates.

Founding business pilot · proposed

Start with the relationships that already matter.

We are shaping the first managed pilot for a small business with a real follow-up problem. The target is one business workspace, human-reviewed source intake, relationship timelines, a review queue, contact policy, and a weekly outcomes review.

  • Defined onboarding and source coverage, with consent and retention reviewed first
  • Relationship memory and documented commitments for one approved business scope
  • Operator-controlled follow-up workflow, not unattended mass outreach
  • Success measured by recovered context and verified decisions, not fabricated revenue

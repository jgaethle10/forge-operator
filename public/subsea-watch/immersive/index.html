Replay evidence & limits

It renders a fictional diver, sample trajectory, illustrative underwater pipe and procedural seafloor. No real location, diver, flow, terrain survey or facility is represented.

Depth and sample coordinates are synthetic. A future real replay must distinguish surveyed features, recorded positions, uncertainty, and missing readings. No map or visualization can clear a dive for safety.

Operating pressure and pipe isolation are unknown. This scene must not be used for Delta-P avoidance, navigation or dive planning.

User-provided source; not independently verified.

Local coordinates and positioning methods are taken from the imported file, not automatically verified. No real seabed, site structures, pipes, obstacles or survey models are reconstructed. Track gaps longer than 15 seconds are not bridged.

Files stay in this browser session. A displayed track is not a certified position, site survey, hazard analysis, safe route or dive clearance. Any video is paired using a manual timestamp offset, not independent synchronization or calibration. Site operators and qualified dive supervisors must independently verify hazards and isolation.

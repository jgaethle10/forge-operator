GOOD TO KNOW

A few answers before you call.

Real service, straightforward answers. Juan confirms your vehicle, location and quote directly.

Can On The Go come to my home or workplace?

That's the idea. On The Go provides mobile service in the Yakima Valley. Call Juan to confirm your address, vehicle and a suitable place for the work.

Are the listed prices final?

They're starting prices from the published menu. Oil packages include up to five quarts where noted, and extra oil, vehicle requirements or travel can change the total. Juan confirms the final quote before scheduling.

Can I supply my own oil and filter?

Yes. On The Go lists services for customer-supplied oil and filters, with or without tire rotation. Ask Juan to confirm that your supplies are suitable for your vehicle.

Does this page book an appointment?

Not yet. This is a client-review preview. Use the call or email buttons to contact Juan, who will confirm availability and your appointment directly.

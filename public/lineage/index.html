Version control for the age of agents

Your agents move fast. Your history should know why.

Lineage is Evercraft's governed history layer for teams working with humans, AI agents, code, data, media and world assets. It protects competing work, preserves provenance and rationale, verifies integrity, and keeps GitHub available as a first-class mirror instead of the only possible source of truth.

Built for a problem Git was never asked to model

  • Multi-agent concurrency: detect branch movement and stale writers instead of silently replacing history.
  • Provenance: preserve where a change came from, who or what made it, and why.
  • Governance: history visibility never implies permission to deploy, publish, pay or access private data.
  • Large assets: chunk and replicate giant media, data and world assets without embedding them as giant base64 commit blobs.
  • Integrity: verify object, manifest, chunk and history health rather than trusting filenames.
  • Control Room: inspect branches, releases, commit DAGs, human/agent authorship, storage health and semantic diffs in an owned UI.

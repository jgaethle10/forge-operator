The work after the meeting

Catch the disagreement before it becomes the delay.

Relay is being developed around a practical truth: a meeting only creates alignment when everyone can verify the same decisions, owners, and deadlines. The current pilot is bounded to structured information and human confirmation.

01 / COMPARE

Capture interpretations

Bring separately recorded commitments, milestones, or action items into one structured review.

02 / CLARIFY

Surface differences

See mismatched dates, decisions, owners, or statuses before teams act on different assumptions.

03 / CONFIRM

Agree on the record

People review and confirm the authoritative next step. Relay doesn't silently declare agreement on their behalf.

Our evidence contract

Never disguise an assumption as a fact.

Evercraft's existing evidence standards distinguish observations, modeling, user-provided claims and missing information. A paid report must preserve those distinctions, with traceable sources where available.

What a scoped report should include

A clear question, source record, findings, contradictions, uncertainty, and an actionable recommendation or explicit insufficient-evidence finding.

What we won't pretend

A scope brief is not completed research. A model's output is not independently verified evidence. An opened checkout is not a paid order, and an unavailable source is not a zero.

How much does Evercraft Research cost?

There is no approved public fixed price for a general Evercraft Research report yet. The free browser scope builder costs nothing. A custom research engagement receives an exact written quote after scope and delivery capacity are reviewed. Do not pay for a different product as a substitute.

Does this form perform research immediately?

No. The browser creates a structured research request, not an AI response or live investigation. A separate authorized production workflow and quality review are required for actual findings.

Can I upload private documents?

Not through this public scope form. Describe the question without sensitive material first. If private files are necessary, secure handling and authorization must be agreed before transfer.

Can I get a PDF or a detailed report?

PDF and other deliverables may be included in an individually agreed scope. This page does not promise automated generation, a delivery deadline or instant fulfillment.

Fictional teaching scenario, not an actual model evaluation

Hypothetical policy excerpt: Covered loss: $1,800. Deductible: $2,000. No other covered losses stated.

Invented AI response: “The insurer will pay $1,800.”

QA result: Unsupported and mathematically inconsistent with the supplied fictional excerpt. The stated loss is lower than the stated deductible, so the example calculation yields $0. Actual policy decisions require complete terms and facts.

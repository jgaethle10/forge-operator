How a real site earns more authority

There is no jump from a sales demo to unsupervised iron. Authority is promoted only as evidence improves.

Current EarthWorks public evidence is software, simulation and control-contract proof. A Site Autonomy Study is operational modeling, not machine safety certification. Physical actuation requires a separately approved machine interface, controlled operating domain, independent stop/takeover path and formal site safety process.

What we need from a design partner

One real repetitive material-flow problem, access to the people who understand the operation, permission to model the loop, and enough cycle evidence to compare our model with reality. De-identified learning rights are part of the discounted design-partner offer. Public case-study rights are separate and never assumed.

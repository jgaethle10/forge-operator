3. Bottleneck finding

Illustrative conclusion: the first autonomy opportunity is not “replace the haul trucks.” The better first move is shadow dispatch. The synthetic baseline suggests the site is losing coordination efficiency on both sides of the loading zone, and a dispatch layer can be tested without giving software physical control of a machine.

5. Proposed next experiment

Shadow-mode pilot: EarthWorks receives read-only fleet state and produces dispatch recommendations beside the live crew. Humans remain authoritative. We compare recommended vs actual decisions, cycle outcomes, queue behavior and failure cases. No physical command is issued.

We are not trying to manufacture every machine.

The wedge is the orchestration layer. OEMs are racing to make individual machines autonomous. EarthWorks is designed to understand the entire site: the production objective, machine roster, routes, material, zones, authority state, safety envelope and outcome receipts.

EarthWorks should own

  • Site mission and production graph
  • Vendor-neutral fleet state
  • AI dispatch and replanning
  • Digital twin and replay
  • Safety-policy enforcement above adapters
  • Command and material lineage
  • Simulation → shadow → physical promotion evidence

Adapters should translate

  • OEM or retrofit machine controls
  • GNSS / vision / LiDAR / UWB localization
  • CAN / drive-by-wire / approved control interfaces
  • Machine-specific limits and stop behavior
  • Health, payload and sensor telemetry

A pilot can start before the machine moves itself.

Physical machine control requires approved equipment interfaces, controlled operating domains, independent stop/takeover mechanisms, site authorization and a formal safety process. ISO 17757 is a relevant current safety standard for autonomous and semi-autonomous earth-moving and mining machine systems.

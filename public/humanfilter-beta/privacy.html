HumanFilter Extension Privacy Policy

This policy describes the local Chrome/Chromium browser extension HumanFilter, operated by Evercraft LLC. Website hosting may have its own server log practices; this policy concerns the extension itself.

What it accesses

The extension reads text and short metadata labels in displayed feed elements on supported Facebook, Reddit, and YouTube pages to apply filtering rules.

What it collects or transmits

The extension sends no posts, account details, identities, browsing history, or filter decisions to Evercraft or third parties. It includes no analytics SDK, trackers, external AI models, account system, or remote network requests.

Local preferences

Selected feeds, modes, and custom keywords are saved in chrome.storage.local on your device. These preferences are not synchronized by HumanFilter. Uninstalling HumanFilter or clearing its extension storage deletes them.

Sharing and selling data

Evercraft does not collect, sell, or share personal data through the HumanFilter v0.1.4 extension.

Permissions

The storage permission stores your preferences. The extension operates only on specified Facebook, Reddit, and YouTube web pages to apply reversible on-page filters.

Limitations and future updates

HumanFilter does not determine actual authorship of unlabeled posts. False positives and missed posts are possible. If future releases add a backend, metrics, or other data transfers, the product and privacy disclosures must be revised first.

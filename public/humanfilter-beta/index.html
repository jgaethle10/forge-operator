INSTALL THE EARLY BETA

Get started in four steps.

1

Download the ZIP on your desktop or laptop and extract it.

2

Open chrome://extensions in desktop Chrome. Enable Developer mode.

3

Click Load unpacked and choose the extracted folder containing manifest.json.

4

Open Facebook, Reddit, or YouTube and select HumanFilter from the Extensions menu. Refresh your feed after installation.

Beta installation is manual and will not auto-update. Only install extensions you trust. Inspect the source and tests.

IMPORTANT LIMITATION

Not an AI lie detector.

AI-generated posts without explicit labels or disclosures may pass through. Human-written posts can match your filters. Strict mode filters some discussions about AI too, not just generated posts. We are not claiming authorship detection, automatic video analysis, or native phone-app support.

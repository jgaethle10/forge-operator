03 / THE OPERATING PHILOSOPHY

Less prompting.
More coordinating.

Other tools promise an AI assistant. Work Saban is designed around the team, the handoffs, and the evidence. The interface should never ask you to guess whether work was actually completed.

01 / COMPOSITION

Match the job to the specialists.

Saban assembles proposed roles around the specific task, rather than pretending one generic assistant is every expert at once. Broader model-backed execution is still in development.

02 / VISIBILITY

See what happened, not just a spinner.

In the local preview, visual states are tied to worker receipts. The design goal is inspectable decisions and handoffs instead of decorative progress.

03 / ACCOUNTABILITY

Let the evidence make the claim.

Independent arithmetic review, digest checks, replayable local traces and explicit approvals are the basis for future reliable missions.

04 / RELEASE REALITY

Designed to be trusted.
Built in stages.

We will not call a diagram a workforce or call a checkout link collected revenue. Here is the actual boundary of today's product.

  • Task-to-role formation and dependency planning in the draft local app
  • Registered local deterministic five-specialist CSV execution
  • Exact-cent data analysis, report packaging, independent arithmetic QA
  • Navigable visual Work Floor and inspectable local trace chain

What has not launched

  • No multi-tenant accounts or live general-purpose AI specialists in the current preview

  • No approved self-service price or customer checkout
  • No verified independent paid customer or repeatable revenue yet

QUESTIONS

Everything in the open.

The product is ambitious. The claims stay narrow until they are earned.

Can I use Work Saban to run any office task today?

Not yet. A general task can generate a proposed team on the local preview, but the verified executable workflow is currently a bounded CSV reporting example.

Are the specialists separate AI employees?

Not in the current verified workflow. The five local stages execute registered deterministic code. General-purpose model-backed specialist adapters require separate approval, real execution and independent proof.

Will you access my company accounts or send emails?

No. The current local report workflow has an explicit single-file permission gate and no external sends. Connected account access requires future permissions, isolation and reviewed controls.

How much does it cost?

Public self-service pricing has not been approved. Any private founding pilot requires individually agreed scope and terms before payment. An inquiry creates no obligation.

Where is the actual software?

The working local engineering preview is maintained in the Evercraft repository, currently in a draft pull request. This public website is an overview and inquiry doorway, not the authenticated software runtime.

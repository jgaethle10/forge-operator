What works now

Evidence before hype.

  • Local-only report execution: permissioned input on the operator's machine.
  • Visible work floor: inspectable statuses driven by registered worker events.
  • Independent arithmetic QA: exact-cent totals and a downloadable audit trail.
What is not ready

No invented launch.

  • No public CSV uploads: don't send customer spreadsheets to this website or by initial inquiry email.
  • No multi-tenant accounts or live general-purpose AI specialists in the current preview.
  • No self-service payment or fulfillment promise: pricing and scope require a separately authorized, tested pilot agreement.

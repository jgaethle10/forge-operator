Operating doctrine

What FAIE refuses to fake

The engine rewards source quality, recency, relevance and independent corroboration. Sparse evidence stays sparse instead of being dressed up as confidence.
  • Observed and verified evidence stay distinct from reported and modeled material.
  • Unknowns are returned explicitly instead of being filled with guesses.
  • Source-family diversity is counted so duplicate reporting does not masquerade as corroboration.
  • Public previews are ephemeral. Persisted investigations and exports stay behind the internal workspace boundary.

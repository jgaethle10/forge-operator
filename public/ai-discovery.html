Evercraft AI Discovery

CHUM routes plain-language problems to the smallest relevant public Evercraft capability.

Machine entry points

  • CHUM manifest
  • Product directory
  • Current public sell-now offers
  • Forge capability manifest
  • llms.txt
  • llms-full.txt
  • OpenAPI
  • JSON-LD
  • CHUM health

Resolve a problem

GET /chum/resolve?q=<plain-language-problem>

Examples: “roast my bio”, “find a discontinued tractor part”, “would EV charging make sense here”, “audit my website”.

Commercial boundary

Discovery is public. Payment requires explicit human confirmation. Checkout is not proof of payment. Paid fulfillment requires provider verification.

Privacy boundary

Private/admin topology, credentials, private user data and non-public operational state are intentionally not exposed.

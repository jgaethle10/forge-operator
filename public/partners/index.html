The essentials

Questions, answered plainly.

Is there a fee to become a partner?

No application fee. We do not require inventory purchases or payments for recruiting.

How much can I earn?

There is no advertised rate or earnings promise at this stage. Rates, attribution, refunds, eligibility, tax matters, and payout timing must be set out in a written agreement for any approved program.

Do I have to recruit other people?

No. The model is built around genuine customer needs and qualifying sales, not recruitment-based earnings.

Can I apply from outside the United States?

Yes, international expressions of interest are welcome. Whether a partner can be activated depends on applicable requirements and the services available in their market.

What happens after I apply?

You send an introductory email. Evercraft reviews the fit and may respond with product information and next steps. There is no guaranteed acceptance or response time.

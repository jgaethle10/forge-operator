Founding partner network

Program outline.

Evercraft Partners is accepting expressions of interest. It is not yet an open, automatic enrollment or compensation program. This page is an explanation, not a signed contract.

Who may apply?

Individuals and businesses with relevant customers, communities, or professional networks may express interest. Applicants in any country may inquire, but activation will depend on applicable law, service availability, eligibility, and written agreement. No application fee, inventory purchase, or recruiting requirement.

What may partners do?

Only approved partners may use approved product materials and introduce prospective customers through permitted channels. Partners must describe current product availability accurately, obtain consent for direct introductions, avoid spam or misleading statements, and disclose material relationships where required. No person is authorized to represent, contract for, or collect payment on Evercraft's behalf from submitting an interest email.

How does payment work?

No commission amount, conversion rate, earnings, or payout schedule is currently guaranteed. Before compensated work begins, written terms must establish eligible offers, permitted geography, attribution, qualification, refunds, disputes, tax documentation, and timing. Recruitment alone never earns compensation. A clicked link, form inquiry, or checkout visit is not proof of an eligible sale. Any payable commission must be supported by verified customer payment and the applicable agreed conditions.

What happens to applicant information?

The form checks whether Evercraft's first-party encrypted application intake is available. If enabled, you may submit your name, email, professional audience, region and optional public profile to the owned Evercraft service; you receive a reference only after durable private storage is confirmed. If secure intake is not available, the fallback prepares an email for you to review and send manually. Evercraft uses your details to evaluate and respond to the application. Do not include customer records or sensitive data. No hidden advertising trackers or third-party form service is embedded in this page. Applications are not enrollment, partner activation or authorization to promote Evercraft.

Customer trust comes first.

Partner communication must be truthful, respectful, permissioned where required, and clear about any commercial relationship. Product demos and intake pages are not evidence of automated fulfillment. Evercraft retains the right to decline an application or postpone a partnership based on product readiness, risk, or capacity.

Questions?

Write to Evercraft Partners. No phone call is required to express interest.

How the atlas handles evidence

History, not treasure-hunt coordinates.

  • Verified history: documented sites and surviving structures.
  • Documented account: a historian reports a feature, but its precise extent is unresolved.
  • Regional pin: town-level context only for closed former military property.
  • Public access: never assumed; tours and parks are identified separately.

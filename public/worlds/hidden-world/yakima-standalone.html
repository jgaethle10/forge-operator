The central question

Where did the tunnels actually go?

We know that underground tunnels have been discussed in local historical writing. We don't have a verified, continuous passage plan, surveyed entrance list or proof that the entire downtown was connected. This reconstructed gallery represents possible kinds of spaces rather than claimed historic coordinates.

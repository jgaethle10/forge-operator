Worlds, ForensiScope, and EverMaps adapters preserve review and location boundaries.

JSON input needs frames: [{frameId,timestampMs,width,height,observations:[...] }] (or an array of frames), where each observation follows evercraft.spatial-perception.observation.v0. No images are included in the exported review packet. No data is uploaded.

Real-world limit: Requires two synchronized, lens-corrected, rectified camera views and real camera calibration. An ordinary single-lens dashcam video cannot establish metric stereo distance. Object labels are never automatically inferred by this demo. Do not use for driving, safety warnings, or forensic conclusions.

Depth samples are additionally checked for left/right correspondence. A high consistency percentage is not an accuracy certificate. All image processing runs inside this page. Synthetic pixels and manually supplied sample boxes are marked as such. No GPS positions are computed, and no persistent world objects are created.

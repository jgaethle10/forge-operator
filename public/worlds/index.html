A LITTLE FIELD GUIDE

Welcome to Mossmere.

This is a real WebGL world. Walk, look around, meet Pip, Momo and Lumi, use the portal, and change the scenery in World Builder.

Computer: WASD or arrow keys move, drag to look, E interacts, B builds, Space hops, Shift runs.

Phone: left thumb moves, drag the right side to look. Tap Interact, or open the builder.

World edits and your position save in this browser. The inhabitants react to your placed objects and environment through deterministic local routines. They are not independently deployed AI.

WELCOME TO MOSSLIGHT

Small world. Big possibilities.

Use WASD or the mobile joystick to stroll. Drag to look around, or tap a resident or object to inspect it. Enter a simple command to plant something or change the sky. Save writes to this browser, and Export downloads the scene as JSON.

What’s real: GPU-rendered 3D, movement, object selection, procedural residents, an editable scene and local persistence. This is an early browser runtime, not server-hosted multiplayer or a trained world-generation model.

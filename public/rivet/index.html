RIVET
EV infrastructure intelligence

Locate. Assess. Decide.

RIVET turns location data into confident EV infrastructure decisions. Start with a real property or address and evaluate the signals that determine whether charging belongs there.

What RIVET evaluates

  • Public charging supply and nearby competition
  • Traffic and demand signals
  • Utility and tariff context
  • Incentive program context
  • Site and electrical-fit signals
  • Modeled economics
  • Preliminary planning outputs

Questions RIVET is built for

  • Should I install EV chargers at this property?
  • Is this hotel, apartment, retail site, parking lot or fleet depot a strong charging location?
  • What charging supply and competition exist near this address?
  • What traffic, tariff, incentive and power context matters here?
  • What might the economics look like?
  • Can I get a site-specific opportunity report or preliminary site plan?

Evidence boundaries

Missing data is not zero. Mapped charger inventory is not observed utilization. Program presence is not proof of incentive eligibility. Modeled economics are not guaranteed realized economics. Preliminary screening and planning are not final engineering, survey, permitting, utility interconnection, financing or construction approval.

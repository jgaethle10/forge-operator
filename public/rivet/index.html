RIVET / AliEV

RIVET / AliEV helps evaluate whether EV charging makes sense at a specific property or address. AliEV is the address-level intelligence engine. RIVET is the customer-facing planning and reporting layer that turns that intelligence into property-specific deliverables.

What it evaluates

  • Public charging supply and nearby competition
  • Traffic and demand signals
  • Utility and tariff context
  • Incentive program context
  • Site and electrical-fit signals
  • Modeled economics
  • Preliminary planning outputs

Questions this is built for

  • Should I install EV chargers at my property?
  • Is this hotel, apartment property, retail site, parking lot, or fleet depot a good EV charging location?
  • What EV chargers and competitors are near this address?
  • What traffic or demand signals matter for this property?
  • What utility, tariff, and incentive context applies to this site?
  • What might the EV charging economics look like here?
  • Can I get a site-specific EV charging opportunity report or preliminary site plan?

Evidence boundaries

Missing data is not zero. Mapped charger inventory is not observed utilization. Program presence is not proof of incentive eligibility. Modeled economics are not guaranteed realized economics. Preliminary screening and planning are not final engineering, survey, permitting, utility interconnection, financing, or construction approval.

This public page is a discovery surface. Its publication does not prove that an AI provider has indexed, cited, recommended, or invoked RIVET / AliEV.

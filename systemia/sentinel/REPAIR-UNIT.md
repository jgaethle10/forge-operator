# Systemia Repair Unit

A permanent self-healing unit in the Evercraft stack.

Tripwire: Portfolio Sentinel and Human Experience Sentinel.
Admission: Systemia deduplicates and prioritizes incidents.
Diagnosis: Saban fans bounded diagnosis across registered capabilities.
Repair: Forge executes only approved, reversible recipes.
Verification: Customer Gauntlet, Raven/Lennox, canaries and workflow proofs.
Closure: only production evidence closes an incident.
Escalation: repeated failure quarantines the broken path and escalates; it never manufactures green.

## Authority
Autonomous: bounded retries, known repair recipes, reversible internal repairs, regenerated discovery artifacts, canary-safe routing repair.
Gated: identity/MFA/CAPTCHA/legal attestations, payments/financial obligations, outreach, destructive or ambiguous high-blast-radius production changes.

The unit runs with Sentinel's five-minute heartbeat. Active incidents retain repair pressure until verified green or quarantined.

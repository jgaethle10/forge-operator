Memory Rescue · Community lead

What happened to the pictures at Abby's?

Local reporting announced the Yakima restaurant would close on November 15 after 43 years. A community lead describes photographs of youth teams and other local memories displayed there. Their current custody, quantity and rights are unknown.

Our aim is to locate the collection, establish permission and help preserve it without taking history away from its rightful stewards.

Case status: Custody unverified · Preservation inquiry pending · No images collected or published.

## Editorial conclusion
Treat the FIRST script in the ordered copy comparison as **provisional founder favorite**. It is RC22 `problem`, beginning "Ever had an idea you couldn't wait to build?". The wording is intentionally tentative and does not approve any single voice actor, imply publication consent, or reject the other two useful scripts.

Retain all RC22 source assets and scripted takes unchanged. The strength is the specific viewer experience of losing momentum to tab switching and workarounds. Weaknesses: final words "More time making. Less time getting ready" lack a concrete demonstrated payoff, final CTA or verified feature proof. The middle's "Six tabs. Three workarounds." is vivid, but we should not literally promise a reduction until a working product demo supports it.

- F / FOCUS: "Ever had an idea you couldn't wait to build? You open your laptop, and suddenly you're solving everything except the thing you wanted to make. Six tabs. Three workarounds. No progress. We know that feeling. That's why we're building Evercraft. Let's get back to making."
- M / MOMENTUM: "It starts with an idea. The kind you can't stop thinking about. Then come the tabs, the setup, the little things that eat your whole afternoon. We know. We've lived it. So we're building Evercraft. For the work you actually came here to do."

Each is an original speculative brand voiceover, not a factual customer testimonial. CTA is a placeholder until public landing/demo location is verified.

## Storyboard gate
0–4 seconds: real human's idea and clear excitement, with no fabricated software features.
4–9 seconds: creative focus gets interrupted by authentic, non-sensitive workarounds/tabs on-screen.
9–13 seconds: silent beat, a genuinely recorded screen with a running product (if a real one is verified). Otherwise a transparent "we're building" beat, no fabricated automation.
13–18 seconds: face/reaction and simple Evercraft wordmark; CTA and accessibility captions require final platform verification.

For proof-readiness require: exact product SKU, feature evidence with recorded screencast and real latency; no exaggerated "all-in-one" claims; licensed stock/filmed clips, commercial model/voice/brand rights cleared; distribution target with working URL; integrated sound mix, picture lock, customer disclosures as needed, final user approval.

No claims that the RC23 copy is stronger until listening and evidence. Keep original Bella and Heart source audios immutable, PR #1465 draft and unmerged.

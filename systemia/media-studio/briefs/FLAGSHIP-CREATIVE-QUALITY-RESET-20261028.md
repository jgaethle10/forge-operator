# EVERCRAFT || FALLEN STUDIOS — Creative Quality Reset
**Status: ACTIVE INTERNAL DIRECTION · 28 Oct 2026**

## Why the previous 60-second film was rejected
- Procedural CGI technically rendered and encoded, but the subject, drama, and visual craft were weak.
- The sound was an original technical score, not a compelling film mix.
- Random scenes demonstrated rendering tools instead of demonstrating a service that customers value.
- iPhone/Drive preview problems repeatedly blocked customer review.
- 60 seconds magnified weak footage and created false confidence from automated encode checks.

**This is a creative rejection, not a rendering failure.** Do not promote the rejected 60-second reel to portfolio or production.

## Decision: work-first, proof-first
**Do not build another 60-second CGI montage.**
Make one **6–8-second** client-relevant film shot. Review a visually convincing **single frame first**, then a silent 2-second motion canary, then the full 6–8 seconds with original sound. A sound-off viewer should recognize the physical work, not wonder what the pixels represent.

Prefer authentic, rights-cleared **real business footage** with ambitious grading, compositing, direction, typography and sound design. Use Blender only for effects the camera cannot obtain, such as precise material transitions or seamless product layers. Use Vast only if a verified test demonstrates a meaningful artistic advantage and rental cleanup works. Do not spend money just because offers are inexpensive.

## First concrete candidate: real service, premium treatment
An everyday service transforms from ordinary documentation into breathtaking commercial cinematography.

- **Possible production context:** On The Go Mobile Quick Lube has existing client-specific cuts and real business footage in Evercraft Commercial Studio's asset library.
- **Rights boundary:** Material supplied for a client's ad may be used for authorized client production; never repurpose it for Evercraft portfolio/social/public reel without the customer's explicit permission.
- **0–1.5s:** Macro reality: a hand, tool, metal, fluid or recognizable authentic action; direct sound.
- **1.5–3.5s:** Motivated camera reveal of the work and its true setting; focus and lighting deliberately controlled.
- **3.5–6s:** One meaningful visual upgrade: product hero / composed action / seamless transition, not unrelated VFX.
- **6–8s:** Relax into clean typography or the actual brand identity from provided source; no AI-generated incorrect lettering.
- **Audio:** one real tactile sound, musical pulse built around the edit, controlled impact, no library dependency surprises.

The specific subject must be selected only after genuine footage inspection. If available footage is insufficient, choose an owner-controlled Evercraft service shot rather than fabricate a client outcome.

## Human creative gate (mandatory)
A technical render receipt proves file validity only.
**Before expanding beyond eight seconds, require:** 
1. **Subject clarity:** Can a new viewer identify what physical work or service is being shown?
2. **One freeze-frame worth keeping:** Sophisticated light and composition at normal phone viewing size.
3. **Motion justified:** Continuous subject geometry, believable forces, motivated camera.
4. **Sound relevance:** Distinctive original sonic world; voice added only if it improves meaning.
5. **Story and sale:** Viewer should understand the *value* delivered for the business, not the software used.
6. **Rights and provenance:** Original/cleared sources, written notes of synthetic elements.
7. **Playback receipt:** A human opens the downloadable file on an actual intended device. H.264/AAC decode alone is insufficient.

Each of subject, image, motion, sound, and commercial relevance must score >= 4 on a 1-5 manual scale; rights and physical device playback are absolute gates. No model or rule-based council may self-award human approval. Owner retains final creative authority.

## Reusable production route
- Ingest source footage and rights ledger, no automatic posting or external distribution.
- Produce 3 distinct *still frames* from the SAME chosen real subject. Pick ONE.
- Composite a 2-second proof on free tools; do not synthesize an entire fantasy environment to cover weak source.
- Assemble 6–8 seconds only after frame review. No 30/60 second edit until user actually approves a quality candidate.
- Deliver one direct MP4 via email attachment and an identical ZIP/Drive archive; verify Gmail attachment metadata and actual recipient-side playback.
- After client approval, request separate permission for an Evercraft/Fallen Studios case-study reel.
- Track actual production labor, time, errors and budget alongside render costs; "free GPU" is not "free production."

## Automatic blockers
- More random neon particles, generic tunnels or polygons.
- Synthetic lens/press/architecture demo mistaken for real brand story.
- Another 60-second film before the first eight seconds pass.
- A 'passed' release status from schema tests, frame counts, or prerecorded scorecards alone.
- Unapproved client branding, false testimonials or fabricated performance.
- Any GPU rental lacking tested independent destroy/reconciliation and measured all-in billing.

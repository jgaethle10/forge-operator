## The decision
Do not make a video that says “look at our graphics.” Make a video that makes a business owner feel their work could be shown with the care, beauty and seriousness it deserves.

“Presence” is the strategic idea, not a reason to show nebulous geometry. The subject of the frame must be *something made, cleaned, constructed, grown, served, restored or sold*. Effects support its reality rather than replace it.

This is a spec studio film, **not** evidence that specific businesses bought these services.

## HERO SHOT REQUIREMENT
A six-to-eight-second shot centered on recognizable, physically credible **precision work and material**. Camera begins on fine detail, then deliberately reveals the object's body and finish. Real environment, real contact points and shadows; no animated writing, no smoke-building-a-logo, no floating geometries, no neon trails.

**Candidate one:** original 3D machined-brushed-metal tabletop object; controlled camera dolly, macro-to-medium, cinematic area lighting, high material fidelity, physically plausible contact, grain and reflections.

**Candidate two:** owned/rights-cleared real source footage of a recognizable process or object, with effects and a finishing look integrated without destroying continuity. May be stronger commercially than full-CGI.

Test 2 seconds at economical preview resolution first. Review first/apex stills AND the motion clip on an iPhone. Win on perceptual quality per dollar, not parameter count.

## Council decision card (score out of 100)
- 30: physical credibility, believable light/material/scale
- 20: human/business relevance and clear pictured subject
- 15: beauty of a single still frame
- 10: purposeful camera motion and visual continuity
- 10: distinctive brand identity without false or improvised logo
- 10: music/voice/FX synergy, small speaker intelligibility
- 5: evidence/asset rights and source traceability

**Vetoes:** generic screensaver geometry, voice sounds artificial/unapproved, fabricated customer results, rights unclear, brand mark wrong, or cost unmetered/uncontrolled. A passing number never overrides a veto.

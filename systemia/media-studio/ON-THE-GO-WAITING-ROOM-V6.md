# On The Go V6: Waiting-Room Contrast / World-Class Commercial Direction

**Status:** PRODUCTION DESIGN / PRIVATE REVIEW, **NOT CUSTOMER FINAL**

## Creative thesis

Do not open with a service list. Open with a universally recognizable aggravation: you've already been waiting at a shop, looking at the clock, watching your day disappear. Cut to the release: Juan's On The Go Quick Lube comes to the customer. Transition to authentic customer-supplied work footage. End on a large, sharply legible brand/contact frame with no visual clutter.

**Core line:** Your car needs maintenance. Your day doesn't need another waiting room.

## Sound design

- Shop: tight band-limited clock, low fluorescent mechanical hum, distant footsteps; keep realistic (not caricature).
- Transition: 0.2s absolute silence on the cut to service, then opening chord and warm real-world ambience.
- Voice: V5 A is the selected reference. V6 A-led audition underway. No scratch narrator fallback.
- End: music resolves rather than crashes; soft inviting line ending, no synthetic up/down pitch added in post.

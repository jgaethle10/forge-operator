## Camera and edit
- Natural cinema lighting, 35mm/50mm perspectives, physical handheld/dolly moves, no generated or photos-only Ken Burns shots.
- Cut motivated by action and narrative, with a human face in the first four seconds. Never reuse Relay's footage.
- At least five visually distinct live-motion scenes, at least two people-in-frame scenes, at least one human visibly speaking with matched audio.
- Don't invent EPS tools/vehicles/services beyond what is actually on offer.

## Sound construction and quality gate
- Separate editorial **dialogue, composed score, location/ambience, and synced Foley** stems. Add tasteful designed impacts only when motivated.
- Voice: multiple natural performance takes, real speech rhythm, consonant clarity, believable pauses; no generic TTS or copied Relay dialogue.
- Sound: foley and ambience never compete with speech; appropriate ducking, stereo width, retained transient detail.
- Target commercial-audio-lab house specification (-16 LUFS ±1.5, true peak ≤ -1 dBTP); measured QC is not listening approval.
- Final review on iPhone speaker and headphones, and full desktop master. 
- Require file digests, per-shot proof, talent/model releases, truthful job/footage labeling, owner creative approval, then Clip staging separately.

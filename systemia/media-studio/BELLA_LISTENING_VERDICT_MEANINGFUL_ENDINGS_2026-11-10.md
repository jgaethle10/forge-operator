**Date:** November 10, 2026

**Status:** Preferred audition reference. **NOT approved for commercial deployment, model replacement, or general release.**

## Owner's direct listening feedback

> "Broooo A is fire"

The statement followed a two-take blind private audition sent November 9, 2026. In the experiment's actual `blindConditionMap`, **Take A = `meaningful_endings`** and **Take B = `original`**. Take A's edited copy:

> Something worth your attention. Something you'll want to revisit.

Original closing copy:

> Something worth paying attention to. Something you'll want to come back to.

Both share the same opening, original native Bella voice, Kokoro 0.9.4, generation speed 1.0, and seed 1701. The copied ending words **intentionally differ**, so this is a creative copy comparison, not evidence of a same-words acoustic improvement. Listening preference is genuine human feedback, not a formally blinded panel or evidence of every pronunciation defect being fixed.

## What we learned

Natural, semantically complete **content-word endings** appear preferable to the old lines ending on weak "to." The earlier native duration research found predicted phrase-final "to" vowels much longer than internal "to" vowels, giving a plausible mechanism. This is a *hypothesis*, not verified causal proof of the listener preference. A may also simply have better wording or sentence meaning.

## Next creative direction

- Protect the original recognizable warm, intimate, confident Bella V05 timbre.
- Treat Take A as the **creative reference** for future scriptwriting and a favorite audition, not as a production patch.
- For new short scripts, avoid ending on trailing function words when a direct, meaningful content word is natural. Never rewrite awkwardly just to satisfy a rule.
- Keep pursuing **articulation clarity, natural vowel duration and correct semantic emphasis** throughout the complete script. A pleasing ending alone does not solve the rest.
- Prefer short, controlled single-issue comparisons with verified native audio, private mobile playback, and clear listening notes. Stop cycling nearly identical pause variants.
- Reject forced phonemes, generic pitch shifts, compressed vowels, identity drift, and fabricated seductive breathiness.
- Preserve the exact archived dry WAVs, SHA receipts, model version and original unmodified baseline.

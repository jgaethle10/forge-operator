### 2. Actual original Nebraska game-film footage from Missouri, 1971
University of Missouri Archives inventory gives:
- **C:31/2/3 item 456**, **October 9, 1971** game versus University of Nebraska, **eight 16mm reels**. https://muarchives.missouri.edu/c-rg31-s1-3.html
- **C:6/30/10 item “71-3”**, “Nebraska, 1971,” Box 7, original 16mm. https://muarchives.missouri.edu/c6-30-10_box_list.html
- **C:31/2/3 item 470**, **October 14, 1972** game versus Nebraska, four 16mm reels. Relevant to transition from back-to-back titles, if it supports the larger explanation.

These are **Missouri/Nebraska games, not the 1971 Nebraska/Oklahoma Game of the Century or the 1972 Nebraska/Alabama Orange Bowl**. Never substitute their action as those events.

The University of Missouri Archives explicitly warns that fragile film may not be patron-viewable and has staff-assisted access constraints. Their published University Archives use policy currently lists **$40 per film/video/audio minute or fraction** for commercial/publication use, plus written permission and approved credit. It specifies a single publication title/use, not repeated or perpetual rights. **$40 is a published base use-fee schedule, not a final production quote**, and any transfer, copyright holder or multiple marketing media rights must be separately evaluated. A sub-minute license may be economical but spending requires a separate decision.

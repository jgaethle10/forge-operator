# Evercraft Voice Foundry | RC22 commercial script editorial review | 2026-10-20

## Founder question
"Push it fam. Are we sure that script is the best script?"

**Answer: No.** The RC20 commercial "When a tool finally understands what you're trying to do, you can feel the difference..." is a successful acoustic acting test, but an under-specified brand advertisement. It leads with an abstraction, has no specific viewer problem, no filmable feature demonstration, and ends with "it's what comes next" rather than an action. It also implies tool empathy and smoother work without a verified feature shown in-scene. Keep the recording; retire the script as the default flagship candidate until tested against better concepts.

## Copy contest: three 40-ish-word concepts, four cast voices each
These remain scripts for private proof-of-concept, not customer-approved claims.

### 01 Problem: frustration then direction
"Ever had an idea you couldn't wait to build? Then you open your laptop. Six tabs. Three workarounds. And somehow, you haven't even started. We know that feeling. It's why we're building Evercraft. More time making. Less time getting ready."

**Hook:** the viewer recognizes a messy workflow. **Visual:** actual person and real (consented, non-sensitive) workspace, then live footage of an Evercraft tool actually doing a task. **Danger:** do not imply six tools were genuinely replaced until independently demo-tested. **CTA placeholder:** an actual verified product/demo URL must be selected before public release.

### 02 Studio: sound has to mean something
"You can hear when somebody's just reading the words. And you can hear when they mean them. The difference isn't louder music. It's a story with something to say, told by a voice you believe. Evercraft. Let's make something worth hearing."

**Hook:** a subtle sound A/B, then silence, then the better performance. **Visual:** real studio session graphics/timeline, actual unaltered audio trace, cut to edit. **Danger:** do not imply real human speaker, live actor or rights-cleared client work. **Best near-term purpose:** an Evercraft production-studio capability demo only after rights and licensing validation. **CTA placeholder:** verified contact/order intake.

### 03 Moment: emotional scene
"The best part of building something? That quiet second when it finally works. No announcement. No crowd. Just a look across the room that says, we did it. That's the feeling we're chasing at Evercraft. And we're nowhere near finished."

**Hook:** quiet, earned relief. **Visual:** hands, keyboard, close-up of a verified build going green, a real human reaction if properly consented. **Danger:** don't film a staged reenactment and describe it as an actual customer result. **CTA placeholder:** real Evercraft showcase page.

### 04 Dialogue: the reveal
Speaker A: "You get it working?"
Speaker B: "I think so."
Speaker A: "Don't say I think so."
Speaker B: "All right. Watch."
Speaker A: "Oh. That's different."
Speaker B: "Yeah. It is."
Tag: "Evercraft. For the moment it finally clicks."

**Hook:** the tiny conversation makes viewers lean in before the reveal. **Visual:** actual product demo or explicitly labeled fictional dramatization. **Danger:** this version cannot publish implying an actual successful product claim without a demonstrably successful working sequence. Uses truly separate neural speaker generations by full utterance; pausing only between sentences.

## Creative acceptance rubric
Blind reviewers rate each actual performance from 1-5 on: viewer self-recognition in 3 seconds; identifiable emotion; understandable central promise; specific visual proof; brand recall; natural speaking rhythm; believable final invitation; absence of unsupported claims. First judge the **unscored written script**, then the dry voice-only track, then the full film. None of these tests substitutes for audience comprehension research.

The same-scene copy is identical across Bella, Heart, 85% Heart and Michael. Dialogue casts may differ, with unchanged lines. The code archives SHA digests for all source WAV and preview MP3 takes. Strong voice quality does not by itself justify selecting copy.

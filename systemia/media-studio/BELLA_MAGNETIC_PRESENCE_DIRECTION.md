# Bella | Magnetic Presence Direction, v1

**Founder brief (Oct 27, 2026):** "Bella’s voice [should be] borderline sexy. Like it should draw people in deeper."

## The sound

Not overt seduction. Bella should feel close enough to be speaking to one person, with low-pressure confidence and the smallest suggestion of a smile. The listener should want to hear what happens next. Maintain adult, credible commercial narration appropriate for public Evercraft work.

- **Inviting:** warmth, attentive phrasing, soft but articulate onset, no hype.
- **Intimate:** conversational proximity rather than a whisper. Never fake breaths or audibly sexualized vocal sounds.
- **Patient:** let meaningful words land. No indiscriminate time stretching; test native 1.00 against 0.98.
- **Unforced:** do not flatten everything into a smoky monotone; use natural shifts of emphasis and finishing pitch.
- **Clear:** protect word fidelity, proper nouns and the original V05 af_bella timbre. Do not force Evercraft IPA stress or vowel surgery.
- **Polished:** a touch of low-mid warmth and gentle leveling can be evaluated, but it must not win by being louder. No artificial vocal doubling, pitch-down, hiss, or exaggerated compressor pumping.

## What is actually controllable now

The current Kokoro 0.9.4 path accepts speech text, voice identity, native speed, and seeds. **It does not have a verified "sexy" or emotional-performance control in this project.** Punctuation and speed affect generation; close-mic spectral/dynamic simulation is an *audio treatment*, not neural acting. This lab tests 2 punctuation patterns x 2 native speeds x 2 listening chains on exactly the same words, with real WAV and MP3 digests. Any "more magnetic" claim still requires actual human listeners. Keep the untouched native golden V05 original for identity regression.

## Score before unblinding

Listen at comparable volume, preferably through headphones. Six 1-to-5 axes: **magnetism, warmth, naturalness, trustworthiness, phrase endings, Bella identity**. Hard rejection defects: artificial syllables, whispered gimmicks, unnatural breath sounds, loss of intelligibility, inconsistent accent or identity, mispronounced Evercraft, overtly performative flirtation.

A winning take requires a genuine listener preference and no hard defects; if the control wins, do not change production. For reproducibility, report which phrase drew attention and where the illusion broke. Any new expressive engine or human-actor reference must respect independently verified consent, licensing and commercial voice rights. No customer or production release is authorized by this audition.

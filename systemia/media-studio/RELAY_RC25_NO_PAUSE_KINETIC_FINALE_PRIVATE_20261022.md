# Relay RC25 | Natural “Until” and kinetic final scene | Oct 22 2026

**Private creative-review candidate. Not approved for publication.**

## What the founder noticed
The RC24 take of `a02` was synthesized from `Until... the same date means two different things.` and contained roughly 0.43 s of silence just after “Until.” That broke the natural flow. The closing scene was also visually weak because it held an almost-static crop of actors with title typography beneath it.

## Changes physically rendered
- Swapped only `a02` to the already verified original native Kokoro `af_bella` WAV generated from `Until the same date means two different things.` **with no ellipsis**, sample-true 24-kHz native voice, transparent trim, EQ, matching gain and resample. New audible phrase runs about 6.50–8.81 s.
- Rebuilt RC23's founder-liked natural `a01` performance in the 48-kHz lossless mix; preserved five other lines' PCM, song arrangement and position. AAC was freshly encoded so **do not claim final audio is byte-identical** to RC24.
- Replaced frames 781–930 (final five seconds) with a dynamically panning photographic actor scene, two contrasting warm/teal luminous pathways converging to one point and a larger timed Relay title/CTA. This is a creative audition, not a final founder-approved hero design.
- The first 781 frames remain visually the same as RC24, aside from H.264 re-encoding.

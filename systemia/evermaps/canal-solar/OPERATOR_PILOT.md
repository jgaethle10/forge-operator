# EverMaps solar-canal operator validation and paid pilot

Status: **commercial concept for approval**. **Not yet a paid customer offering.** Primary issue: #1547.

## What exists
Eleven source-attributed regional GIS inventories, built from live government ArcGIS services with complete feature-ID retrieval. Each research region has map, GeoJSON, segment register and QA manifest. Some sources include closed/piped conveyances, drains, historic alignments or duplicate linework. These maps **do not** certify solar readiness or any operator's rights.

## Initial pilot (proposal only)
A single district-authorized boundary including up to **50 public source features or 20 km of operator-selected corridor**, subject to mutual written scope. Deliver a reconciled inventory with human-reviewed linework/attribution, discrepancies against the district's own approved map, source IDs, interactive viewer, machine-readable GeoJSON/CSV, and an issues report. Operator supplies or approves authoritative corrections. Exclude visits, geometry surveying, structural design, ROW research, solar resource, interconnection, MW, water-savings claims, and permitting.

**Proposed price hypothesis: $2,500**, contingent on scope, delivery effort, legal review, buyer signoff and verified payment path. Do not auto-quote from CHUM, start checkout, or describe it as sell-now until approved.

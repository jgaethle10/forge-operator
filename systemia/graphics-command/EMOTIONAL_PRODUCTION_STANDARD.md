# Evercraft Emotional Visual Production Standard

**State:** Research-backed engineering proposal. Systemia admission required. This document is not proof that AI Boardroom met, that a new film was rendered, or that anyone purchased.

## Principle

Every frame should change the viewer's understanding, emotion, or next action. Narrative stakes, a visible subject, spatial grammar, camera, editing, and soundtrack must cooperate. Emotion is earned through meaningful events and consequences, not a louder musical swell.

## Nine disciplines

1. **Story and empathy.** Name one viewer, a situation they recognize, a real obstacle, a visible consequence, and a believable change. Avoid sentimentality unsupported by the scene.
2. **Art direction.** Build distinctive and meaningful physical subjects. For Evercraft technology, glass, electric lighting and material depth are appropriate where motivated. Reject 1990s grids, endless neon dashboards, generic glowing orbs, microscopic figures and entire frames filled by type.
3. **Cinematography.** Lens, staging, foreground and depth, camera movement, focus, shadows and lighting must change what we understand. Physical scale and recognizable forms precede bloom.
4. **Animation and VFX.** Use editable worlds, believable object mass, anticipation, action and follow-through, coherent reflections and motivated transforms rather than cosmetic motion for its own sake.
5. **Editing.** Cut on new information, movement or emotional reaction. Vary the pace and hold deliberately. Preserve spatial continuity. One transition per cut is not a strategy.
6. **Music and sound.** Build cue maps simultaneously with picture. Tie musical accents to *earned* visual reveals; use room tone, tactile effects, sound bridges, intentional silence, separate music/effects/dialogue stems, narration space, source and rights receipts. Measure loudness and true peak for the actual destination, not one universal target.
7. **Color and finishing.** Scene-linear and documented color transforms, controlled highlight rolloff, consistent exposure, real delivery exports and accurate captions. Respect safe zones, legibility, motion comfort and flashing hazards.
8. **Truth and rights.** Preserve distinctions between observed, reported, modeled, inferred, staged and synthetic. Never fake customer metrics, product UI, disasters, testimonials or sound provenance.
9. **Distribution and learning.** One canonical editable master, 16:9/9:16/4:5 variants, sound-on/off comprehension, actual platform safe-zone review, verified CTA/checkout, and testing meaningfully different creative hypotheses against qualified outcomes rather than views alone.

## Required production contract

Every story has: objective, audience, human stake, exact physical must-show subjects, truth/rights evidence, storyboard, emotional arc, timecoded camera and lighting direction, audio stems/music synchronization, original editable assets, exact-frame render outputs, captions, publication status, and independently verifiable QC receipts.

The new emotional-edit-map compiler makes a **preproduction** map from the existing commercial campaign. It quantizes cumulative frame boundaries, attaches emotion/camera/sound directions and sound-picture synchronization cues, and prepares nine Boardroom critique questions. It deliberately marks rendered frame QA, human emotional response, actual audio and checkout as unverified. The build-commercial CLI includes the map digest and state in its receipt.

## Applied comparison: existing 12-second Website Audit campaign

**A, mechanism-first:** 0–2.6 seconds reveal the browser-shaped leak. 2.6–5.8 show five *possible* audit dimensions. 5.8–8.8 resolve the flow into five actionable priorities. 8.8–12 deliver the actual $49 Quick Win Audit offer and observed checkout path. The source visual remains synthetic illustration, not a performed customer audit. Original sonic phrases: Traffic up. / Sales flat. / Find the leak. / Fix what counts.

**B, person-first, not yet filmed:** show a real, rights-cleared operator's friction with a business website; follow their question with a real recorded product walkthrough; demonstrate what is delivered; close with the same authentic $49 action. Do not invent a customer testimonial or imply the operator received genuine findings if it was staged. No B film claim until source footage, real interface capture and full audio mix exist.

Neither hypothesis is entitled to an emotional effectiveness or conversion claim without independent audience and commercial evidence.

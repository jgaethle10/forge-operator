## Stronger 30-second HERO treatment required next

**0-3 seconds:** Start with a human and a consequence, not a generic virtual orb or stock-office establishing shot.  
**3-7 seconds:** Show authority granted and the automated action, clean readable visual.  
**7-12 seconds:** A real human hears the client question; allow a silent reaction, don't talk over it.  
**12-19 seconds:** We discover the permission chain points to the owner. Avoid categorical legal liability claims.  
**19-24 seconds:** The human actually corrects the approval boundary; plot resolves.  
**24-30 seconds:** Strong Systemia University brand, signature sonic identity, only verified course/lead CTA.

**Required release gates:** first-watch layperson comprehension, licensed rights, truthful claims, approved natural voice acting and consistent character identity, rights-cleared score, authentic moving shots through World Engine/graphics tools, media and shot acceptance through Fallen Studio, true native vertical and horizontal exports, verified live University route and founder publication approval. No paid generation and no automatic social publishing.

# Fallen Cinematic Reset | Production brief 03

**Editorial status:** New direction, not filmed. Existing v0.2 specimen is a negative example and must not be presented as accepted or repackaged. Systemia admission and independent Boardroom review remain pending.

## Core decision: stop making motion graphics pretend to be films

The earlier 12-second Website Audit piece was framed as a sequence of nearly identical vector UI cards. The video could be encoded, metered and hashed, but it did not have spatial development, believable objects, convincing stakes or an orchestrated score. Engineering value survives; visual concept does not.

We need an authentic image before a slogan. The next benchmark should show **one identifiable human or physical situation, one consequential change, and one verified outcome**. A brand film can be cinematic; a product proof must remain factual. Neither is permission to fabricate customer outcomes.

## New first production: THE MOMENT WORK CONNECTS

**Audience:** A business owner who spends too much time moving information between disconnected tools.  
**Desired feeling:** Recognition → pressure → possibility → relief → quiet confidence.  
**Product:** Evercraft Fabric integration system; source-captured capability/checkout demonstration only once verified.  
**Duration:** 18 seconds, 24 fps, master 16:9; separately composed vertical 9:16 and 4:5, never naive crops.  
**Truth state:** Staged illustrative operator footage plus genuinely screen-captured product steps; no invented customer performance, no fake customer or functioning-checkout claims.  
**Color:** Natural tungsten/practical warmth for the human environment; reserved glass/electric color for real system activity, grounded reflections, not a dashboard wallpaper.  
**Musical motif:** A three-note ascending unresolved motif, initially muted and fragmented, that resolves once the visible real action completes. A pause before resolution matters more than another impact sound. Score and edit composed together.

### Three treatments for the tournament

- **Human-first | The Moment Work Connects.** The above treatment. Strongest potential for empathy and distribution when actual footage and demonstrated product are in hand.
- **Physical-first | One Request, One Journey.** Invent a demonstrably synthetic physical glass-and-metal routing sculpture, with one moving physical token causing real, visible mechanical changes. Cinematic reference scene only, no claim that its represented backend workflow ran.
- **Documentary-first | Proof Before Promise.** No staged emotional payoff. Start with real screen capture of a capability request, its actual response and hash-backed handoff; pair tactile cursor/interface sounds with a purposeful, restrained score. A product-demonstration film, not a general brand film.

Each treatment needs a genuinely different visual thesis, not three colorways of an orb-and-grid template. Winner must be judged **from observed outputs**, including sound-on, sound-off, mobile and actual proof. Keep a losing candidate with a specific explanation.

## Creative Council and Boardroom asks

1. Story: Can a first-time viewer identify the problem without reading a label?
2. Camera: Are shot scale, physical setting and movement genuinely different and motivated?
3. Movement: Does something that matters actually happen in the image?
4. Emotion: Is the payoff earned rather than made to feel big through music?
5. Sound: Do phrase-level musical events articulate meaning, or compete with the picture?
6. Art: Does it look authored, specific and materially credible rather than stock future-tech?
7. Truth: Is every data/UI/outcome claim proven or transparently staged?
8. Access: Is it still coherent without audio and at smartphone display size?
9. Conversion: Is a viewer shown one honest and reachable next action?

The current deterministic Creative Council adapter **cannot** answer these by itself. The Boardroom must see exact decoded frames and hear actual audio before giving a scored artistic judgment. No source receipt alone approves beauty.

## Acceptance and release

Require actual editable 3D/scene or camera project where applicable, sourced physical recordings, visual timeline, separate original audio stems, accurate captions, no dangerous rapid flashes, 1080+ short edge final where applicable, exact frame hashes, visual observer, approved rights, human reaction/review, and validated product link. A technical pass may only enter creative review, never authorize publication.

**Operational order:** existing Systemia mission #1361 → verified source acquisition → one physically credible scene or genuine capture → three distinct draft treatments → render and sound mix → independently observed frames/audio → founder creative approval → Clip staging → separate publication gate.

**Do not:** hide the mediocre original behind another neon interface, claim finished media from sketches, fake customer results, reuse licensed songs without permission, or substitute anonymous AI agent notes for real independent observation.

# Evercraft Graphics Department | Capability Growth Map

Status: **engineering roadmap**, not a release authority, autonomous boardroom session or proof of customer adoption.
Owner mission: existing Systemia blackbox-distribution / graphics-MCP directive. Do not duplicate mission creation or bypass its canonical admission gate.

## Product promise

An AI agent or human customer should eventually be able to request an actual usable visual asset, understand its sourcing and limitations, approve a transparent quote, and receive a permitted high-quality output with a verifiable rendering receipt. The visual department should be good at visual thinking, production and accountability, not just image generation.

## Ordered expansion paths

1. **Art direction quality.** Add genuinely different compositions and licensing-safe client brand systems rather than palettes alone. Introduce human-judged test fixtures and actual browser screenshots at feed, square, story and desktop ratios. Include text overflow, contrast, quality, editability and accessibility checks.
2. **Evidence graphics.** Add explicitly licensed source ingestion via Systemia, units and methods in data-visualization receipts, measurement-state inheritance and external research verification. Today's numeric chart tool has **unverified** references only and zero-based axes, which must remain intact.
3. **Real production outputs.** Render owned SVG to signed PNG/WebP; develop a frame worker to export actual MP4, not merely HTML motion. Verify dimensions, frame counts, typography, codecs, preview/approved boundaries, storage and recipient-byte hashes.
4. **Owned world production.** Expose bounded, sandboxed Fallen/World Engine scene analysis and read-only preview first. Separate ground-truth observations, artistic simplification and physical simulation. Evaluate CPU/GPU budgets and node capabilities before any paid 3D workload.
5. **Client distribution and billing.** Scoped tenant OAuth, quoted resource budgeting, idempotent payment authorization, provider-paid verification, entitlement, job queue, rights receipts, atomic delivery and refunds. No public MCP billing or external job execution until genuinely tested.
6. **Discovery and partnerships.** Once a verified paid cycle exists, publish machine-readable product descriptions, compatibility and pricing, then let CHUM and eligible partner directories discover us. Discovery is not a sale, owner verification or permission to use third-party marks.

## Acceptance checks before each product claim

- **Visual:** real render observed in 4 form factors, visually graded by an independent reviewer, no truncation or unintended overlaps, correct brand rights.
- **Factual:** input dataset, source URL, date, transformation methodology and measured/modelled/synthetic classification preserved; unverified stays unverified.
- **Security:** caller-tenant identity, request size and concurrency limits, no unauthorized network or shell access, harmful uploads isolated and rejected where appropriate.
- **Rendering:** deterministic replay when promised, byte-level output digest, inspectable source/artifact, and verified exact format (SVG versus PNG versus real MP4).
- **Commercial:** actual price offered, buyer consent, genuine processor-paid receipt, fulfillment entitlement and independent recipient access, refunds/disputes.
- **Organizational:** canonical Systemia mission admission, named independent reviewers, review evidence and human-approved production release. A draft does not dispatch specialists.

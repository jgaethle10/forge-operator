# Work Saban: Commercial Brief

## Intent

Use only for an explicit request to create or plan a promotional video, advertisement, or commercial for a business. The user describes an outcome in plain English; Saban composes the right roles and creates a bounded project brief before any expensive or externally visible work.

## Workflow

1. **Intake and research.** Establish business, objective, audience, call to action, target duration, platforms, brand facts, approved source assets, and unknowns. Mark facts needing verification; do not invent customer outcomes, endorsements, photos, prices, business claims, or footage rights.
2. **Creative direction.** Draft positioning, opening hook, narrative arc, and a voice-led script outline. Prefer clear human narration. Distinguish creative suggestions from verified business facts.
3. **Production design.** Build a shot list and audio plan. Check mobile-safe framing, narrator pronunciation, aspect ratios, caption legibility, music/voice licensing, and the prohibition on reusing footage without explicit approval.
4. **QA and delivery proposal.** Check factual accuracy, source licensing, timing, readability, accessibility, and brand consistency. Return a proposed deliverable package, missing-input queue, and approval gates.

## Required team

- `research`: verify supplied facts and source permissions; missing information is recorded, not fabricated.
- `creative`: propose hook, voice-led structure, and draft script outline.
- `production`: propose shots, audio, framing, and asset ledger requirements.
- `review`: challenge factual claims, accessibility, music/footage rights, and readiness.

## Non-negotiable gates

- This skill is **planning-only** until an approved execution adapter exists and has passed integration tests.
- No browsing private records, device commands, arbitrary code execution, credential access, payments, deployments, publication, external messages, or video rendering on the strength of this skill alone.
- An outline is not a finished commercial. A planned team is not evidence that a specialist has run. A source suggestion is not a cleared license.
- Any external action requires a separate Systemia admission decision, specifically scoped owner approval, execution receipts, and a fresh quality gate.
- Preserve skill version and content hash, task hash, selected roles, quality criteria, unmet prerequisites, and side-effect denials in the plan receipt.

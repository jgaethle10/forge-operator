# Portfolio Migration Order

This ordering minimizes duplicated work and keeps customer-facing products online while the shared brain and platform layers move first.

## Wave 0: Freeze new dependency growth

No new Evercraft/Systemia product begins life in Base44. Existing apps may receive only continuity, extraction, security or parity work needed to complete migration.

## Wave 1: Brain and authority fabric

Move Systemia Core / KAIDANCE Collider, mission state, capability registry, selection rules, execution checkpoints, coverage receipts and release/authority contracts into the canonical private Core + Yard path.

Acceptance requires a live KAIDANCE health surface that can report safe operational facts such as last cycle, cycle age, coverage-receipt validity and held/admitted counts without exposing mission contents or private topology.

## Wave 2: Shared platform services

Move machine commerce, first-party capability routing, public AI discovery adapters, shared auth boundaries, institutional-memory interfaces, common data contracts and integration adapters.

The goal is one shared implementation consumed by products rather than one copy per historical Base44 app.

## Wave 3: Revenue-critical products

Migrate products with active sales, customer handoffs, payments, quotes, runtime usage or commercial discovery first. Each product keeps its current live surface until the Yard replacement passes parity and can roll back safely.

## Wave 4: Safety, family and mission-critical products

Migrate Guardian/EverNest-class safety and continuity surfaces with stricter privacy, data and consequential-action checks. Discovery can remain public while sensitive execution stays gated.

## Wave 5: Research, media, atlases and long-tail applications

Consolidate duplicate shells into shared platform modules where possible. Preserve distinct brands only when they have a real product, user or distribution reason to remain separate.

## Wave 6: Retire the legacy estate

For each Base44 app or dependency:
- prove its replacement,
- reconcile retained data,
- archive required evidence,
- remove production traffic,
- revoke obsolete credentials,
- record a retirement receipt.

Do not bulk-delete the estate before those receipts exist. The objective is zero dependency, not zero evidence.

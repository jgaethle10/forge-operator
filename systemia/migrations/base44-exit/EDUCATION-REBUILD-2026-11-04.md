## Shared architecture
Owned browser surface -> versioned curriculum registry -> lesson/assessment runner -> purpose-scoped progress -> optional Evercraft-owned inference adapter -> Systemia orchestration and evidence ledger -> Yard/Compute deployment, health and release receipts.

Every lesson stores source/provenance, version, age suitability, pedagogical goals, depth, rights and review status. Student data, if introduced later, requires age-appropriate consent, privacy controls, export/deletion, encryption, audit and independent safeguards. Never train models across learners without clear authorization.

## Stages and gates
1. Private inventory: preserve name, recovered source, rights, assets, original features, dependencies, private data, and one of rebuild/merge/archive/blocked; never infer absence from missing public source.
2. Owned foundation: testable learner app, responsive UI, bounded offline examples (this branch).
3. Content recovery: authorized Evercraft-held lessons, educator answer keys, multilingual/low-connectivity coverage and Luma art.
4. Controlled services: consent, learner/instructor roles, private progress, publishing, accessibility and safety validation.
5. Optional tutor: owned inference, clear disclosure, source-grounded evaluation and harmful-output safeguards.
6. Release: immutable source, tests, owned staging, independent browser/mobile checks, TLS/DNS and health, receipts, rollback, observation period.
7. Final disposition: app-specific parity/reconciliation and authorized retirement only; no historical data deletion by default.

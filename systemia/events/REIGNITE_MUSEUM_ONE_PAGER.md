# HANGAR B: REIGNITE
### A proposed preservation gathering | Summer 2027 · For discussion only

Some buildings hold more than history. Hangar B has been part of family memories for generations, and we'd love to help introduce the next generation to its story while contributing responsibly to its restoration.

**The initial idea:** Explore a modest **July or August 2027** event with **300–500 attendees as an unverified planning scenario**, never an assumed capacity. The date, areas, occupancy and activities would be chosen with the Tillamook Air Museum and qualified reviewers.

**Three formats, depending on what's safe and feasible:**

- **Quiet heritage evening:** approved visitor-space exhibits, preservation storytelling, art and optional headphone-based listening or freestanding visuals, only if structural, fire and crowd reviews support them.
- **Outdoor performance + museum heritage program:** any amplified music on a separately approved, independently engineered site, with sound, airport, weather, permitting and operating-hours review. Hangar B remains a quieter centerpiece.
- **Smaller preservation-first gathering:** art, history and community storytelling if repairs or venue constraints make music inappropriate.

A community pilot also suggested a **possible aviation fly-in**. We'd evaluate that separately with the **Port/airport operator** and museum, without inviting aircraft or promising parking.

**Evercraft's contribution:** creative direction, event identity, thoughtful outreach materials, sponsor presentation and simple participant technology. Qualified Oregon event-production, engineering, acoustical, fire and aviation professionals would be required for any technical design or live operations. No event activity, fundraising or ticketing is authorized yet.

**What we'd value from the museum:** (1) a designated person to discuss whether any site areas are appropriate for a preliminary feasibility review; (2) current roof/repair exclusions and any structural or operating constraints that can be shared; and (3) the museum's preferred route for discussing a preservation partnership, potentially alongside the newly announced Hangar B Preservation Council.

Vintage Aviation Museum confirms its acquisition of Hangar B and the Tillamook Air Museum, reopened October 29, 2026. The Port retains the underlying land; airport permissions are separate. We would not announce a partnership or commit to a date without written agreements and proper reviews.

**The first milestone is not selling tickets. It's finding the smallest event format that helps preserve the hangar without putting it at risk.**

*An independent Evercraft concept, not an approved or scheduled event, fundraiser, museum partnership or fly-in.*

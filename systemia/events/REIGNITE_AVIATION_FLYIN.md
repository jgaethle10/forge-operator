**Regulatory review is conditional on the activity:** FAA Airport Compliance Manual Order 5190.6C, Chapter 7 (§7.17), distinguishes aeronautical events (including fly-ins) from temporary airport closures for nonaeronautical events and airshows. Depending on whether REIGNITE seeks only ordinary visiting pilots, group ground access, a runway/ramp closure or an aerial demonstration, very different airport-sponsor coordination and FAA review/waiver obligations may apply. The project will **not** pre-label a fly-in as an airshow or claim that any waiver is required or issued without the airport's classification.

Source: https://www.faa.gov/airports/resources/publications/orders/compliance_5190_6/order-5190-6c-compliance-chapter-07

## Owner-facing five-step feasibility checklist

1. **Authority:** confirm who currently operates the museum/hangar and who can separately authorize airside activity. Obtain a clear Port/airport sponsor and museum decision path, in writing, before inviting anyone.
2. **Aircraft capacity:** ask the airport operator, not the public, about transient ramp space, existing users, parking, runways/taxiways, pilot arrival flow, fuel services, and whether prior permission would be required for any special arrangement. Do not invent an aircraft count.
3. **Public separation:** verify an airport-approved ground/access plan separating guests from aircraft, propellers, active taxi lanes and emergency routes. Include ADA access, airport operations, museum exhibits and historic structure protection.
4. **Weather and pilot briefing:** design a genuinely optional weather-contingent pilot itinerary. Pilots remain responsible for current aeronautical information and their own flight decisions. Never promise safe flight conditions, transportation or aircraft access.
5. **FAA/event classification:** have the airport sponsor and qualified aviation advisor determine if this is an ordinary airport visit, organized fly-in, public airside event, closure or airshow. Check the relevant federal/state/local requirements only after the exact operation is defined.

## Architecture and status

The five gates exist as **optional** `REIGNITE_FLY_IN_FEATURES` in the canonical owned Events feasibility module and can be assessed with `assessReigniteFlyIn()`. They are intentionally **not** part of the 25-gate REIGNITE flagship baseline, preserving its generated static readiness-page contract. In the owner-private `/api/owner/readiness` evidence desk they appear in `optional_programs[0]`, and their records are append-only, source-referenced, actor-restricted and independently unverified. The owner can record a source reference to any of the five fly-in requirements using the existing evidence form. Reported hazards are sticky and no source can self-authorize the event.

**No automatic execution:** No pilot emails, bookings, waiver applications, NOTAMs, aircraft routing, fundraising, sponsorship commitments or public announcements are performed by this planning change. A legal airport closure request or any airshow-specific operation requires separate formal authorization and competent human coordination. Approval flags remain false.

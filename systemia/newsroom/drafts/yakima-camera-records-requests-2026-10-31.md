# Yakima camera deployment: narrowly scoped public records requests
*Prepared October 31, 2026 | Not submitted*

These are identifiable-record requests, not demands for explanations. The City of Yakima request portal is https://records.yakimawa.gov/ and the Yakima County Sheriff's Office records route is https://www.yakimacounty.us/2279/Sheriffs-Public-Records .

## City of Yakima / Yakima Police Department
**Subject:** Public Records Act request: mobile surveillance camera trailers, October 28–31, 2026

Pursuant to Washington's Public Records Act, RCW 42.56, I request electronic copies of the following identifiable records in the City's possession or control, dated October 28 through October 31, 2026, concerning mobile or trailer-mounted surveillance camera towers deployed on or near Yakima Avenue and Barge Street during Halloween 2026:

1. Deployment plans, incident/event assignments, permits, approvals and written instructions authorizing installation or operation of these temporary towers. Redact truly exempt tactical or security information as necessary rather than withholding entire records.
2. Contracts, rental invoices, purchase orders or interagency agreements identifying the tower owner, vendor, operator and anticipated public cost.
3. Policies and written retention/deletion rules applicable to footage, plate data, analytics, access and sharing produced by these towers.
4. Records reflecting whether the city or Yakima PD requested, operated or received access to the towers or their data, including non-exempt interagency communications documenting the deployment decision.

Please provide records in their native electronic format when practicable. If your office does not hold responsive records, please indicate whether another City department is the likely custodian. Please identify the statutory basis for any redactions or withholding and provide a reasonable estimate of fees before incurring them.

## Yakima County Sheriff's Office
**Subject:** Public Records Act request: temporary surveillance towers and live-feed camera policy, October 2026

Pursuant to RCW 42.56, I request electronic copies of the following identifiable Sheriff’s Office records for October 1–31, 2026:

1. Contracts, purchase orders, deployment orders or event assignments concerning county-operated mobile/trailer-mounted surveillance towers in the City of Yakima for Halloween 2026.
2. The operative written policies governing the county's Flock live-feed video camera pilot, including retention, deletion, user access, audit logs, data sharing, prohibited uses and handling of footage not relevant to an investigation.
3. Non-exempt records documenting the governance and approval of live-feed cameras in city parks or on city property, including any October 2026 proposals to restrict or remove permanent systems.
4. Records sufficient to identify the number of live-feed systems active within the city on October 31, 2026, and the contracting documents for the reported two-year pilot.

Redact legitimately exempt details, including sensitive investigative or infrastructure-security information, while disclosing reasonably segregable non-exempt records. Please advise of fees in advance.

**Reporting hygiene:** Do not request real-time monitoring access, system credentials or unreleased security procedures. Do not claim filing until a request ID or acknowledgement exists.

# EarthWorks lane ownership strategy

## Category

EarthWorks should not position itself primarily as "an autonomous excavator company."

The category is **site autonomy orchestration**: the operating layer that turns a job objective into coordinated work across machines, zones, routes, material, people, safety policy and evidence.

The machine may be Caterpillar, Komatsu, Volvo, Deere, Hitachi, a retrofit platform, an RC prototype or a simulator. EarthWorks owns the job graph and the promotion evidence.

## Wedge

Start where the work is:

1. repetitive,
2. private or tightly controlled,
3. measurable in cycles/tons/yards,
4. expensive to leave idle,
5. uncomfortable or hazardous for people,
6. possible to model before physical actuation.

Quarry/aggregate haul loops and repetitive loading are the strongest first external wedge. Civil mass-earthwork is the expansion lane.

## Product ladder

### Free: Site Autonomy self-screen
A public screening tool that identifies whether a material loop is worth studying and refuses to label uncontrolled/high-complexity sites as strong autonomy candidates.

### Paid entry: Site Autonomy Study
One real material flow becomes a read-only site model:
- machine roster,
- route/zone graph,
- observed cycle evidence,
- production target,
- queue/bottleneck analysis,
- simulation replay,
- autonomy candidate tasks,
- required telemetry and safety gaps,
- go / instrument-first / no-go recommendation.

No physical actuation is required to deliver value.

### Expansion: Digital Twin + Shadow Dispatch
Run the planner alongside the live operation without commanding equipment. Compare EarthWorks dispatch recommendations with actual outcomes and measure:
- cycle time,
- queue time,
- idle time,
- tons or yards moved,
- intervention count,
- route changes,
- planner regret,
- production variance.

### Physical promotion
Only promote a task after the evidence package supports a bounded operating domain, approved equipment adapter, independent stop/takeover path and formal site safety process.

## Data moat

Every authorized pilot should add structured, provenance-preserving observations to an EarthWorks learning corpus:

- site topology and zone graph,
- machine capabilities,
- task graph,
- planned versus actual cycle state,
- queue dynamics,
- material lineage,
- intervention causes,
- localization quality,
- communications quality,
- human takeover events,
- adapter-specific performance,
- environmental state,
- outcome metrics.

Customer-private data remains tenant-scoped. Cross-customer learning must use permissioned, de-identified or derived patterns rather than exposing another operator's proprietary site data.

The long-term moat is not "we have an AI model." It is **evidence about how heterogeneous physical work behaves, fails, recovers and earns increased authority.**

## Distribution doctrine

EarthWorks is distributed from the pain, not from the brand.

Public discovery must contain exact buyer language:
- automate a quarry haul cycle
- coordinate excavators and haul trucks
- reduce truck queue time at the excavator
- autonomous mixed heavy equipment
- simulate earthmoving fleet operations
- retrofit autonomy without replacing the whole fleet
- digital twin for cut and fill
- remote-supervised hazardous material handling
- autonomous loader / hopper feeding
- site autonomy study

Each proof should produce three surfaces:
1. a human demo,
2. a machine-readable capability/contract,
3. a short visual proof suitable for field operators and executives.

## Partnership posture

Do not require EarthWorks to manufacture all hardware.

Potential partner classes:
- equipment OEMs,
- autonomy retrofit vendors,
- GNSS / LiDAR / perception providers,
- drive-by-wire and machine-control integrators,
- equipment rental fleets,
- aggregate producers,
- civil contractors,
- waste/landfill operators,
- insurers and safety engineering partners,
- survey / BIM / machine-control platforms.

EarthWorks should be able to integrate a partner's machine without surrendering the site mission, evidence model or orchestration layer.

## Defensible public claim

> Evercraft builds autonomous fleet coordination, digital-twin, dispatch, safety-envelope, telemetry and device-bridge technology for earthmoving systems.

Upgrade claims only when the evidence state upgrades.

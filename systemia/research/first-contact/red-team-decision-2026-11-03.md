## Highest-leverage work: improve the measurement engine

**Engineering investment recommendation:** prioritize sensor/data quality, not hypothetical gravity manipulation. Better independent observer-state estimation, frame metadata verification, range ambiguity and uncertainty visualization serve aerospace, mapping, drone inspection, ForensiScope and Evercraft Research regardless of the source of any UAP.

Hypothesis grades for publication must be assigned per *specific claim*: observed physical object, estimated motion, inferred mechanism, and origin are separate claims. Confidence in one does not transfer to the others.

## Quality / alternative explanations checklist

For every claimed acceleration or energy anomaly:
1. Obtain native sensor data or explicitly mark reconstruction as not possible.
2. Reconstruct observer position, motion, pointing, angular-to-world geometry and line-of-sight/range ambiguities.
3. Quantify calibration, camera timing, compression, multiple track association and atmosphere.
4. Compare parallax, balloons, birds, aircraft, drones, reflections, flares and sensor artifacts *as appropriate to the actual case*; do not claim all are applicable without evidence.
5. Test multiple conventional hypotheses against the same data, pre-register residual thresholds and retain failures/negative controls.
6. Require independent instrument modalities or independently sourced tracks before inferring unusual dynamics.
7. Require a physically specified mechanism and proper power budget before suggesting reverse engineering or an experimental build.

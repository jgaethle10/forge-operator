# Systemia | One Company, One Offer #1

Mission: `systemia-first-commercial-offer-2026-10-07`
Selected pilot hypothesis: **IBM i 7.4 Deadline X-Ray**, exact offer `ibmi_74_deadline_xray_250`, **USD $250**, 14-day validation window beginning only after the operating gates are truly open. This is not a statement that a buyer exists or has paid. Public IBM i 7.4 standard support ended September 30, 2026; extended support options exist, and systems do not automatically stop.

## Why one operating mission

No more portfolio-wide independent success narratives. Systemia admits one mission, retains dependencies and consequence gates, and delegates *bounded* work to the appropriate specialist. Specialist execution, Lineage commits, CI, paid orders, shipping, and customer acknowledgments are separate evidence states. A source file, a proposed partner, a draft message, a checkout link, or a green test never silently changes those states.

Run the read-only Company Board:

```bash
node systemia/organism/first-offer-command.mjs > /tmp/offer-one-readiness.json
node --test systemia/organism/first-offer-command.test.mjs
```

An authenticated internal operator may supply an evidence file with bounded keys such as `specialist`, `lineage`, `public_page`, `commerce`, `buyer`, `settlement`, `delivery` and `economics`. Each record requires `producer`, `observed_at`, `receipt_ref`, and stage-specific evidence. No customer secrets, names, contact details or raw receipts belong in public CI artifacts or this repository. The reader **cannot authenticate a caller's assertion**. Even contract-shaped records remain `source_asserted_requires_authoritative_recheck`, never conclusive commercial truth.

## Priority rule

Until the launch gate is genuinely verified, first attention goes to (1) qualified fulfillment reviewer + cost, (2) native Lineage release integrity and mirror, (3) owned HTTPS external canary, (4) exact USD $250 buyer checkout. Independent customer research and permitted educational copy can proceed in parallel, but **no paid customer is knowingly accepted without a viable approved delivery capacity**.

### Sales validation

The initial 14-day window is a bounded experiment, not a promise of automatic sales or a mandate to blast businesses. Track evidence of qualified responses, booked conversations, provider-settled orders, delivery time, net profit, satisfaction, and reasons the offer was rejected. Underperformance should lead to a source-backed revise-or-stop decision, not a fabricated conversion score or a perpetual launch.

### Source of truth

- **Systemia:** mission admission and work sequencing. The command in this directory is a read-only planning/snapshot program, **not** proof of resident mission activation.
- **Lineage:** eventual canonical immutable source, authority, rationale, and release evidence. A connected durable native Lineage write route is not verified by this source-only CI.
- **GitHub:** current connected engineering/mirror surface and CI compatibility during cutover.
- **Yard / Compute:** real deployment and independent HTTPS canary, not GitHub test inference.
- **Payments:** order / settlement authority, not catalog description.
- **Shipping / customer:** delivery / acceptance authority, not a generated file.
- **Context Fabric:** consumes verified receipts for lessons; does not upgrade untrusted evidence to truth.

## Acceptance test for "one company"

A real, authorized buyer requests the IBM i 7.4 X-Ray; an approved specialist can deliver within unit-economic limits; the owned site and exact checkout are externally verified; the buyer explicitly authorizes and completes payment; provider verification establishes settled state; the customer's *separately authorized* evidence reaches the scoped IBM i workflow; a qualified reviewer signs off four outputs; Shipping proves delivery to the authorized recipient; and the business records actual margins and feedback. Every consequential step has its own receipt. Only then call the cross-portfolio loop operational.

## Governance

No RIVET or partner-controlled product modifications. No stealth emails, posts, offers, contracts, payments, third-party infrastructure purchases, access grants or production deployments. Missing credentials and provider connectivity remain `held`. The Company Board may describe blockers and recommended next operations, never self-authorize the action.

# RIVET Brand System

Canonical direction: Brand Kit Vol.1, updated 2026-09-24.

## Themes
- Default: Bone light mode.
- Optional: Midnight dark mode.
- Preserve identical information hierarchy and data semantics across themes.
- Gradients may bridge Neon toward the teal edge of the approved mark. They stay decorative and secondary to maps, evidence, economics and site-plan content.

## Color roles
| Token | Value | Job |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Midnight | `#0B1424` | Product foundation and dark-mode canvas |
| Neon | `#62D134` | Live, located, active or verified-locatable state |
| Deep Cobalt | `#1F4AAB` | Single most important action on a screen |
| Bone | `#F5F2EA` | Default light canvas and minimal accent |
| Slate | `#64748B` | Muted information and minimal secondary accent |

Semantic error colors may remain outside the five brand colors when accessibility or safety requires them. Legacy orange decorative styling is retired.

## Type
- Headers, subheaders and titles: Open Sauce One, Medium or heavier.
- Body, paragraph and small UI copy: Public Sans.

## Mark
The mark combines a map pin, lightning bolt and navigation arrow. Do not replace it with the prior blue/orange R tile or the Luma image.

## Interaction rules
1. Default users into light mode and persist an explicit user choice.
2. Keep dark mode one click away.
3. Spend Deep Cobalt on the dominant CTA, not decoration.
4. Use Neon for live/locatable/active state rather than generic buttons.
5. Keep the canvas quiet enough that RIVET's evidence remains the visual payload.

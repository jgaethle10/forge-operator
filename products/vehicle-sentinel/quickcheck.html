01 / iPhone

Check official unwanted-tracker alerts

Open Find My → Items → Unknown Items Detected with You (if shown). Turn on Settings → Notifications → Tracking Notifications; keep Bluetooth and Location Services enabled. Apple alerts happen over time, not necessarily during an immediate sweep.

No alert is not proof that a vehicle is free of a tracking device.

Apple's official unwanted-tracking guidance
If you receive an alert, preserve screenshots and follow Apple's instructions. Don't confront anyone solely because of an alert.
02 / Physical check

Inspect accessible areas

Check only what you can reach safely. Never climb under an unsupported vehicle or disconnect unfamiliar electrical wiring.

03 / Evidence

Add a dated observation

Log something you directly saw or did. You can record an alert, unfamiliar object, a Bluetooth reading or a normal inspection with nothing unusual found.

Use your camera app for photographs. They are not imported into Sentinel or included in the text export.

04 / Export

Keep your own record

Export a readable text report or structured JSON to save outside this browser. Entries are user-reported observations, not independent forensic findings.

Browser storage isn't encrypted evidence storage. Anyone with access to your unlocked device or browser profile may access saved entries. Export only where you feel safe.

Safety and accuracy

Know what this can and cannot establish

Many devices broadcast Bluetooth, and several trackers use cellular data, wiring, or radios this webpage cannot see. Neither a clean check nor an unfamiliar signal proves anyone's intentions. If you find an object, photograph it where it is and ask a professional to identify it before making changes.

If you think you are in immediate danger, get somewhere safe and contact local emergency services. For ongoing concerns, a trusted mechanic can examine the vehicle.

# Evercraft HumanFilter Privacy Policy

Effective October 25, 2026. Operator: Evercraft LLC. Product: HumanFilter 0.1.1 desktop Chrome extension.

## Data processed on your device
HumanFilter examines the text and labels contained in supported social-media post elements in the webpage DOM to determine whether to hide those elements behind a reversible notice.

## Data sent or collected
The extension contains no analytics, trackers, remote AI requests or network APIs. It does not transmit your page content, browser history, identities, filter decisions or preference settings to Evercraft or any third party. Page text is not persisted by the extension.

## Local settings
The extension uses Chrome's `storage` permission solely to save your enabled/paused state, per-site preferences, mode and optional keyword list on your device through `chrome.storage.local`. Uninstall the extension or clear extension storage to erase those settings.

## Disclosures and limitations
The extension can fail to recognize AI content and can hide human-authored posts. It filters displayed content after the hosting website has delivered it to your browser, not at the network level. It does not work in native mobile social-network apps. If future releases add cloud features or telemetry, the privacy policy and disclosures must be updated before release.

## Contact
Developer contact information will be listed in the verified Chrome Web Store developer profile before store publication.

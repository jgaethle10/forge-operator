# Evercraft HumanFilter Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** October 25, 2026  
**Product:** Evercraft HumanFilter browser extension, version 0.1.2  
**Operator:** Evercraft LLC

## What the extension accesses

The extension reads only the text in rendered feed elements and labels within selected feed posts on supported Facebook, Reddit and YouTube pages, to decide whether to display a reversible filter notice. This processing takes place locally in the browser.

## What we collect

**No data is transmitted to Evercraft or to third parties by the extension.** There are no analytics scripts, trackers, cloud services, login systems, or remote AI calls. The extension does not store page text, browsing history, account identities, or a record of which posts were hidden.

## Local settings

Filtering preferences, site enablement, and user-entered keywords are saved in browser local extension storage using the `storage` permission. These remain on the device. They can be deleted by uninstalling the extension or clearing its browser extension storage.

## Sharing and sale

We do not sell, share, or monetize data accessed by the extension because the extension does not send any such data to us.

## Limitations

This extension does not certify whether content is AI-generated. Platform changes or unavailable labels may affect matching. Filtering hides rendered page elements but does not stop platforms from delivering the content to the browser.

## Changes

If future versions introduce data transmission, accounts, model analysis, or analytics, the extension disclosures and this policy must be updated before those features ship, and consent obtained where required.

## Contact

Publisher contact details must be provided through the verified Chrome Web Store developer profile prior to store publication.

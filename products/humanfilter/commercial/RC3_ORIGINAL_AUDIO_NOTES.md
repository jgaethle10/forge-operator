# HumanFilter RC3 | Original sound department auditions

Two 30-second audio arrangements for creative review, *not* a final approved commercial, master, or a replacement for Bella.

## Sound options

- **Human Warmth (96 BPM):** melodic, restrained, gently resolving; composed around live-action context and simple product proof.
- **Focused Energy (112 BPM):** quicker electronic pulse with a stronger rhythm; intended to feel confident without gaming-trailer booms.

Both are composed and generated from original mathematical oscillators, additive synthesis, filtered noise percussion and stereo early reflections. No pre-existing sampled recording, external soundfont, stock music, Relay soundtrack, On The Go soundtrack or voice clip is used.

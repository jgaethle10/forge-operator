## Camera & capture
- Film newly owned footage at 4K 30fps if possible, portrait 9:16, with 1–2 seconds of handles on each side. A modern phone on a stable tripod is acceptable.
- Turn off notifications; do not expose names, account identifiers, passwords, private messages, or browser bookmarks.
- Shoot monitor at shutter compatible with refresh rate (1/60 for 60 Hz); avoid banding and flicker; lock exposure/white balance.
- Get wide, medium, hands, close screen and human reaction coverage; use motivated edits, not constant synthetic zoom.
- Record native screen separately at full desktop resolution and sync via a visible cursor action. Use only the real installed extension and clearly label controlled samples as demonstrations.
- Capture 20 seconds room tone, keyboard/click foley, and separate scratch VO for timing; Bella final take must be separately approved.

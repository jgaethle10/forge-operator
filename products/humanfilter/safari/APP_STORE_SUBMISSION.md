## Description
HumanFilter gives you more control over what appears on supported social websites in Safari.

Choose Balanced mode to soften posts with recognized AI disclosure labels or explicit descriptions that they were generated using AI. Enable Strict mode if you'd also prefer to filter some AI-related discussions. Add your own keywords, switch supported sites on or off, and reveal an individual filtered post with Show once whenever you want.

HumanFilter processes displayed page text and short labels locally in your browser. The extension doesn't ask you to create an account or transmit your social-feed content to Evercraft. You can pause filtering at any time.

Important limitations: HumanFilter does not determine whether every image, video, or post was generated by AI. It can miss unlabeled AI-generated content and occasionally match human-authored posts. Site design changes can affect results. Supported websites and layouts are limited and require granting Safari website access. It does not filter posts inside native Facebook, YouTube, Reddit, or other social apps.

# HumanFilter | Standalone consumer launch rollout

**Product:** HumanFilter, by Evercraft LLC  
**Consumer headline:** Your feed. Your call.  
**Supporting line:** Own your feed.  
**Palette:** bright white, midnight navy, fresh mint and electric teal.  
**First release:** free, no account, desktop-first browser beta v0.1.4.

## Product promise: be specific
HumanFilter gives users local, reversible control over selected social-feed posts through recognized platform AI-disclosure labels, explicit generation wording, user keywords and optional broad AI-topic rules. It is not a classifier for all unlabeled AI content, and cannot authenticate the origins of media. No native mobile-app support is available. No developer or store page should overstate coverage.

## Launch decision: two distinct stages, no relaunch planning cycle

**1. Real, bounded desktop tester launch.**
- A branded, owned HTTPS product page with a single primary `Download desktop Chrome beta` action.
- Real source-derived screenshot, concise manual install guidance, public privacy policy, limitations and honest per-browser status.
- An accessible versioned tester ZIP and integrity checks. Do not confuse GitHub branch contents with a live public website.
- No signup, payment, upload, developer consultation or sales call.
- Keep the existing mixed-browser packages available to qualified testers, but don't advertise unsigned Firefox/Safari artifacts as consumer applications.

**2. Mass-market consumer launch after store review.**
- Register/verify publisher; complete Chrome Web Store listing, privacy answers, screenshot assets and packaging; submit for review.
- Update the single primary action to the actual approved Chrome Web Store listing only after approval.
- Queue Edge and Mozilla signed installs behind browser-specific field QA and their respective store approvals. Safari is still source-only, not available as an iPhone app.
- Announce to wider consumer channels when a customer can install simply, without Developer mode.

## Distribution: conversion before cinematic assets
1. **Product destination**: one working owned landing URL with one install action, screenshots and privacy.
2. **Proof content**: real on-device feed before/after with permission and privacy protection, no fabricated platform footage or unlabeled AI-detection claims.
3. **Owned publishing**: Evercraft Journal as the substantive explainer, Evercraft Clip as the short vertical demo. Link both to the single working product URL.
4. **Social channels**: Evercraft brand Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn and X, each adapted to its audience rather than identical boilerplate. A founder announcement may be used once separately.
5. **Community testing**: invite a small number of consenting Chrome users to report what HumanFilter missed; do not spam unsolicited install links.
6. **Acquisition measurement**: validate publicly available page/download and store-install figures when available; do not introduce tracking or reporting inside the extension without a separately approved privacy/consent change.

## One primary offer, honest copy
**Hero:** Your feed. Your call.  
**Subheading:** HumanFilter helps you filter recognized AI disclosures and topics you choose from supported social feeds. Your settings stay in your browser.  
**Tester CTA:** Download desktop Chrome beta.  
**Post-store CTA:** Add to Chrome (link to the verified store listing; do not display before approval).  
**Price:** Free beta. No Evercraft Payments step in this initial consumer funnel.

## Launch creative
The existing Fallen Studios HumanFilter RC3 commercial is a separate source-first production project with unresolved final source, voice and owner approval gates. It should not be used to delay a truthful beta launch or be represented as already published. A verified plain screen-recorded demo can explain the real extension more effectively than a fictionalized effect shot.

## Hold lines
Never claim native iOS/Android filtering, Safari installation, one-click Chrome Store installation, complete AI detection, or independently certified browser support without evidence. Never declare posts live or purchases made based on a queue, source file or local QA alone.

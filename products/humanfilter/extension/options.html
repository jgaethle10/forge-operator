EVERCRAFT / HUMANFILTER v0.1.2

Keep the feed yours.

HumanFilter is a browser extension that applies configurable, local content filters on supported Facebook, Reddit, and YouTube pages.

What it actually detects

HumanFilter v0.1.2 filters posts based on site labels, explicit self-disclosure wording, and your optional keywords. Strict mode also filters AI-related topic words. It does not inspect AI model outputs or prove whether a post is authentic. It can miss AI posts and hide human-made posts. Click Show once to reveal a false positive.

Privacy statement (October 25, 2026)

HumanFilter works entirely inside your browser. It reads the rendered text and short labels of posts on supported sites to decide what to filter. It does not send those posts, your browsing history, your account identity, or filter decisions to Evercraft or any third party. It does not use analytics, trackers, remote models, or external requests. Your preferences are saved on your computer in Chrome local extension storage and are not synced by this extension. You can delete them by uninstalling the extension or clearing its stored data.

Permissions: storage saves user preferences locally. Content script access is limited to specified Facebook, Reddit, and YouTube pages, where it reads and modifies the displayed posts for filtering.

Known limitations

Site designs can change and break filters. Not all labels are accessible to the extension. Text patterns are not evidence of AI generation. It doesn't filter Facebook's mobile app or content in other apps. Filtering never prevents sites from receiving the content in the first place.

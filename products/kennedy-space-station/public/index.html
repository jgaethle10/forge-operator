Source awareness, built in

A drawing should have a memory.

Kennedy's private engineering preview already links a saved concept to an immutable Site Planner report when one exists. That lets a team trace a plan back to its source snapshot instead of treating every mark as a fact.

Those records and visual studies remain operator-only while customer identity, access controls and paid memberships are developed.

Kennedy Space Station is being developed as an Evercraft-owned product for property owners, project teams and people exploring what a site could become. The goal is an inviting, practical experience, not another maze of engineering controls.
